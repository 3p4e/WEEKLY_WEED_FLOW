Switch a nominal on or offEvery whole number from 6 to 30 is a switch — odd as well as even. ○ is off, ● is on. A nominal switched on starts at its full ±10 %.
Drag either end to set toleranceBoth ends move together. The dashed outline is the ±10 % maximum you cannot exceed.
Pink overlap, green gapBetween two neighbouring nominals that are on. The number is the width in % points.
Drag the diamond to set a borderSnaps two neighbours to meet at that point — no overlap, no gap — and recalculates both tolerances.
Add or remove resultsOpen Results… on any strain to type values in or exclude ones you don't want. Solid dots come from certificates, hollow ones you typed.
Analyse & proposeGroups the results, then finds the tolerances for the nominals that are on which cover the most results with no overlap and, where possible, no gap — applied live, with a summary and an undo.
New strain

Builds an empty scale, filed under SPC drafts · Purely To Tetra — switch grades on, set tolerances, or let Analyse & propose do it. Nothing is locked until you finish it.

Conventions. A nominal N with tolerance t covers N − t to N + t − 0.01, matching the specification pages (20.00 ± 2.00 → 18.00 – 21.99). Two neighbours are joined when the lower one's top is exactly 0.01 below the upper one's bottom. Tolerance is capped at 10 % of the nominal and never below 0.01.

What is saved, and where. Every change — a nominal switched on, a tolerance dragged, a result typed or excluded, a spec finished — is written to the shared database the moment you make it, so it survives a reload, a hard refresh and a closed laptop, and everyone opening this link sees the same specs. The status line at the top right says which: Saved to the server with the time, or a warning if a change could not get through, in which case keep the tab open until it can. Two computers may work at once; each picks up the other's changes within about 20 seconds, and two people editing the same strain in the same moment will see the later change win. Source results are read-only; the nominals, the tolerances and your own entered results are yours to change. “Reset” returns a strain to its specification nominals — for a Versa strain, the nominals its product codes state — at ±10 %.
