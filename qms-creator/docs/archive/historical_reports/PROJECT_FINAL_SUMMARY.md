## Document Categories

### 1. Quality Assurance (15 SOPs)
Core quality system procedures including Quality Manual, Document Control, CAPA, Change Control, Deviation Handling, Supplier Qualification, Site Master File, and Management Review.

### 2. Quality Control/Testing (14 SOPs)
Laboratory procedures including Batch Release, Sampling, Stability Studies, Cannabinoid Testing (HPLC), Microbial Testing, Heavy Metals (ICP-MS), Pesticide Residue Testing, and Method Validation.

### 3. Production (7 SOPs)
Manufacturing procedures for cultivation, harvesting, drying, curing, trimming, and packaging operations.

### 4. Human Resources (7 SOPs)
Personnel management, qualification, GMP training program, hygiene requirements, gowning procedures, and visitor management.

### 5. Equipment & Facilities (4 SOPs)
Equipment operation, calibration, preventive maintenance, and facility management.

### 6. Sanitation (2 SOPs)
Cleaning and sanitation procedures, cleaning validation protocols.

### 7. Validation (3 SOPs)
Validation Master Plan, Process Validation Protocol, and Equipment Qualification.

### 8. Records Management (2 SOPs)
Record keeping, retention, and archival procedures.
