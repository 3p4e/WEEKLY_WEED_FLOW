NEW DOCUMENT CODING SYSTEM EXPLANATION
================================================================================

Why a New System?

Your old system (QAS-01.00.00) was good for your facility, but it had some 
limitations:
  - Three-digit subcategories ran out of space
  - Difficult to organize related documents together
  - Limited room for future expansion

New System:

Format: [CATEGORY]-[SUBCATEGORY]-[DOCUMENT]-V.X.X
Example: QAS-01-001-V.1.0

Benefits:
  ✓ Scalable: 999 documents per subcategory (unlimited growth)
  ✓ Organized: Related documents grouped by subcategory
  ✓ Logical: Clear hierarchy makes navigation easy
  ✓ Consistent: Same format for all 100+ documents
  ✓ Future-proof: Room for expansion as your facility grows

Categories:
  QAS (Quality Assurance)      HR  (Human Resources)
  SAN (Sanitation)             EQU (Equipment)
  PRO (Production)             PRE (Premises)
  SEC (Security)
  REC (Records)

Subcategories:
  01 = Main function (e.g., QAS-01 = Quality Management & Policy)
  02 = Related function (e.g., QAS-02 = Documentation Control)
  03 = Next related (e.g., QAS-03 = Validation & Qualification)
  And so on...

Full Explanation: See QMS_DOCUMENT_REGISTRY.txt (first section)

           • Write facility-specific quality policy

Day 1:     QAS-02-001 - Document Management

Day 6:     REC-02-001 - Records Management

For EACH SOP:
  1. Copy the SOP_TEMPLATE_Standard_Format.txt
  2. Replace all placeholders with facility-specific info
  3. Customize procedures to match YOUR actual operations
  4. Have reviewed by department manager
  5. Get QA approval
  6. Get facility manager sign-off
  7. File in appropriate folder
  8. Train personnel

ESTIMATED EFFORT & TIMELINE
================================================================================

RAPID IMPLEMENTATION (4-6 weeks):
  • Focus on essential documents only
  • Less customization detail
  • Faster approval process
  • Risk: May miss important procedures
  • Good for: Emergency licensing situations

STANDARD IMPLEMENTATION (8-12 weeks):
  • Comprehensive document customization
  • Complete procedure coverage
  • Thorough testing before implementation
  • Risk: Takes longer but more complete
  • Recommended: Best practice

THOROUGH IMPLEMENTATION (12-16 weeks):
  • Maximum customization and optimization
  • Extensive testing and validation
  • Detailed training program
  • Risk: Takes longest but most robust
  • Recommended: For large facilities or complex operations

ONGOING MAINTENANCE:
  • Annual review of all documents (1-2 weeks)
  • Changes as operations evolve (ongoing, as needed)
  • Personnel training updates (quarterly)
  • Regulatory compliance verification (quarterly)
