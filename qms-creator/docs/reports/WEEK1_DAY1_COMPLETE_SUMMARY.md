   - 5-day retrieval requirement
   - Escalation process for unreturned copies
   - Emergency procedures if obsolete doc found in use
4. **Marking Obsolete Documents**:
   - Paper: "OBSOLETE - DO NOT USE" red stamp on each page
   - Electronic: watermark, metadata, access restriction
5. **Archiving Obsolete Documents**:
   - Archive organization structure (physical and electronic)
   - Archive storage requirements (climate control, security, fire protection)
   - Archive register maintenance
6. **Retention Periods** (per EU GMP 4.11-4.12):
   - Batch documentation: 1 year after expiry OR 5 years after QP certification (whichever longer)

   - Training records: Employment duration + 5 years
   - Deviation/CAPA/Audit: 10 years minimum
   - Equipment records: Equipment life + 2 years
7. **Retrieval of Archived Documents** (investigation, audit, litigation purposes)
8. **Disposal of Archived Documents**:
   - Annual review and disposal eligibility

   - Document Destruction Log
9. **Special Circumstances**:
   - Obsolete documents found in use (emergency procedures, deviation, batch impact)
   - Regulatory inspection requests
   - Litigation hold procedures
   - Lost or destroyed archived documents

11. **Metrics and Monitoring** (monthly metrics, quarterly audits, annual review)
