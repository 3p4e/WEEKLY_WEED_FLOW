## 💡 The Solution: Automated QMS Document Generation

We have developed a **fully automated Quality Management System documentation platform** that transforms our EU GMP compliance approach from manual, time-intensive work to instant, error-free document generation.

### What We Built

A Python-based automation system that:

1. **Generates 26+ GMP documents automatically** from central facility data
2. **Produces inspector-ready PDFs** with professional styling
3. **Validates compliance** with automated cross-reference checking
4. **Reduces manual work by 99.4%** - from 8-10 hours to 3 minutes
5. **Excel interface** for non-technical staff to update facility data
