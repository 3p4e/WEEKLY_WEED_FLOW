UNDERSTANDING YOUR QMS DOCUMENT STRUCTURE
================================================================================

LEVEL 1: QUALITY MANUAL (This is Your Policy Document)
    └─ Quality policy, management commitment, overall QMS structure
    └─ Reference: QAS-01-001

LEVEL 2: SYSTEM SOPs (How Your Company Manages Specific Functions)
    ├─ Quality Management: QAS-01, QAS-02, QAS-03, QAS-04, QAS-05, QAS-06, 
    │                      QAS-07, QAS-08, QAS-09, QAS-10
    ├─ Sanitation: SAN-01, SAN-02, SAN-03, SAN-04, SAN-05
    ├─ Production: PRO-01, PRO-02, PRO-03, PRO-04, PRO-05, PRO-06
    ├─ Security: SEC-01, SEC-02, SEC-03, SEC-04
    ├─ Records: REC-01, REC-02, REC-03, REC-04
    ├─ HR: HR-01, HR-02, HR-03, HR-04
    ├─ Equipment: EQU-01, EQU-02, EQU-03, EQU-04
    └─ Premises: PRE-01, PRE-02, PRE-03

LEVEL 3: WORK INSTRUCTIONS (Detailed Step-by-Step Procedures)
    └─ How to perform specific tasks (attached as annexes to SOPs)
    └─ Cultivation steps, equipment operation, testing procedures, etc.

LEVEL 4: FORMS & TEMPLATES
    └─ Batch records, logs, certificates, checklists
    └─ Where actual data is recorded during operations

LEVEL 5: RECORDS (Completed Forms with Actual Data & Signatures)
    └─ Historical documentation of what was done
    └─ Retained for regulatory compliance (5+ years typically)

================================================================================

This QMS framework addresses the following EU GMP requirements:

☑ QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - Overall framework and documentation
☑ PERSONNEL - Training, qualifications, competency
☑ PREMISES & FACILITIES - Facility design, maintenance, environmental control
☑ EQUIPMENT - Validation, maintenance, calibration
☑ PRODUCTION - Process controls, batch documentation, traceability
☑ QUALITY CONTROL - Sampling, testing, specification setting
☑ DOCUMENTATION - Document control, records, archives
☑ COMPLAINTS & RECALLS - Complaint handling, CAPA, recalls
☑ SELF-INSPECTION & AUDITS - Internal audits, deviation investigation
☑ VALIDATION - Equipment, process, cleaning validation

IMPORTANT NOTES
================================================================================

THESE ARE TEMPLATES, NOT FINAL DOCUMENTS
→ Every template contains [PLACEHOLDERS] that must be customized for YOUR facility
→ You must adapt procedures to match YOUR actual operations
→ No document is complete until customized, reviewed, and approved by management
→ Incomplete placeholders make documents non-compliant

DOCUMENT CONTROL IS ESSENTIAL
→ Track which personnel have which copies of controlled documents
→ Update all copies when procedures change
→ Archive previous versions (don't destroy them)
→ Maintain version history showing all changes

TRAINING IS CRITICAL
→ All personnel must be trained on procedures before they work
→ Training must be documented
→ Periodic refresher training is required
→ New employees must complete training before starting

REGULATORY COMPLIANCE RESPONSIBILITY
→ Your facility management is responsible for ensuring compliance
→ These templates are a framework - your facility must ensure they're followed
→ Regular audits and inspections should verify compliance
→ Keep records of audit findings and corrective actions
