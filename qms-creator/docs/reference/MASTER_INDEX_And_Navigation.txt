SOP_TEMPLATE_Standard_Format.txt
  Purpose: Template showing the standard format for all SOPs
  Key sections:

    • Purpose & Scope (what the SOP covers)
    • Responsibilities (who's responsible)
    • Procedure (step-by-step instructions)
    • Quality Checkpoints (how quality is ensured)
    • Training Requirements (who needs training)
    • Records & Documentation (what forms to use)
    • Deviations (what to do if something goes wrong)
  When to use: As a template for creating/customizing all System SOPs

FAQ_Common_Questions.txt
