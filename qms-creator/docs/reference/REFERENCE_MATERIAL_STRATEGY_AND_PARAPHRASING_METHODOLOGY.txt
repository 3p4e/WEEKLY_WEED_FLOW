   specific processes to be effective

5. Regulatory Inspection: Inspectors recognize generic/copied procedures and
   will question if they truly reflect your operations

□ Manifest accuracy verified
□ Product seals intact and documented
□ Temperature/humidity monitoring device installed (if required)
□ Vehicles inspected for cleanliness and condition
□ Delivery date and expected transport conditions confirmed
□ Receiving facility contact confirmed

[RETENTION_PERIOD] years per REC-02-001 (Record Retention)..."

✓ Facility name changed (HAPA → Purely Plant)
