## 1. PURPOSE

**1.1 This SOP establishes the process for developing, documenting, approving, and maintaining product quality specifications at Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility.**

**1.2 To ensure that all specifications comply with EU GMP Chapter 6.14-6.15 requirements, Ph.Eur. Cannabis Flos Monograph 3028, and EudraLex Annex 7 (Herbal Products) guidelines.**

**1.3 To establish clear acceptance criteria for all quality parameters ensuring consistent product quality, safety, and compliance with regulatory requirements.**

**1.4 To define the change control process for specification modifications ensuring proper documentation and approval.**

---

## 2. SCOPE

### 2.1 Specifications Covered

- **Product Specifications:** Dried cannabis flower (Flos Cannabis)
- **Quality Parameters:** Physical, chemical, microbiological, and contaminant testing
- **Analytical Methods:** HPLC (cannabinoids), ICP-MS (heavy metals), microbiology, etc.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Defined limits and pass/fail determination
- **Documentation:** Specification master files, certificates of analysis

### 2.2 Exclusions

- Manufacturing process specifications (see production SOPs)
- Starting material specifications (see supplier control SOP)
- Equipment specifications (see equipment management SOP)

---

## 3. RESPONSIBILITIES

### 3.1 Head of Quality Control

- Define and maintain all quality specifications
- Approve analytical methods used for specification testing
- Ensure specifications reflect manufacturing capability
- Recommend specification changes based on data
- Review and approve all specification documents

**Authority:**
✅ Approve specifications within established quality objectives
✅ Determine suitability of analytical methods
✅ Recommend changes to specifications

### 3.2 Quality Manager

- Review specifications for consistency and compliance
- Ensure alignment with regulatory requirements
- Approve quality specification documents
- Provide quality policy guidance on specification limits

### 3.3 Qualified Person (QP)

- Final approval authority for all critical specifications
- Certify that specifications are scientifically justified
- Approve specification changes affecting product safety/efficacy
- Legal responsibility for specification compliance per EU GMP Article 51

### 3.4 Quality Assurance Manager

- Oversee change control process for specifications
- Audit specification compliance in batch records
- Ensure personnel training on specifications
- Coordinate inter-departmental approval process

---

## 4. KEY DEFINITIONS

**Specification:** The documented quality parameter including analytical method, acceptance criteria, and justification for a product or material.

**Acceptance Criteria:** The defined limits that results must meet for batch release.

**Quality Objective:** Overall goal for product quality (e.g., "Cannabinoid content consistent with therapeutic claim").

**Justification:** Scientific rationale explaining why a specific acceptance criterion was chosen.

**Quality Parameter:** A measurable characteristic of a product (e.g., THC content, moisture, microbial count).

## 5. PROCEDURE

### 5.1 CANNABIS FLOWER QUALITY SPECIFICATIONS

The following specifications are established for dried cannabis flower (Flos Cannabis) at Purely Plant:

#### 5.1.1 POTENCY TESTING (CANNABINOID CONTENT)

**Parameter:** Δ9-Tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) Content

- **Method:** HPLC (High-Performance Liquid Chromatography)
- **Reference Standard:** USP or Ph.Eur. certified Δ9-THC reference material
- **Specification Limit:** [18.0 - 22.0 % w/w] (example for medical cannabis)
- **Acceptance:** Result must fall within specified range
- **Justification:**
  - Based on therapeutic efficacy data
  - Manufacturing process validation data
  - Regulatory compliance with North Macedonia regulations
  - Patient dosing requirements

**Parameter:** Cannabidiol (CBD) Content

- **Method:** HPLC
- **Reference Standard:** USP or Ph.Eur. certified CBD reference material
- **Specification Limit:** [<1.0 % w/w] (example; may vary by product)
- **Acceptance:** Result ≤ specification limit
- **Justification:**
  - Product identity confirmation
  - Ensures THC-to-CBD ratio maintained
  - Supports therapeutic claim

**Parameter:** Total Cannabinoid Content

- **Method:** HPLC quantification of all cannabinoids (THC, CBD, THCA, CBDA, minor cannabinoids)
- **Specification Limit:** [20.0 - 25.0 % w/w] (example, dependent on product)
- **Acceptance:** Result within range
- **Justification:**
  - Overall potency indicator
  - Stability monitoring during shelf-life

**Parameter:** Product Identity

- **Method:** Chromatographic fingerprint comparison
- **Acceptance Criteria:** HPLC pattern matches approved reference fingerprint
- **Justification:** Confirms product is genuine cannabis flower, not substituted or adulterated

#### 5.1.2 MICROBIOLOGICAL QUALITY PARAMETERS

**Parameter:** Total Aerobic Microbial Count (TAMC)

- **Method:** Viable plate counting per Ph.Eur. 2.6.12
- **Equipment:** ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory (internal or contract)
- **Specification Limit:** ≤ 10^5 CFU/g (per Ph.Eur. 3028)
- **Acceptance:** Count ≤ specification limit
- **Justification:**
  - Patient safety (immunocompromised patients)
  - General herbal product quality standard
  - Ph.Eur. monograph requirement

**Parameter:** Total Yeast and Mold Count (TYMC)

- **Method:** Viable plate counting per Ph.Eur. 2.6.12
- **Specification Limit:** ≤ 10^4 CFU/g (per Ph.Eur. 3028)
- **Acceptance:** Count ≤ specification limit
- **Justification:**
  - Mold growth risk (mycotoxin potential)
  - Regulatory requirement
  - Product stability monitoring

**Parameter:** Absence of Pathogenic Microorganisms

Test for and confirm negative results (absence) in specified mass:

| Organism | Test Mass | Method | Acceptance Criteria |
|----------|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| **Salmonella** | 25g | Culture enrichment | Absent |
| **E. coli** | 1g | Culture enrichment | Absent |
| **Listeria monocytogenes** | 25g | Culture enrichment | Absent |
| **Enterobacteriaceae** | 1g | Culture method | Absent |

- **Justification:**
  - Patient pathogen exposure risk prevention
  - Ph.Eur. 3028 requirements
  - Regulatory compliance

#### 5.1.3 PHYSICAL AND CHEMICAL PARAMETERS

**Parameter:** Loss on Drying (Moisture Content)

- **Method:** Gravimetric analysis per Ph.Eur. 2.2.32 (vacuum drying at 105°C) or Karl Fischer titration
- **Specification Limit:** 8.0 - 12.0 % w/w
- **Acceptance:** Result within range
- **Justification:**
  - Prevents mold growth (>12% moisture allows TYMC growth)
  - Preserves cannabinoid stability (<8% may cause degradation)
  - Storage stability indicator
  - Ph.Eur. 3028 requirement

**Parameter:** Total Ash Content

- **Method:** Gravimetric combustion per Ph.Eur. 2.4.16
- **Specification Limit:** ≤ 7.0 % w/w
- **Acceptance:** Ash content ≤ specification limit
- **Justification:**
  - Indicator of soil contamination
  - Process residue monitoring
  - Standard for herbal products

**Parameter:** Appearance/Identity (Visual Assessment)

- **Color:** Green to brownish-green, characteristic of dried cannabis flower
- **Odor:** Characteristic cannabis plant odor (not musty, moldy, or off-odor)
- **Form:** Dried flower buds, not ground material
- **Foreign Matter:** <2.0% by weight (visual assessment or sieving)
- **Acceptance:** Batch meets visual specification
- **Justification:**
  - Product identity confirmation
  - Quality consistency for patient
  - Absence of mold, insect damage, or other defects

#### 5.1.4 CONTAMINANT TESTING

**Parameter:** Heavy Metals Analysis

Perform multi-element ICP-MS analysis for:

| Element | Specification | Method | Equipment |
|---------|---------------|--------|-----------|
| **Lead (Pb)** | ≤ 2.0 ppm | ICP-MS | Validated ICP-MS system |
| **Cadmium (Cd)** | ≤ 0.5 ppm | ICP-MS | Validated ICP-MS system |
| **Mercury (Hg)** | ≤ 0.1 ppm | ICP-MS | Validated ICP-MS system |
| **Arsenic (As)** | ≤ 2.0 ppm | ICP-MS | Validated ICP-MS system |

- **Reference:** Ph.Eur. 2.2.58 (ICP-MS method)
- **Justification:**
  - Plant bioaccumulation of soil heavy metals
  - Patient toxicity risk prevention
  - EU regulatory limits compliance

**Parameter:** Pesticide Residues

- **Method:** Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS) or Liquid Chromatography-MS (LC-MS)
- **Acceptance Criteria:** All pesticide residues below EU Maximum Residue Limits (MRL) or detection limit
- **Common Pesticides Tested:** Organophosphates, pyrethroids, neonicotinoids, fungicides per EU guidance
- **Justification:**
  - Cultivation pesticide residue monitoring
  - Regulatory compliance with EU MRL
  - Patient safety

**Parameter:** Mycotoxin Content

- **Method:** Liquid Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS)
- **Parameters Tested:**
  - Aflatoxin B1: ≤ 4 µg/kg
  - Total Aflatoxins (B1+B2+G1+G2): ≤ 10 µg/kg
  - Ochratoxin A: ≤ 20 µg/kg
- **Justification:**
  - Mold contamination indicator
  - Patient carcinogenic toxin exposure prevention
  - EU regulatory compliance
  - Drying and storage condition monitoring

### 5.2 QUALITY OBJECTIVES

The following quality objectives guide specification development and maintenance:

1. **Safety:** All specifications designed to eliminate or minimize risks:
   - Pathogenic microorganisms absent
   - Contaminants below regulatory limits
   - No evidence of mold or disease

2. **Efficacy:** Cannabinoid specifications ensure therapeutic effectiveness:
   - THC content supports claimed dosing
   - CBD content supports therapeutic claims
   - Stability data confirms potency maintained throughout shelf-life

3. **Consistency:** Batch-to-batch uniformity:
   - Appearance and physical characteristics consistent
   - Cannabinoid content within narrow range
   - Quality parameters stable over time

4. **Compliance:** All specifications aligned with:
   - EU GMP requirements
   - Ph.Eur. 3028 Cannabis Flos Monograph
   - EudraLex Annex 7 herbal product requirements
   - North Macedonia medical cannabis regulations

5. **Continuous Improvement:** Specifications enable learning:
   - Data trending identifies process improvements
   - Stability data supports shelf-life extension
   - Regulatory updates incorporated through change control

### 5.3 SPECIFICATION DEVELOPMENT PROCEDURE

**Step 1: Initial Specification Development**
- Head of QC determines required quality parameters based on product requirements
- Analytical methods identified (reference to SOP QCS_01.09_v1)
- Literature review and historical batch data analyzed
- Justification document drafted

**Step 2: Preliminary Limits Establishment**
- Analysis of manufacturing process validation data
- Batch data from pilot/validation batches
- Stability study results reviewed
- Preliminary specification limits proposed

**Step 3: Internal Review**
- QC Head reviews technical accuracy
- Quality Manager reviews regulatory alignment
- Discussion to reach consensus

**Step 4: Qualified Person Review**
- QP evaluates scientific justification
- QP assesses regulatory compliance
- QP determines suitability for product

**Step 5: Approval and Implementation**
- Specification document finalized
- All required signatures obtained
- Specification becomes effective date
- Communication to all relevant departments

**Step 6: Ongoing Monitoring**
- Batch data collected and trended
- Specification capability versus actual results reviewed annually
- Modifications processed through change control

---

### 5.4 SPECIFICATION CHANGE CONTROL

**Reasons for Specification Changes:**
- Stability data supporting wider limits
- Process improvement enabling tighter tolerances
- Regulatory requirement changes
- Equipment upgrade improving analytical capability
- CAPA implementation addressing non-conformance

**Change Control Process:**

1. **Initiation:** Change Control Request (per SOP QA_00.05_v1) submitted with:
   - Current specification
   - Proposed specification
   - Justification and supporting data

2. **Assessment:** Quality Manager and QA Manager evaluate:
   - Impact on product quality and safety
   - Regulatory implications
   - Risk assessment
   - Validation requirements

3. **Approval:** Change Control Board reviews and votes:
   - Approved / Approved with conditions / Rejected
   - QP provides final authority

4. **Implementation:** Department executes change:
   - Document updates
   - System changes
   - Training of affected personnel

5. **Verification:** Quality Assurance confirms:
   - Change implemented correctly
   - Expected results achieved
   - No unintended consequences

6. **Closure:** Change Control Board documents closure:
   - Effectiveness confirmed
   - Records archived

### 5.5 SPECIFICATION DOCUMENTATION

**Every specification must include:**

1. **Identification Section**
   - Specification code (format: SPEC-PPL-####)
   - Product name
   - Effective date
   - Version number
   - Supersedes (previous version reference)

2. **Quality Parameters Section**
   - Parameter name and units
   - Analytical method and SOP reference
   - Specification limits (acceptance criteria)
   - Testing frequency
   - Responsible department

3. **Testing Methods Section**
   - Method name and reference (Ph.Eur., USP, etc.)
   - Equipment required
   - Reference standards needed
   - Validation status (validated/verified)

4. **Justification Section**
   - Regulatory requirement citations
   - Historical batch data summary
   - Stability study results
   - Risk assessment
   - Literature references

5. **Approval Section**
   - Signature blocks for QC Head, Quality Manager, QP
   - Dates of approval
   - Distribution list

6. **Shelf-Life Section**
   - Proposed shelf-life (e.g., 24 months)
   - Storage conditions
   - Stability monitoring requirements
   - Retest interval (if applicable)

---

### 5.6 SPECIFICATION COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION

**Batch Release Process:**

For each batch prior to release:

1. **Sampling:** Representative sample collected per SOP QC_01.02_v1
2. **Testing:** All specified parameters tested per analytical methods
3. **Assessment:** Results compared to specification limits
4. **Documentation:** Certificate of Analysis prepared
5. **Decision:** Batch assessed as:
   - **Pass:** All parameters within specification → Approved for release
   - **Hold:** Pending re-test or investigation → Batch quarantined
   - **Fail:** Parameter(s) outside specification → Investigation initiated (SOP QA_00.06_v1)

6. **QP Release:** Final batch certification by Qualified Person per SOP QC_01.01_v1

## 6. QUALITY ASSURANCE

### 6.1 Specification Audit

**Quarterly Specification Compliance Audit:**
- Review 3-5 recent batch release records
- Verify all specification parameters tested
- Confirm results within specification or handled appropriately
- Document audit findings

### 6.2 Specification Trending

**Annual Specification Data Review:**
- Compile all batch test results for past year
- Calculate mean, standard deviation, range
- Compare to specification limits
- Identify trends or concerning patterns
- Recommend specification adjustments if data supports change

### 6.3 Training on Specifications

**Personnel Training Requirements:**
- All QC personnel: Specification understanding training (4 hours)
- Production personnel: Knowledge of applicable specifications (2 hours)
- Annual refresher: Updates to specifications (1 hour)
