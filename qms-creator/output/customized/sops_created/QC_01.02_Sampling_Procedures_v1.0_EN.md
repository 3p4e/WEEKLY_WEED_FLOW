## 1. PURPOSE

**1.1 This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes the requirements and procedures for the systematic sampling of cannabis flower batches throughout all production stages at Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility.**

**1.2 To ensure that representative samples are collected from each production batch using statistically valid sampling plans that comply with EU GMP Chapter 6.11 requirements.**

**1.3 To maintain sample integrity and chain of custody from collection through completion of testing.**

**1.4 To provide a documented basis for quality decisions and batch release throughout the product lifecycle.**

## 4. DEFINITIONS

**Batch/Lot:** A quantity of cannabis flower manufactured in a single production run intended to have uniform character and quality.

**Sampling Plan:** The documented statistical method for selecting representative samples from a batch based on batch size and quality risk.

**Representative Sample:** A sample adequately reflecting the characteristics of the entire batch from which it is drawn.

**Sample Increment:** An individual portion taken from a specific location within the batch.

**Cumulative/Group Sample:** The combined result of multiple sample increments from different locations, creating a single batch sample.

**Sample Integrity:** The condition that a sample remains uncontaminated, unaltered, and maintains representative characteristics from collection through analysis.

**Chain of Custody:** The documented transfer of sample possession from collector through analysis, showing who had custody and when.

**Control Plant/Tray:** Individual plant or container selected according to the sampling plan for in-process monitoring.

## 5. PROCEDURE

### 5.1 SAMPLING PLAN AND SAMPLE SIZE DETERMINATION

**5.1.1 Sampling Formula**

The sampling plan for Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Facility uses the following statistically-based formula:

**Sample Size = √(Batch Parameter) × 1.5**

Where the Batch Parameter varies by production stage:
- Flowering Control: √#fP (total flowering plants)
- Drying Control: √N (total plants in drying)
- Curing Control: √N (total storage containers)
- Pre-Packaging: √cT (total curing trays)

**Example Calculations:**

| Batch Size | Formula | Calculation | Sample Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 plants | √50 × 1.5 | 7.07 × 1.5 | 11 |
| 90 plants | √90 × 1.5 | 9.49 × 1.5 | 14 |
| 100 plants | √100 × 1.5 | 10 × 1.5 | 15 |
| 200 plants | √200 × 1.5 | 14.14 × 1.5 | 21 |

**5.1.2 Documentation of Sample Size Determination**

Prior to sampling initiation:
1. Batch size confirmed from production records
2. Sample size calculated using formula above
3. Sample size documented in Request for Sampling and Analysis form
4. Head of QC approves sample size and sampling plan
5. Record maintained with batch documentation

### 5.2 STAGE-SPECIFIC SAMPLING PROCEDURES

### 5.2.1 STAGE 1: FLOWERING CONTROL (Plant Development Monitoring)

**Purpose:** Monitor trichome maturity and determine harvest readiness

**Timing:** When flowering plants reach estimated 80-90% maturity (visual assessment by Production)

**Sample Size Calculation:**
- **Formula:** fPs = √#fP × 1.5
- Where fPs = Number of flowering control plants to select
- #fP = Total number of flowering plants in cultivation area

**Selection Method - Random Distribution:**
1. Divide cultivation area into grid sections
2. Randomly select plants distributed evenly across cultivation room
3. Plants selected from all height levels and locations (ensure representation)
4. Do NOT cluster selections in single area

**Sample Identification:**
- Mark selected plants with yellow label strips
- Document plant location and ID in sampling records
- Photograph location grid (if documentation system allows)

**Sample Collection:**
- Collect 3-5 flowering buds from upper third of plant
- Include mix of sizes (small, medium, large buds)
- Collect samples in paper envelope labeled with plant ID
- Maintain separateness of samples from different plants

**Analysis Performed:**
- Microscopy of trichome maturity (visual assessment)
- Photography of trichome development
- Determination of cannabinoid profile (if HPLC available)
- Decision: Ready for Harvest OR Continue Growing

**Pass Criteria:**
- Minimum 80% of control plants meet maturity indicators
- Trichomes showing 50-80% opaque/amber color
- No mold or disease indicators present

**Sample Disposal:** Samples returned to production for composting/disposal after analysis

---

### 5.2.2 STAGE 2: DRYING IN-PROCESS CONTROL (Moisture Management)

**Purpose:** Monitor moisture loss during drying process and ensure even drying

**Timing:** Daily during drying process (typically 7-10 days)

**Sample Size Calculation:**
- **Formula:** cP = √N × 1.5
- Where cP = Control plants to sample
- N = Total number of plants loaded into drying room

**Drying Room Sampling Grid System**

The drying room shall be virtually divided into a 3×3×3 grid system:

```
          Top Level (III)
     III.1    III.2    III.3
     III.4    III.5    III.6
     III.7    III.8    III.9

        Middle Level (II)
     II.1     II.2     II.3
     II.4     II.5     II.6
     II.7     II.8     II.9

       Ground Level (I)
     I.1      I.2      I.3
     I.4      I.5      I.6
     I.7      I.8      I.9
```

**Distribution Across Levels:**
1. Divide total sample count (cP) by 3 levels
2. Allocate approximately equal numbers per level (LcP)
3. Ensure coverage of all three height levels

**Example Distribution:**
- Total control plants needed: 14
- Per level allocation: 14 ÷ 3 = ~5 plants per level
- Distribution: Level I = 5, Level II = 5, Level III = 4

**Selection Within Each Level:**
1. Randomly select grid locations within each level
2. **Critical Requirement:** Include 3-5 plants with **lowest mass** and 3-5 with **highest mass**
3. Distribute lowest and highest mass plants evenly across all levels
4. Record grid location for each selected plant

**Sample Collection from Drying Room:**

For each control plant location:
1. Collect 4-5 sample increments from different depths/locations within drying area
2. Sample increments from:
   - Upper portion of hanging material
   - Middle portion of hanging material
   - Lower portion of hanging material
   - Interior (if stacked material)
3. Combine all increments from same level into one cumulative sample
4. **CRITICAL:** Do NOT combine increments from different height levels
5. Place cumulative sample in labeled container with:
   - Batch ID
   - Height Level (I, II, or III)
   - Date and time collected
   - Analyst initials

**Testing Performed:**
- Loss on Drying (Moisture Content) - Halogen Moisture Analyzer
  - Acceptance: ≤15.0% for both GACP and GMP products
- Visual assessment of drying uniformity
- Mold/disease assessment
- Color assessment

**Pass Criteria:**
- Minimum 80% of control plants meet moisture content criteria (≤15.0%)
- Uniform drying across all height levels
- No visible mold or disease
- Mass reduction appropriate for product type

**Sampling Frequency:**
- Daily sampling during drying process
- Continue until 3 consecutive days meet acceptance criteria
- Final day sample approved before stage completion

---

### 5.2.3 STAGE 3: CURING PROCESS (Homogenization and Stability)

**Purpose:** Monitor homogenization and cannabinoid stability during curing

**Timing:** Daily to 3x weekly during curing phase (typically 14-21 days)

**Sample Size Calculation:**
- **Formula:** cP = √N × 1.5
- Where cP = Number of curing containers to sample
- N = Total number of stainless steel curing containers

**Container Selection:**
1. Randomly select containers per calculated sample size
2. Distribute selections across curing room
3. Ensure variety of container positions (perimeter, interior, different heights)
4. Document container IDs selected

**Sample Collection from Curing Containers:**

For each selected container:
1. Open container with clean technique
2. Collect 1-3 untrimmed cannabis flowers from different:
   - Depths within container
   - Locations within container (top, middle, bottom area)
   - Sizes (small, medium, large flowers present)
3. Combine all flower samples into one group sample
4. Label with:
   - Batch ID and container ID
   - Date and time of collection
   - Analyst name and initials
   - Stage: "Curing - Day X of Y"

**Testing Performed:**
- Loss on Drying (Moisture Content)
  - Acceptance: 8.0 - 12.0% for both GACP and GMP
- Qualitative cannabinoid assessment (if HPLC available)
- Visual homogeneity assessment
- Color assessment

**Pass Criteria:**
- Minimum 80% of sampled containers meet moisture criteria (8.0 - 12.0%)
- Samples show consistent color and structure
- No visible mold or disease
- Cannabinoid profile stable (if HPLC performed)

**Sampling Frequency:**
- Daily during first week
- 3x weekly after first week
- Continue until 3 consecutive tests meet acceptance criteria
- Final sampling date approved before stage completion

---

### 5.2.4 STAGE 4: PRE-PRIMARY PACKAGING (Final Batch Release Preparation)

**Purpose:** Comprehensive testing prior to packaging for distribution

**Sample Size Calculation:**
- **Formula:** cT = √cT × 1.5
- Where cT = Number of curing trays to sample
- Total = All deep curing trays in batch

**Visual Inspection Sampling:**
1. Randomly select calculated number of trays
2. Visually inspect each selected tray for:
   - Uniformity of flower size
   - Color consistency
   - Absence of foreign material
   - Absence of mold or disease

**Batch Representative Bulk Sample Collection:**

1. **Target Sample Size:** 550.0 - 650.0g total
2. **Per Tray Contribution:** aBs = Bs / #Dt
   - Where Bs = Total bulk sample target weight
   - #Dt = Total number of deep trays
   - aBs = Amount to collect per tray

3. **Collection Method:**
   - Collect 4-8 portions from each tray
   - Collect from various locations and depths
   - Combine all portions into single cumulative bulk sample
   - Place in clean, disinfected stainless steel deep tray with lid

4. **Sample Labeling:**
   - Batch ID
   - Date and time of collection
   - Total weight (g)
   - Analyst name and initials
   - "Pre-Packaging Bulk Sample"

**Complete QC Assay Testing:**

From bulk sample, perform all required testing:
- Qualitative & Quantitative Cannabinoid Analysis (HPLC) - 10-20g
- Total Ash Content - 3.0-5.0g
- Heavy Metals Analysis (ICP-MS) - 3.0-5.0g
- Pesticide Residues Testing - 3.0-5.0g
- Microbiological Purity Testing (TAMC, TYMC, pathogens) - 36.0-40.0g
- Mycotoxin Testing (Aflatoxin B1, etc.) - 10.0-12.0g
- Plant Health Testing (botanical origin verification) - 5.0-6.0g
- Water Activity (aw) Determination - 5.0-6.0g
- Foreign Matter Determination - 50.0g minimum

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Cannabinoid Content: Within established specification (e.g., THC 18.0-22.0%)
- Ash: <7.0%
- Heavy Metals: Below limits (Pb <2.0 ppm, Cd <0.5 ppm, etc.)
- Pesticides: All <LOD or below limits
- TAMC: <10^5 CFU/g
- TYMC: <10^4 CFU/g
- Pathogenic bacteria: Absent
- Mycotoxins: <10 ppb (aflatoxin B1)
- Water Activity: 0.65 - 0.80
- Foreign Matter: <2.0%
- Visual Assessment: Uniform appearance, acceptable color

**Pass/Hold Decision:**
- If ALL tests pass: Batch approved for packaging
- If ANY test fails: Batch placed on hold, investigation initiated per SOP QA_00.06_v1

**Retention/Counter-Sample:**
- Retain 200.0 - 250.0g from bulk sample in sealed container
- Label with batch ID, date, storage location
- Maintain for minimum 1 year (or product shelf life + 6 months)

### 5.3 SAMPLE INTEGRITY AND STORAGE

**5.3.1 Container Requirements**

**Sampling Containers:**
- Material: Stainless steel (Grade 304), food-grade rated
- Type: Deep innox tray with fitted lid
- Capacity: No more than 2/3 full of container volume
- Maximum weight: 1,600.0g net weight per container
- Cleaning: Each container cleaned and disinfected ex tempore (immediately) before use
- Drying: Air-dried or wiped with clean cloth before use

**Container Preparation:**
1. Remove container from clean storage
2. Inspect for damage, cleanliness, and integrity
3. Rinse with distilled water if dust visible
4. Air dry or wipe dry with clean cloth
5. Verify label/ID is correct
6. Proceed with sample collection

**5.3.2 Sample Labeling**

Each sample container must be labeled with:
- ☐ Batch ID / Batch Number
- ☐ Product Name
- ☐ Production Stage (Drying / Curing / Pre-Packaging)
- ☐ Date of Collection (DD-MMM-YYYY)
- ☐ Time of Collection (HH:MM)
- ☐ Total Weight or Count (as applicable)
- ☐ Analyst Name / Initials
- ☐ QC Reference Number (assigned by QC Lab)
- ☐ Storage Conditions (if applicable)
- ☐ Retention Period / Disposal Date

**5.3.3 Sample Storage**

**Short-Term Storage (pending analysis):**
- Location: ROOM-09 Secure Storage or QC Laboratory (ROOM-06 adjacent)
- Temperature: 18-25°C (ambient)
- Humidity: 40-60% RH
- Light: Protected from direct sunlight
- Duration: Not to exceed 48 hours before analysis initiation
- Container: Original stainless steel tray with lid

**Long-Term Retention Storage:**
- Location: ROOM-09 Secure Storage (designated retention sample area)
- Temperature: 15-25°C
- Humidity: 30-60% RH (desiccant packets if needed)
- Light: Protected, cool dark storage
- Container: Airtight container or original container with seal
- Label: Retention sample label with batch info, storage date, disposal date

**5.3.4 Sample Integrity Verification**

Before analysis, QC Analyst shall verify:
- ☐ Container sealed and undamaged
- ☐ Label information legible and complete
- ☐ Sample mass/quantity as expected (no obvious loss)
- ☐ No visible contamination, mold, or deterioration
- ☐ Temperature log (if applicable) shows appropriate conditions
- ☐ Chain of custody record complete

If any integrity concern identified:
- Sample rejected
- Production notified of sampling issue
- New sample collected
- Issue documented in QC deviation record

### 5.4 CHAIN OF CUSTODY PROCEDURES

**5.4.1 Sampling Request and Approval**

**Step 1: Production Submits Request**
- Production Department submits Request for Sampling and Analysis (RSA) form (Annex A01)
- Form completed with:
  - Batch ID and batch completion date
  - Product type and quantity produced
  - Expected testing requirements
  - Requested sampling date/timeline
  - Production Manager signature and date

**Step 2: QC Approves Sampling Plan**
- Head of QC reviews RSA form
- Verifies all required information present
- Calculates sample size per formula
- Approves sampling location and plan
- Signs RSA form with date and time
- Forwards to QC Analyst for implementation

**Step 3: QC Notifies Production**
- QC sends approved sampling plan to Production
- Communicates timing and location for sampling
- Confirms access to production areas
- Provides sampling containers (if applicable)

**5.4.2 Sampling Execution and Documentation**

**During Sampling:**
- QC Analyst collects sample(s) per approved plan
- All locations/grid positions documented
- Sample weight/count recorded
- Temperature and environmental conditions recorded (if applicable)
- Photographs taken (optional but recommended)
- All entries signed and dated in real-time

**Documentation Form (Annex A02 - Sampling Execution Record):**
- Batch ID confirmed
- Sampling stage and locations
- Sample size and weight
- Collection time and date
- Analyst name and signature
- Any issues or observations noted
- Photographs/location map attached

**5.4.3 Sample Transfer and Receipt**

**Transport to Laboratory:**
- QC Analyst transports sample immediately after collection
- Sample maintained in sealed container
- Transport time minimized (goal: <1 hour)
- Sample protective case used if available
- Temperature protection ensured (if required)

**QC Laboratory Receipt:**
- Laboratory technician receives and inspects sample
- Verifies container integrity and label accuracy
- Confirms weight/quantity and condition
- Records receipt in QC Laboratory Notebook
- QC Analyst and Lab Technician both sign receipt
- Sample assigned QC Test Number (QC-YYYY-###)
- Sample placed in designated storage location

**5.4.4 Analysis and Final Disposition**

**During Analysis:**
- All testing documented per SOP QC_01.06_v1
- Testing performed and results recorded
- All calculations verified
- Results compared to acceptance criteria

**Results Reporting:**
- Test results compiled in batch summary report
- Summary signed by QC Analyst and QC Head
- Results distributed to Quality Assurance Manager
- Batch disposition (Release / Hold) documented
- Qualified Person provides final approval

**Retention Sample Archival:**
- Retention sample (if applicable) labeled with disposal date
- Transferred to long-term retention storage
- Entry recorded in Retention Sample Log
- Retained per Section 5.3.3 storage requirements

**Modifications require:**
1. Technical justification documented
2. Head of QC review and approval
3. Quality Assurance Manager approval

## 6. QUALITY ASSURANCE

### 6.1 Sampling Audit

**Quarterly Sampling Compliance Audit (by QA Manager):**
- Review 5-10 completed sampling records
- Assess compliance with this SOP
- Verify sample integrity maintained
- Check documentation completeness
- Verify chain of custody maintained
- Document audit findings
- Issue corrective actions for deficiencies

**Personnel Authorized for Sampling:**
- Attend initial 8-hour Sampling Training course
- Practical demonstration of sampling technique
- Written assessment of understanding
- Supervised sampling of 3-5 batches
- Formal sign-off by Head of QC before independent sampling

**Annual Refresher Training (minimum 2 hours):**
- Review of SOP changes
- Review of sampling audit findings
