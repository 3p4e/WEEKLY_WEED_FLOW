## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

### 1.1 PURPOSE

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes the Quality Control (QC) Laboratory as an independent organizational function within Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility, with clearly defined organizational structure, responsibilities, facilities, and equipment to ensure compliance with EU GMP Chapter 6 requirements.

**This SOP establishes:**
- The organizational independence of QC from all other departments
- Authority, qualifications, and responsibilities of Head of Quality Control
- QC Department structure, staffing, and operational hierarchy
- Facilities, equipment, and resource requirements for QC laboratories
- Procedures for personnel competency and training within QC
- Quality assurance oversight mechanisms for QC operations
- Interface between QC and other departments

## 2. DEFINITIONS & ABBREVIATIONS

### 2.1 KEY DEFINITIONS

**Quality Control (QC):** The set of operations intended to verify and maintain all aspects of the quality of a pharmaceutical product, including chemical, physical, biological, and microbiological characteristics.

**Quality Control Department:** An independent organizational function responsible for all testing, sampling, specifications development, documentation, and quality decisions that impact product safety and efficacy.

**Head of Quality Control (Laboratory Director):** A qualified natural person with appropriate education, training, and experience who bears responsibility for establishing, implementing, and overseeing all Quality Control procedures and ensuring independence from Production.

**Independence (QC Independence):** The structural, organizational, and operational separation of Quality Control from Production and other departments to ensure unbiased quality decision-making.

**Control Laboratory:** The facility or facilities designated for performing analytical testing, microbial analysis, stability testing, and other quality control procedures.

**Reference Material (RM):** Certified material with one or more properties that can be used to calibrate analytical equipment and verify analytical method performance.

**Retention Sample:** A sample of finished product or intermediate material retained for future testing, investigation, or regulatory compliance purposes.

**Cross-Contamination:** Unintended transfer of material, microorganisms, or contaminants from one area or sample to another.

## 3. RESPONSIBILITIES AND AUTHORITY

### 3.1 HEAD OF QUALITY CONTROL

**Reports to:** Quality Assurance Manager
**Direct Reports:** QC Analysts, QC Equipment Technician

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**
- Establish, validate, and maintain all Quality Control procedures
- Ensure all laboratory operations comply with EU GMP
- Oversee all analytical testing, sampling programs, and quality decisions
- Approve or reject all batches based on testing results
- Define and maintain quality specifications for all products
- Manage reference and retention sample programs
- Ensure competency of all QC personnel through training
- Manage laboratory equipment maintenance, calibration, and qualification
- Ensure proper documentation and archival of all QC records

**AUTHORITY - Can Independently:**
✅ Release or rejection of batches based on test results
✅ Approve analytical methods and testing procedures
✅ Approve equipment maintenance and calibration programs
✅ Revise QC Standard Operating Procedures
✅ Assess and certify competency of QC personnel
✅ Full access to all production areas for sampling and investigations

**Cannot Independently (Requires Approval):**
❌ Change quality specifications (requires QA Manager + QP approval)
❌ Override decision from Qualified Person on batch release
❌ Allocate capital funds >$10,000

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:**
- Bachelor's degree in Pharmacy, Chemistry, or Analytical Sciences
- Minimum 5 years of pharmaceutical or cannabis quality control experience
- EU GMP training certification (minimum 40 hours)
- Familiarity with HPLC and analytical chemistry methods
- Experience with Good Documentation Practice (GDP)
- Training in cannabinoid analysis and cannabis product testing
- Knowledge of North Macedonia pharmaceutical regulations

### 3.2 QUALITY CONTROL ANALYST

**Reports to:** Head of Quality Control

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**
- Receive, store, and prepare quality control samples
- Perform analytical testing following validated methods
- Record all testing results accurately and contemporaneously
- Monitor analytical results against acceptance criteria
- Identify Out-of-Specification (OOS) results immediately
- Alert QC Supervisor/Head immediately if OOS detected
- Maintain laboratory cleanliness and good housekeeping
- Adhere to laboratory safety procedures and PPE requirements
- Participate in required training programs

**AUTHORITY - Can Independently:**
✅ Perform testing within scope of validated and approved methods
✅ Record testing results accurately and completely
✅ Alert management to OOS or concerning results
✅ Recommend sample rejection for integrity issues

**Cannot Independently:**
❌ Modify analytical procedures or parameters
❌ Release batches or make accept/reject decisions
❌ Change testing acceptance criteria
❌ Approve method validation or changes

## 4. PROCEDURE

### 4.1 QUALITY CONTROL DEPARTMENT ORGANIZATION

**4.1.1 Organizational Independence**

The Quality Control Department is established as an **independent organizational function** with the following structural characteristics:

**Independence Principle:**
- QC is NOT subordinate to Production Department for operational decisions
- QC maintains independent authority over quality-related decisions
- QC reports to Quality Assurance Manager for oversight
- QC cannot be pressured to approve non-conforming quality

**Organizational Chart:**

```
Facility Manager (Stefan Petrov)
    ↓
Quality Assurance Manager (Ana Dimitrova)
    ├─ Head of Quality Control ← INDEPENDENT AUTHORITY
    │  ├─ QC Analyst 1
    │  └─ QC Analyst 2
    │
    ├─ Qualified Person (Blagoj Nikolov)
    │  └─ Final batch release authority
```

**Key Independence Provisions:**

1. **Functional Independence:**
   - QC establishes acceptance criteria independently
   - QC testing results reviewed and interpreted by QC personnel only
   - QC has authority to delay batch release for quality reasons
   - QC can reject batches without Production override

2. **Physical Independence:**
   - QC laboratory facilities physically separated from Production areas
   - QC sampling done independently
   - QC equipment dedicated to QC use
   - Microbiological lab separated to prevent cross-contamination

3. **Personnel Independence:**
   - QC personnel have no reporting relationship to Production
   - QC staff cannot be reassigned to Production without Management approval
   - Personnel performing testing have no conflict of interest in outcomes

4. **Decision Independence:**
   - QC recommendations on batch release are binding unless QP overrides
   - Production cannot modify QC test methods
   - QC can access production records and areas for quality investigations

   - Management review confirms selection

**Professional Experience:**

**Training and Certifications:**
✓ GMP training certification (minimum 40 hours, current within 3 years)

**Technical Knowledge:**

### 4.3 QUALITY CONTROL FACILITIES REQUIREMENTS

**4.3.1 Facility Location and Design**

**General Laboratory Area:**

Location:
- Dedicated laboratory space physically separated from Production areas
- Proximity to Production sufficient for sampling efficiency
- Assigned location: [ROOM_NUMBERS - TBD]

Area Classification:
- Grade D for non-critical functions (sample receipt, record keeping)
- Appropriate environmental controls for sensitive equipment areas
- Microbiological laboratory area with enhanced contamination control

Environmental Controls:
- Temperature controlled to ±2°C of target (typically 18-25°C)
- Relative humidity controlled to ±5% of target (typically 45-55%)
- Adequate lighting for analytical work (minimum 500 lux)
- Controlled air pressure and ventilation

**4.3.2 Microbiological Laboratory Segregation**

The microbiological laboratory shall be physically segregated:
- Separate room or clearly separated area within main QC lab
- Dedicated equipment not used in other laboratory areas
- One-way airflow from clean areas
- Dedicated waste disposal facilities
- Access restrictions to trained personnel only
- Dedicated storage for microbiological media and cultures

**4.3.3 Sample Storage and Reference Materials Area**

Storage Facility:
- Temperature and humidity controlled storage
- Segregation of retained samples, reference standards
- Organization system for quick retrieval
- First-in, first-out (FIFO) system for sample aging
- Security and access control to authorized personnel

Reference Material Storage:
- Climate-controlled storage (temperature: 15-25°C, humidity: 40-60%)
- Storage conditions per certificate of analysis
- Protection from light exposure if applicable
- Organization by material type and expiration date

### 4.5 QUALITY CONTROL PERSONNEL COMPETENCY

**4.5.1 Initial Competency Assessment**

1. **Education and Experience Review**
   - Academic qualifications evaluated
   - Prior experience in QC or analytical testing reviewed
   - Certifications and training records verified

2. **Technical Knowledge Assessment**
   - Written examination on GMP principles and laboratory safety
   - Verbal assessment of understanding of test methods
   - Practical demonstration of analytical technique

3. **Supervised Work Period**
   - Minimum 4-week supervised period (8 hours/day)
   - Shadowing of experienced analyst
   - Performing tests under direct observation
   - Gradual increase in independence

4. **Formal Competency Sign-Off**
   - Head of QC formally documents competency achievement
   - Permission to perform testing independently issued
   - Competency record maintained in personnel file

**4.5.2 Ongoing Competency Maintenance**

**Annual Competency Requirements:**

1. **Annual Refresher Training (minimum 16 hours):**
   - GMP and GDP refresher (4 hours)
   - Safety and laboratory practices (2 hours)
   - Method-specific technical training (6 hours)
   - Cannabis product knowledge update (2 hours)

2. **Annual Competency Assessment:**
   - Review of testing performance and accuracy
   - Assessment of record quality and documentation
   - Review of any errors or discrepancies

3. **Proficiency Testing:**
   - Quarterly performance of reference material testing
   - Results compared to expected values
   - Repeatability and reproducibility assessed

## 6. TRAINING & COMPETENCY

### 6.1 QUALITY CONTROL TRAINING REQUIREMENTS

**Initial Training (Before Independent Performance):**

1. **GMP Principles Training (16 hours)**
   - Overview of Good Manufacturing Practice requirements
   - EU GMP Chapter 2-6 key requirements
   - Cannabis industry GMP considerations

2. **Laboratory Safety Training (8 hours)**
   - Chemical safety and hazardous material handling
   - Laboratory emergency procedures
   - Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) requirements

3. **Good Documentation Practice (GDP) (8 hours)**
   - ALCOA+ principles overview
   - Electronic records and signatures
   - Laboratory notebook standards

4. **Cannabis Product Knowledge (8 hours)**
   - Cannabis plant biology and cannabinoid content
   - Legal status in North Macedonia and EU
   - Product forms manufactured

5. **Quality Control Department Orientation (4 hours)**
   - Department organization and personnel roles
   - Equipment inventory and capabilities
   - Quality specifications for products

**Method-Specific Training (Before Using Method):**
- 4-8 hours of hands-on training from experienced operator
- Theory of the analytical method
- Equipment operation and calibration
- Sample preparation and analysis
- Result interpretation and acceptance criteria
- Signed verification of competency by trainer

### 6.2 ONGOING COMPETENCY MAINTENANCE

**Annual Refresher Training (Minimum 16 hours):**
1. GMP Refresher (4 hours)
2. Technical Updates (6 hours)
3. Quality System Updates (4 hours)

**Competency Assessment Schedule:**
- Quarterly: Review of testing accuracy, documentation quality
- Semi-Annually: Proficiency testing with reference materials
- Annually: Formal competency assessment by Head of QC
