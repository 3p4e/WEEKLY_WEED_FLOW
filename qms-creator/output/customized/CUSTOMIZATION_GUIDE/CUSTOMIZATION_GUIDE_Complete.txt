1. GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

WHAT'S INCLUDED IN THE TEMPLATE PACKAGE:

Your QMS template package contains:

✓ Master Documents (Quality Manual, Facility Profile, Organizational Templates)
✓ System SOPs (Governance procedures - Quality, Sanitation, Production, Security, etc.)
✓ Operational SOPs (Step-by-step procedures for specific activities)
✓ Forms & Templates (Batch records, logs, certificates, checklists)
✓ Customization Guide (This document)
✓ Document Registry (Complete list of all documents)
✓ Implementation Checklist (What to do first)

PREPARATION CHECKLIST:

Before you begin customizing, gather the following information:

□ Company legal name, registration number, facility address
□ Names and qualifications of key personnel (Facility Manager, QA Manager, 
  Production Manager, Security Manager, HR Manager, etc.)
□ License number and regulatory contact information
□ Description of your facility layout and operations
□ List of equipment and testing capabilities
□ Personnel training schedule and requirements
□ Existing operating procedures (if any)
□ Regulatory authority contact details

RECOMMENDED WORKFLOW:

1. Start with MASTER DOCUMENTS first (Quality Manual, Facility Profile)
2. Then SYSTEM SOPs (Quality, Documentation, Training, Validation)
3. Then OPERATIONAL SOPs (Cultivation, Production, Testing)
4. Finally FORMS & TEMPLATES (Logs, records, certificates)

WHY? Master documents and system SOPs define the framework; operational 
procedures and forms implement the system.

2. PLACEHOLDER IDENTIFICATION & REPLACEMENT
================================================================================

WHAT ARE PLACEHOLDERS?

Placeholders are facility-specific fields marked with [SQUARE BRACKETS AND 
CAPITAL LETTERS]. They indicate where YOUR facility information goes.

TYPES OF PLACEHOLDERS:

Type 1: REQUIRED Single-Value Placeholders
Format: Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility, Stefan Petrov, +389 70 123 456
Action: Replace with single facility-specific value
Example: 
  BEFORE: "Facility Name: Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility"
  AFTER:  "Facility Name: Purely Plant GmbH"

Type 2: OPTIONAL Conditional Placeholders
Format: Section applies if in-house lab exists ... End of conditional section
Action: DELETE the entire section if it doesn't apply to you
Example:
  Original: "We conduct testing Section applies if in-house lab exists in our laboratory 
            End of conditional section Section applies if using contract laboratory through an external laboratory 
            End of conditional section"
  If using contract lab only: "We conduct testing through an external laboratory"

Type 3: LIST/ARRAY PLACEHOLDERS
Format: Zero critical deviations per quarter, 100% batch release compliance with specifications, 95%+ personnel training completion rate
Action: Replace with your facility's actual items
Example:
  BEFORE: "Quality Objectives: Zero critical deviations per quarter, 100% batch release compliance with specifications, 95%+ personnel training completion rate"
  AFTER:  "Quality Objectives: Zero critical deviations, 100% training 
           completion, regulatory compliance"

Type 4: CHECKBOX PLACEHOLDERS
Format: ☐ Cannabis cultivation, processing, and packaging operations
Action: Check the box (☑) if applicable; delete rows that don't apply
Example:
  ☑ Cultivation (Mother plants, cloning, vegetative growth)
  ☐ Testing Services for External Clients
  ☑ Packaging & Labeling

4. DOCUMENT-SPECIFIC CUSTOMIZATION NOTES
================================================================================

QUALITY MANUAL (QAS-01-001)
Key Sections to Customize:
  • Facility description and scope
  • Management structure and contact info
  • Quality policy and objectives (very important!)
  • Roles and responsibilities
  • Regulatory framework and authority contacts
  • Document retention periods (based on YOUR requirements)

Red Flags to Watch:
  • Ensure quality policy reflects YOUR facility's actual commitment
  • Verify all regulatory contacts are current
  • Ensure roles/responsibilities match your actual organization

VALIDATION MASTER PLAN (QAS-03-005)
Key Sections to Customize:
  • List of equipment that requires qualification
  • Timeline for validation activities
  • Responsible persons and approval chain
  • Scope of validation (what's included/excluded)

Note: Validation is technically complex - consider engaging a consultant if new to GMP

SYSTEM SOPs (e.g., Quality, Documentation, Validation)
Key Sections to Customize:
  • Responsibility assignments
  • Specific processes as they occur at YOUR facility
  • Approval requirements and signatures
  • Document references (update to new code system)
  • Forms you'll actually use

Common Mistakes:
  • Copying procedures verbatim without customizing for your facility
  • Not updating document cross-references to new codes
  • Forgetting to assign specific people responsible
  • Not accounting for differences between small and large facilities

OPERATIONAL SOPs (Cultivation, Production, Testing)
Key Sections to Customize:
  • Step-by-step procedures EXACTLY as conducted at your facility
  • Specific equipment, techniques, and parameters you use
  • Environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, light)
  • Safety procedures specific to your facility
  • Waste disposal procedures

Critical: These must be factually accurate! Personnel will follow these SOPs daily.

FORMS & TEMPLATES (Logs, Records, Certificates)
Minimal Customization Needed:
  • Replace Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility with your company name
  • Customize for equipment you actually have
  • Add facility-specific information fields
  • Customize signature blocks based on approval chain

Testing: Print test versions and have end-users provide feedback before finalizing

□ Are all placeholders completed with actual values?

□ Are all signatures and dates present?
□ Are cross-references updated to new document codes?

───────────────────────

APPROVAL SIGN-OFF

Once customized documents have been reviewed and tested, they require formal 
approval before implementation.

APPROVAL CHAIN (Standard):

1. Document Creator/Originator
   Signature: ________________  Date: _______
   (Confirms document accuracy and completeness)

2. Department Manager / Subject Matter Expert
   Signature: ________________  Date: _______
   (Confirms operational accuracy)

3. Quality Assurance Manager
   Signature: ________________  Date: _______
   (Confirms GMP compliance)

4. Facility Manager / Director
   Signature: ________________  Date: _______
   (Final authorization for implementation)

IMPLEMENTATION STEPS

Once approved:

1. Print master controlled copies
   - Number each copy (e.g., "CONTROLLED COPY #1 of 3")
   - Mark with controlled copy stamp
   - Record in Master Document List

2. Distribute to relevant personnel
   - Maintain distribution list (who has which copy)
   - Require signed acknowledgment of receipt
   - Train on new procedures before implementation

3. Archive superseded versions
   - Mark with date of supersession
   - Store in archive location
   - Note in document version history

4. Post implementation notice
   - Notify all personnel of new procedure
   - Schedule training on updated procedures
   - Conduct meetings to explain changes

5. Monitor compliance
   - Observe first 2-4 weeks of implementation
   - Collect feedback from users
   - Address any issues or questions
   - Document any deviations

7. TROUBLESHOOTING & SUPPORT
================================================================================

COMMON CUSTOMIZATION ISSUES & SOLUTIONS

Issue 1: "There are too many placeholders - where do I start?"
→ Start with FACILITY PROFILE SHEET first. This single document becomes your 
  reference, so fill it out completely. Then reference it for all other documents.

Issue 2: "Some procedures in the templates don't match our actual operations"
→ CORRECT THE PROCEDURE! The templates are generic - you should customize every 
  procedure to match YOUR facility's actual processes. This is required for GMP 
  compliance and personnel to follow procedures correctly.

Issue 3: "I'm not sure if we have adequate approval signatures"
→ Minimum: Document Creator → Department Manager → Quality Manager → Facility Manager
  You can add additional approvals as needed. Define your approval chain in 
  the Quality Manual and use it consistently.

Issue 4: "What if we need to change a procedure after approval?"
→ Use CHANGE CONTROL process (SOP-QAS-08). All changes to approved procedures 
  must go through change control, documented, and re-approved.

Issue 5: "Who should review equipment-specific procedures?"
→ Equipment operators, maintenance technicians, and the equipment manufacturer's 
  documentation. Ensure procedures match manufacturer recommendations.

Issue 6: "How do we handle procedures for equipment we don't have?"
→ DELETE those documents or clearly mark them as "Not Applicable" with a change 
  control record explaining why they were removed.

WHEN TO SEEK PROFESSIONAL HELP

Consider engaging a GMP consultant if:
✓ You're new to EU GMP compliance
✓ You need validation protocols
✓ You're uncertain about regulatory requirements
✓ You need help with complex procedures
✓ You're preparing for a regulatory inspection

8. VERSION CONTROL FOR CUSTOMIZED DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

VERSION NUMBERING SYSTEM

Format: V.Major.Minor
Examples: V.1.0, V.1.1, V.2.0, V.3.2

When to Increment:

V.1.0 → V.1.1 (Minor version change)
  - Typo or formatting corrections
  - Small clarifications that don't change procedure
  - Update of contact information or non-critical details

V.1.1 → V.2.0 (Major version change)
  - Significant procedure changes
  - Changes in responsibility or approval chains
  - Changes to regulatory requirements
  - Changes that require personnel retraining

VERSION TRACKING

Maintain a VERSION HISTORY section in each document:

Version | Date       | Description          | Changed By        | Approved By
--------|------------|----------------------|-------------------|---------------
1.0     | 01/01/2026 | Initial customization | Blagoj Nikolov       | Stefan Petrov
1.1     | 15/02/2026 | Updated contact info  | Blagoj Nikolov       | Stefan Petrov
2.0     | 01/06/2026 | Procedure revision    | Blagoj Nikolov       | Stefan Petrov

COMMUNICATING CHANGES

When you update a document:
1. Note the version change in the header
2. Highlight what changed (use Track Changes in Word if available)
3. Notify affected personnel
4. Conduct training on new procedures (if Version 2.0+)
5. Maintain archive copy of previous version
6. Update cross-references in related documents
