## FORM USAGE NOTES

### When to Use This Form:

**YES - Use CAPA Request Form:**
- Out-of-specification results or nonconformances
- Product quality issues
- Deviations from procedures
- Customer complaints
- Equipment failures or process upsets
- Regulatory observations or findings
- Recurring problems (3+ occurrences)
- Preventive action for identified risks

### Tips for Completing Form:

1. **Be Specific**: Vague descriptions delay investigation. Provide exact data, dates, and details.
2. **Include All Evidence**: Attach batch records, test results, deviation reports as references.
3. **Assess Severity Honestly**: Accurate severity rating ensures appropriate timeline and resources.
4. **Identify Similar Issues**: Note if this problem has occurred before (look for previous deviations/CAPAs).
5. **Recommend Investigation Approach**: Your input on root cause method helps focus the investigation.
6. **Ask Questions**: Contact QA Manager if unsure how to complete any section.

### Form Submission:

**Submit completed form to:**
- Quality Assurance Manager
- Email: ana.dimitrova@purelyplant.mk
- Physical location: QA Office

**Response Time:**
- Low-risk issues: CAPAN assigned within 1 working day
- Medium-risk issues: CAPAN assigned within 24 hours
- High/Critical issues: CAPAN assigned immediately with same-day RCA meeting notification
