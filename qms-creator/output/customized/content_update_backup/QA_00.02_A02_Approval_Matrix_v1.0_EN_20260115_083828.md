## PURPOSE

This annex defines the approval requirements for all types of GMP documents within the Quality Management System, ensuring appropriate review and authorization based on document criticality and regulatory impact.

---

## 1. APPROVAL LEVELS DEFINITION

### Level 1: CRITICAL DOCUMENTS
**Definition:** Documents with direct impact on product quality, patient safety, or regulatory compliance.

**Approval Sequence:**
1. **Prepared by:** Document Author / Subject Matter Expert
2. **Reviewed by:** Quality Manager
3. **Approved by:** Qualified Person (QP)

**Examples:**
- Quality Manual (QA_00.01_v1)
- Batch Release SOP
- Product Specifications
- Batch Production Records (Master)
- Qualification/Validation Master Plans
- Site Master File

---

### Level 2: HIGH PRIORITY DOCUMENTS
**Definition:** Documents with significant impact on GMP compliance and operational quality.

**Approval Sequence:**
1. **Prepared by:** Document Author / Subject Matter Expert
2. **Reviewed by:** Departmental Manager
3. **Approved by:** Quality Manager

**Examples:**
- Quality Control SOPs
- Production SOPs
- Validation Protocols
- Cleaning/Sanitation SOPs
- Deviation Management procedures
- CAPA procedures
- Audit procedures

---

### Level 3: STANDARD DOCUMENTS
**Definition:** Supporting documents for GMP operations without direct product impact.

**Approval Sequence:**
1. **Prepared by:** Document Author
2. **Reviewed by:** Quality Assurance Manager
3. **Approved by:** Departmental Manager

**Examples:**
- Equipment operation instructions
- Facility maintenance procedures
- Standard forms and checklists
- Training materials
- Logbook templates
- Administrative procedures

---

## 3. APPROVAL AUTHORITY DEFINITIONS

### 3.1 Qualified Person (QP)

**Authority:**
- Final approval for all **Level 1 Critical Documents**
- Batch release certification
- Regulatory submissions
- Site Master File approval
- Deviation from approval matrix (exceptional cases)

**Qualifications:**
- Per EU Directive 2001/83/EC Article 49 and Annex XVI
- Registered Pharmacist or equivalent
- Documented GMP training and experience

**Delegation:**
- QP may delegate review but NOT final approval for Level 1 documents
- Delegation documented in writing with clear scope and limitations

---

### 3.2 Quality Manager

**Authority:**
- Approval of all **Level 2 High Priority Documents**
- Review of all **Level 1 Critical Documents** before QP approval
- Management of Document Approval Matrix
- Approval of deviations from standard document format

**Qualifications:**
- Minimum 5 years pharmaceutical/GMP experience
- Degree in pharmaceutical sciences, chemistry, or related field
- Documented GMP training

---

### 3.3 Quality Assurance Manager

**Authority:**
- Review of all **Level 3 Standard Documents**
- Day-to-day document control system management
- Assignment of document codes
- Enforcement of approval matrix compliance

**Qualifications:**
- Minimum 3 years GMP experience
- Technical degree or equivalent
- Document control system training

---

### 3.4 Departmental Managers

**Authority:**
- Approval of **Level 3 Standard Documents** for their department
- Review of **Level 2** documents related to their area
- Identification of training needs related to new/revised documents

**Departments:**
- Production Manager
- QC Manager
- Engineering Manager
- Facilities Manager
- Warehouse Manager
- HR Manager

---

### 3.5 Document Authors / Subject Matter Experts

**Responsibility:**
- Technical accuracy of document content
- Compliance with applicable regulations
- Clarity and usability of procedures
- Responding to reviewer comments

**Qualifications:**
- Technical expertise in subject area
- Training on document preparation (QA_00.02_A02_v1)
- Training on Good Documentation Practice

---

## 4. SPECIAL APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS

### 4.1 Urgent Documents (Expedited Approval)

**Criteria:**
- Regulatory requirement with short deadline
- Critical safety issue requiring immediate SOP update
- Production stoppage requiring immediate procedure clarification

**Process:**
- QP may authorize expedited review (5 working days instead of 10)
- May reduce effective date to 7 days (instead of 30)
- Expedited approval documented with justification

---

### 4.2 Interim Approvals (Temporary Procedures)

**Purpose:**
- Emergency situations requiring immediate procedural guidance
- Maximum validity: 90 days

**Requirements:**
- Interim SOP marked "INTERIM - Valid until [Date]"
- Requires same approval level as permanent SOP
- Must be replaced by permanent SOP before expiry
- Extension beyond 90 days requires QP written approval

---

### 4.3 External Documents (Vendor Manuals, Regulatory Guidance)

**Review and Acceptance:**
- Equipment manuals: Reviewed by Engineering Manager, accepted by QA Manager
- Regulatory guidance (EudraLex, ICH): Reviewed by QA Manager, accepted by Quality Manager
- External audit reports: Reviewed by Quality Manager, accepted by QP

**Controlled Distribution:**
- External documents entered into Document Control Register
- Assigned unique reference code (EXT-[Category]-[Number])
- Distributed and controlled per QA_00.02_A02_v1

---

### 4.4 Multi-Departmental Documents

**Procedure:**
- Documents affecting multiple departments require review by all affected Department Managers
- Final approval per document criticality level
- All departmental reviews documented before final approval

**Example:** SOP for Batch Release (affects Production, QC, QA, Warehouse)
- Reviewed by: Production Manager, QC Manager, Warehouse Manager, Quality Manager
- Approved by: QP (Level 1 document)

## 5. APPROVAL WORKFLOW

### 5.1 Standard Workflow

```
Document Prepared
  ↓
Author Self-Review
  ↓
Submit to QA Manager
  ↓
QA Manager assigns to Reviewer(s) per Approval Matrix
  ↓
Reviewer(s) review document (10 working days max)
  ↓
Author addresses comments
  ↓
Significant changes? → YES → Re-submit for review
  ↓ NO
Route to Approver per Approval Matrix
  ↓
Approver reviews and approves
  ↓
Document effective per QA_00.02_A02_v1 Section 5.3.3
```

### 5.2 Electronic Approval Workflow

For facilities using validated electronic document management systems:

- Electronic signatures compliant with EU Annex 11 / 21 CFR Part 11
- Approval routing automated per this matrix
- Audit trail captures: who, what, when for all approvals
- Electronic approval has same regulatory weight as wet-ink signature

## 6. APPROVAL MATRIX MAINTENANCE

### 6.1 Review and Updates

**Review Frequency:**
- Annual review by Quality Manager
- Updated whenever organizational structure changes
- Updated when new document types created

**Update Process:**
- Changes to matrix follow QA_00.05_v1 Change Control SOP
- Updated matrix requires QP approval
- All document authors notified of changes

### 6.2 Deviations from Approval Matrix

**Permitted Deviations:**
- Substitute approver (temporary absence, delegation)
- Additional approvers (for multi-departmental SOPs)

**Documentation:**
- Deviation documented with justification
- Substitute approver must have equivalent qualifications
- QP approval required for deviations from Level 1 approvals

---

## 7. TRAINING ON APPROVAL MATRIX

**Required Training:**
- All document authors: Initial and every 24 months
- All approvers: Initial and upon appointment to role
- QA personnel: Comprehensive training on matrix enforcement

**Training Content:**
- Understanding document criticality levels
- Approval sequence for each document type
- Responsibilities of each approval role
- Deviation and expedited approval processes
