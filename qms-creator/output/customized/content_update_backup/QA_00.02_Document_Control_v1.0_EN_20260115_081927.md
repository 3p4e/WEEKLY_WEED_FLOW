## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes the requirements and procedures for the creation, review, approval, distribution, revision, retirement, and archiving of all documents within the Quality Management System (QMS) of Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility.

1.2 To ensure that all GMP documentation is controlled, accurate, current, and compliant with EU GMP requirements as specified in EudraLex Volume 4 Chapter 4 - Documentation.

1.3 To maintain document integrity throughout the document lifecycle in accordance with ALCOA+ principles (Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available).

---

## 2. SCOPE

2.1 This SOP applies to all documents within the Quality Management System including, but not limited to:

**Instruction-Type Documents:**
- Quality Manual and Facility Profile
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
- Specifications (starting materials, packaging materials, finished products)
- Manufacturing Formulae and Processing Instructions
- Batch Manufacturing Records (BMRs)
- Packaging Instructions
- Analytical Methods and Test Procedures
- Validation Protocols and Master Plans
- Technical Agreements
- Site Master File

**Record/Report-Type Documents:**
- Batch Production Records
- Laboratory Records and Results
- Certificates of Analysis (CoA)
- Validation Reports
- Deviation and Investigation Reports
- Change Control Records
- CAPA Records
- Audit Reports
- Training Records
- Equipment Logbooks and Maintenance Records

2.2 This SOP applies to all document formats including paper-based, electronic, and hybrid systems.

2.3 This SOP applies to all personnel involved in creating, reviewing, approving, using, or managing QMS documents at Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility.

## 4. DEFINITIONS

**ALCOA+:** Data integrity principles - Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available.

**Approval:** Formal authorization by designated personnel that a document is suitable for its intended use.

**Archive:** Long-term secure storage of documents per retention requirements.

**Controlled Document:** A document managed under this document control system with unique identifier, approval status, and distribution tracking.

**Document Control Register:** Master list of all controlled documents showing current version, status, and location.

**Effective Date:** The date on which an approved document comes into operational use.

**Electronic Signature:** Computer-generated identifier that authenticates the identity of the signer, compliant with 21 CFR Part 11 or EU Annex 11 where applicable.

**Good Documentation Practice (GDP):** Principles ensuring documentation is contemporaneous, accurate, legible, indelible, and compliant with GMP.

**Master Document:** The original approved document from which working copies are reproduced.

**Obsolete Document:** A document that has been superseded by a new version or is no longer in use.

**Quality Management System (QMS):** Formalized system documenting processes, procedures, and responsibilities for achieving quality policies and objectives.

**Revision:** A change to an approved document that requires re-approval and issuance of a new version.

**Review:** Periodic evaluation of document content for continued accuracy, suitability, and compliance.

**Supersede:** To replace an existing document with a new version.

**Uncontrolled Copy:** A document copy not tracked in the distribution system, typically for reference only (clearly marked "UNCONTROLLED COPY").

**Version:** Unique identifier (e.g., 1.0, 1.1, 2.0) indicating the revision status of a document.

## 5. PROCEDURE

### 5.1 Document Types and Classification

#### 5.1.1 Document Coding System

All controlled documents shall be assigned a unique identification code following the format:

**[CHAPTER]-Master Documents-[XXX]_v1.0**

**Chapter Codes:**
- QAS: Quality Assurance System
- QCS: Quality Control & Sampling
- VAL: Validation & Qualification
- SAN: Cleaning & Sanitation
- PRO: Production Operations
- MAT: Materials Management
- EQU: Equipment Management
- FAC: Facilities & Utilities
- HRM: Human Resources & Training
- SEC: Security & Access Control
- REC: Recall & Traceability
- LOG: Logistics & Distribution

**Category Codes:**
- 00: Master/Policy Documents
- 01-10: Core procedures
- 11-99: Specific procedures

**Example:** QA_00.02_v1 = Quality Assurance System, Master Document, Second SOP, Version 1.0

#### 5.1.2 Document Classification by Criticality

**Critical Documents (Level 1):**
- Require QP approval
- Direct impact on product quality and patient safety
- Examples: Quality Manual, Batch Release SOP, Specifications, Manufacturing Instructions

**High Priority Documents (Level 2):**
- Require Quality Manager approval
- Significant impact on GMP compliance
- Examples: Quality Control SOPs, Validation Protocols, Deviation Procedures

**Standard Documents (Level 3):**
- Require Departmental Manager approval
- Support GMP operations
- Examples: Equipment operation SOPs, Cleaning procedures, Training records

Refer to **Annex A02: Document Approval Matrix** for complete approval requirements.

### 5.2 Document Creation and Preparation

#### 5.2.1 Document Initiation

1. **Needs Assessment:**
   - Document Author or Department Manager identifies need for new document
   - Completes Document Creation Request form (if applicable)
   - Obtains approval from Quality Assurance Manager to proceed

2. **Template Selection:**
   - Select appropriate document template from approved templates (see **Annex A01: Document Template Standards**)
   - For SOPs: Use Standard SOP Template
   - For Forms: Use Standard Form Template
   - For Protocols: Use Validation Protocol Template

3. **Document Code Assignment:**
   - Contact Quality Assurance Manager to obtain unique document code
   - QA Manager assigns code and logs in Document Control Register

#### 5.2.2 Document Drafting Requirements

All documents shall be prepared according to the following requirements (per EU GMP Chapter 4):

**Format Requirements:**
- Use approved templates (Annex A01)
- Clear, unambiguous language
- Imperative mandatory style for SOPs ("shall," "must," not "should")
- Logical, orderly layout
- Easy to check and follow
- Adequate white space for readability
- Numbered sections and pages

**Content Requirements:**
- Unique document identifier
- Document title
- Version number
- Effective date and review date
- Approval signature block
- Purpose and scope clearly defined
- Responsibilities clearly assigned
- Procedures in step-by-step format
- References to related documents
- Annexes if applicable

**Technical Requirements:**
- Documents shall NOT be hand-written (except data entry fields)
- Documents shall be created using approved software ([DOCUMENT_SOFTWARE])
- Documents shall include sufficient space for required entries
- Electronic documents shall be created in validated systems where applicable

#### 5.2.3 Draft Document Review Cycle

1. **Author Self-Review:**
   - Author reviews draft for technical accuracy, completeness, and compliance
   - Performs spell-check and grammar review
   - Verifies all placeholders filled and cross-references accurate

2. **Peer Technical Review (optional but recommended):**
   - Subject matter expert reviews technical content
   - Feedback documented and incorporated

3. **Submission for Formal Review:**
   - Author submits draft to Quality Assurance Manager
   - QA Manager assigns to appropriate reviewers per Annex A02

### 5.3 Document Review and Approval

#### 5.3.1 Formal Review Process

1. **Review Assignment:**
   - QA Manager distributes document to designated reviewers per Annex A02
   - Review period: Maximum 10 working days (5 days for urgent documents)

2. **Reviewer Responsibilities:**
   - Review for compliance with regulatory requirements (EudraLex, ICH guidelines)
   - Verify consistency with related documents
   - Check clarity, completeness, and accuracy
   - Ensure feasibility and practicality
   - Document comments using Document Review Form

3. **Comment Resolution:**
   - Author addresses all reviewer comments
   - Documents resolution in Comment Resolution Log
   - Re-submits revised draft if significant changes made

#### 5.3.2 Approval Process

**Approval Sequence (per Annex A02):**

**Level 1 (Critical Documents):**
1. Prepared by: Document Author
2. Reviewed by: Quality Manager
3. Approved by: Qualified Person (QP)

**Level 2 (High Priority Documents):**
1. Prepared by: Document Author
2. Reviewed by: Departmental Manager
3. Approved by: Quality Manager

**Level 3 (Standard Documents):**
1. Prepared by: Document Author
2. Reviewed by: QA Manager
3. Approved by: Departmental Manager

**Approval Documentation:**
- Each approver signs and dates the Approval Signature Block
- Electronic signatures acceptable if validated system per EU Annex 11
- Wet ink signatures required for paper-based systems
- Approval signifies: document is accurate, complete, compliant, and suitable for use

#### 5.3.3 Effective Date Assignment

- **Effective Date:** The date the document becomes operational
- Standard: 30 days after final approval (allows time for training and preparation)
- Can be shortened to 7 days for urgent updates with QP approval
- Effective date clearly marked on document header
- Prior to effective date, document status = "APPROVED - PENDING IMPLEMENTATION"
- On effective date, document status = "EFFECTIVE"

### 5.4 Document Distribution and Access Control

#### 5.4.1 Master Document Storage

- **Master Document:** Original approved document with original signatures (or validated electronic equivalent)
- Storage location: Quality Assurance Document Control area
- Access: Restricted to QA Manager and designated QA personnel
- Security: Locked cabinet (paper) or password-protected folder (electronic)
- Backup: Master documents backed up weekly (electronic) or photocopied and stored off-site (paper)

#### 5.4.2 Controlled Copy Distribution

1. **Distribution List:**
   - QA Manager creates distribution list for each controlled document
   - Specifies recipient name, department, copy number, format (paper/electronic)
   - Recorded in Document Control Register

2. **Issuance of Controlled Copies:**
   - Each controlled copy marked with unique copy number (e.g., "Copy 1 of 5")
   - Paper copies: Stamped "CONTROLLED COPY" in red
   - Electronic copies: Watermarked "CONTROLLED COPY" and access-controlled
   - Recipient signs Document Receipt Log confirming receipt

3. **Access to Electronic Documents:**
   - Electronic documents stored in controlled shared drive: [DOCUMENT_STORAGE_LOCATION]
   - Access granted by QA Manager based on role and training status
   - Read-only access (no editing of master documents)
   - Automatic version control showing current version only

#### 5.4.3 Uncontrolled Copies

- Uncontrolled copies permitted for reference, training, external audit preparation
- Clearly marked "UNCONTROLLED COPY - FOR REFERENCE ONLY"
- Printed in different color (e.g., blue) or watermarked differently
- User responsible for verifying currency before use
- Not valid for operational use

### 5.5 Document Revision and Change Control

#### 5.5.1 Initiating Document Revision

**Triggers for Revision:**
- Scheduled periodic review (every 24 months per QAS-00-005 Change Control SOP)
- Regulatory requirement changes
- Process improvements or deviations/CAPAs
- Audit findings or inspector observations
- Equipment or facility changes
- Organizational changes

**Initiation Process:**
1. Initiator completes Document Change Request form (**Annex A03**)
2. Describes reason for change and impact assessment
3. Submits to QA Manager for evaluation
4. QA Manager determines if formal Change Control required (QAS-00-005)
5. If approved, initiates revision process

#### 5.5.2 Revision Process

1. **Retrieve Current Version:**
   - QA Manager retrieves current master document
   - Assigns new version number per versioning system:
     - **Minor changes** (typos, clarifications, formatting): Increment decimal (1.0 → 1.1)
     - **Major changes** (procedure changes, scope changes): Increment whole number (1.1 → 2.0)

2. **Document Revision:**
   - Author makes changes to document
   - Uses "Track Changes" feature or revision marks to show modifications
   - Updates version number, revision date, and "Supersedes" field
   - Creates Summary of Changes table listing all modifications

3. **Review and Approval:**
   - Revised document follows same review/approval process as new document (Section 5.3)
   - All original approvers must re-approve unless delegated
   - Changes highlighted for reviewer attention

4. **Implementation:**
   - New version assigned effective date (typically 30 days post-approval)
   - Training conducted if procedure changes affect operations
   - Training documented before new version effective date

#### 5.5.3 Version Control

**Version Numbering System:**
- Initial release: v1.0
- Minor revisions: v1.1, v1.2, v1.3...
- Major revisions: v2.0, v3.0, v4.0...
- Draft versions (before approval): v1.0_Draft, v2.0_Draft_Rev1

**Version History:**
- Every document includes "Revision History" table listing:
  - Version number
  - Effective date
  - Description of changes
  - Sections modified
  - Changed by (name)

**Superseded Document Handling:**
- See Section 5.6.2 and **Annex A04: Obsolete Document Handling**

### 5.6 Document Retention and Archiving

#### 5.6.1 Retention Requirements (per EU GMP 4.11-4.12)

**Batch-Related Documentation:**
- Retention period: **1 year after product expiry** OR **5 years after QP batch certification**, whichever is LONGER
- Examples: Batch Production Records, Packaging Records, QC test results, CoAs

**Investigational Medicinal Products:**
- Retention period: **5 years after clinical trial completion or formal discontinuation**

**Validation and Stability Data:**
- Retention period: **Lifetime of Marketing Authorization** or until superseded by full new dataset
- Examples: Validation protocols/reports, stability study data, method validation

**General QMS Documents:**
- SOPs, specifications, approved forms: **Lifetime of current version + 5 years after superseded**
- Training records: **Duration of employment + 5 years**
- Audit reports, CAPA records: **10 years minimum**
- Deviation and investigation records: **10 years minimum**
- Equipment qualification records: **Lifetime of equipment + 2 years**

**Exception:** Regulatory authority may specify longer retention for specific document types.

#### 5.6.2 Obsolete Document Handling (See Annex A04)

**Upon Effective Date of New Version:**

1. **Retrieval of Obsolete Copies:**
   - QA Manager notifies all controlled copy holders
   - Obsolete copies retrieved within 5 working days
   - Recipients sign confirmation of return and receipt of new version

2. **Marking Obsolete Documents:**
   - All obsolete copies stamped "OBSOLETE - DO NOT USE" in red diagonally across each page
   - Master copy marked "SUPERSEDED BY [Document Code] v[New Version]"
   - Effective date and superseded date recorded

3. **Archiving:**
   - One master copy of obsolete version archived per retention schedule
   - Stored in secure archive area separate from current documents
   - Indexed in Archive Register with location and retention expiry date

4. **Destruction:**
   - After retention period expires, obsolete documents destroyed
   - Destruction witnessed and documented in Document Destruction Log
   - Electronic files securely deleted (non-recoverable)

### 5.7 Document Inventory Management

#### 5.7.1 Document Control Register (Master List)

The QA Manager maintains a Document Control Register containing:

**For Each Controlled Document:**
- Document code
- Document title
- Document type
- Version number
- Effective date
- Review due date
- Approval status
- Approvers (names and dates)
- Distribution list (copy numbers and recipients)
- Storage location (master and controlled copies)
- Retention category
- Supersedes (previous version)
- Related documents (cross-references)

**Register Maintenance:**
- Updated within 24 hours of any document status change
- Reviewed monthly for accuracy
- Backed up weekly
- Available for audit at all times

#### 5.7.2 Periodic Review Schedule

**Scheduled Review Frequency:**
- **Critical Documents (Level 1):** Every 12 months
- **High Priority Documents (Level 2):** Every 24 months
- **Standard Documents (Level 3):** Every 36 months
- **Batch Records and Forms:** Reviewed upon any related SOP revision

**Review Process:**
1. QA Manager generates monthly list of documents due for review
2. Document owner notified 30 days before review due date
3. Owner conducts review and determines:
   - **No change required:** Document re-approved with date extension
   - **Minor updates needed:** Initiate revision per Section 5.5
   - **Major revision needed:** Initiate change control per QAS-00-005
4. Review completion documented in Document Control Register
5. If not reviewed by due date, document flagged "OVERDUE REVIEW" (escalated to Quality Manager)

### 5.8 Electronic Document Management

#### 5.8.1 Electronic Document Systems

Electronic documents shall be managed in validated computerized systems compliant with:
- EU GMP Annex 11: Computerized Systems
- 21 CFR Part 11 (if applicable)
- ICH Q10: Pharmaceutical Quality System

**System Requirements:**
- User access controls (role-based permissions)
- Audit trail (who, what, when for all changes)
- Electronic signatures (validated and secure)
- Version control (automatic)
- Backup and disaster recovery
- Data integrity (ALCOA+ compliant)

#### 5.8.2 Hybrid Systems

Where documents exist partially in electronic and partially in paper format:

- Clearly define which elements are electronic vs. paper
- Establish control measures for both formats
- Define master copy location and format
- Ensure integrity throughout retention period
- Document system design and controls in validation documentation

### 5.9 Training and Compliance

#### 5.9.1 Training Requirements

**All Personnel:**
- Initial training on this SOP (QA_00.02_v1) during GMP induction
- Re-training every 24 months or upon significant SOP revision
- Good Documentation Practice (GDP) training
- Training on specific document types relevant to their role

**Document Authors:**
- Advanced training on document creation and template use
- Training on regulatory requirements (EudraLex Chapter 4)
- Review and approval process training

**QA Personnel:**
- Comprehensive training on full document control system
- Training on Document Control Register management
- Electronic document system administration (if applicable)

**Training Documentation:**
- All training recorded in Training Database per HRM-02-001 Training Management SOP
- Training effectiveness verified through assessment
- Re-training triggered by errors in documentation practices

#### 5.9.2 Monitoring and Compliance

**Monthly Metrics:**
- Number of documents overdue for review
- Average time for document approval (target: ≤ 20 days)
- Number of obsolete documents not retrieved
- Number of document control deviations
- Training compliance rate

**Quarterly Audits:**
- Random sampling of controlled documents (minimum 10% of active documents)
- Verification of:
  - Current version in use at point of use
  - Obsolete documents removed
  - Signatures and dates complete
  - Document Control Register accuracy
- Audit findings addressed through CAPA system
