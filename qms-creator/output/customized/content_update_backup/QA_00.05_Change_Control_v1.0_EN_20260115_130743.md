## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes a systematic approach to identifying, evaluating, approving, implementing, and reviewing all changes that may impact product quality, GMP compliance, or regulatory status at Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility.

1.2 To ensure that all changes are appropriately authorized, documented, and validated before implementation, minimizing risks to product quality and patient safety.

1.3 To comply with ICH Q10 Pharmaceutical Quality System requirements for change management and control.

1.4 To provide a framework for risk-based decision making when evaluating proposed changes to facilities, equipment, processes, systems, materials, or documentation.

## 4. DEFINITIONS

**Change Control:** A formal system to ensure that changes to facilities, equipment, utilities, processes, computerized systems, materials, or documentation are evaluated, authorized, documented, and verified in a controlled manner.

**Change Control Board (CCB):** Cross-functional team responsible for reviewing, approving, and overseeing implementation of changes.

**Change Control Number (CCN):** Unique identifier assigned to each change control request for tracking purposes. Format: CC-YYYY-### (e.g., CC-2026-001).

**Critical Change:** A change with high potential impact on product quality, safety, efficacy, or GMP compliance. Requires extensive evaluation, validation, and QP approval.

**Impact Assessment:** Systematic evaluation of the potential effects of a proposed change on product quality, processes, systems, documentation, and compliance.

**Implementation Plan:** Detailed action plan specifying tasks, responsibilities, timelines, and success criteria for executing an approved change.

**Major Change:** A change with moderate to significant impact requiring CCB review and approval, possible validation/qualification, and formal documentation.

**Minor Change:** A change with minimal impact on product quality or GMP compliance, requiring departmental approval but not full CCB review.

**Planned Change:** A change initiated proactively to improve processes, comply with new regulations, or implement improvements.

**Risk Assessment:** Evaluation of the probability and severity of potential adverse outcomes resulting from a proposed change.

**Temporary Change:** A change implemented for a defined limited period (maximum 90 days) pending permanent solution. Requires same approval as permanent change.

**Unplanned Change:** A change necessitated by unexpected events such as equipment failure, supply disruption, or urgent regulatory requirement.

**Validation/Qualification Impact:** Assessment of whether a change requires new validation/qualification activities or updates to existing validation documentation.

## 5. PROCEDURE

### 5.1 Change Classification and Risk Assessment

#### 5.1.1 Change Categories

All changes shall be classified into one of three categories based on risk and impact:

**CRITICAL Changes (Level 1)**

**Definition:** Changes with potential to directly impact product quality, patient safety, or regulatory compliance.

**Characteristics:**
- Direct impact on Critical Quality Attributes (CQAs)
- Affects product safety or efficacy
- Changes to validated processes or systems
- Regulatory filing implications
- New equipment or facility areas for GMP operations

**Examples:**
- Changes to manufacturing process parameters within validated ranges
- New analytical method for release testing
- Change in active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) supplier
- HVAC system modification affecting cleanroom classification
- Software upgrade to validated computerized system
- Batch size changes outside validated range
- Change in sterilization parameters

**Approval Required:** Change Control Board + QP Final Approval

**Validation Impact:** Likely requires validation/qualification or revalidation

**Timeline:** Typically 60-180 days from request to implementation

---

**MAJOR Changes (Level 2)**

**Definition:** Changes with moderate impact on operations, processes, or systems, but indirect or minimal impact on product quality.

**Characteristics:**
- Affects supporting processes or utilities
- Impacts efficiency or capacity
- Requires procedural or documentation updates
- May require qualification or performance verification

**Examples:**
- Replacement of non-product contact equipment with equivalent model
- Changes to facility layout not affecting GMP areas
- Implementation of new document management system
- Addition of environmental monitoring points
- Changes to cleaning procedures for non-product contact surfaces
- Organizational structure changes (non-key personnel)
- Supplier change for non-critical materials (packaging, labels)

**Approval Required:** Change Control Board (QP approval optional, based on CCB decision)

**Validation Impact:** May require performance verification or installation qualification

**Timeline:** Typically 30-90 days from request to implementation

---

**MINOR Changes (Level 3)**

**Definition:** Changes with minimal or no impact on product quality, GMP compliance, or validated processes.

**Characteristics:**
- Administrative or cosmetic changes
- Equipment or facility changes not in GMP areas
- Non-GMP documentation updates
- Routine upgrades within specifications

**Examples:**
- Office equipment replacement
- Non-GMP area painting or maintenance
- Administrative software updates (email, word processing)
- Changes to non-GMP forms or templates
- Replacement of measuring device with calibrated equivalent (like-for-like)
- Minor SOP clarifications or corrections (managed under QA_00.02_v1)

**Approval Required:** Department Manager + QA Manager notification

**Validation Impact:** None

**Timeline:** Typically 7-30 days from request to implementation

---

#### 5.1.2 Risk Assessment Tools

**For all CRITICAL and MAJOR changes, use risk assessment methodology:**

**Risk Assessment Matrix:**

| **Impact →** | **Low** | **Medium** | **High** | **Critical** |
|--------------|---------|------------|----------|--------------|
| **Likelihood ↓** |  |  |  |  |
| **Almost Certain** | Medium | High | High | Critical |
| **Likely** | Low | Medium | High | Critical |
| **Possible** | Low | Medium | High | High |
| **Unlikely** | Low | Low | Medium | High |
| **Rare** | Low | Low | Low | Medium |

**Impact Criteria:**
- **Critical:** Direct impact on patient safety or product sterility/purity
- **High:** Potential impact on CQAs or critical process parameters
- **Medium:** Impact on supporting processes or quality attributes
- **Low:** Minimal impact on quality or compliance

**Likelihood Criteria:**
- **Almost Certain:** Expected to occur (>90% probability)
- **Likely:** Probably will occur (60-90% probability)
- **Possible:** Might occur (30-60% probability)
- **Unlikely:** Could occur but not expected (10-30% probability)
- **Rare:** May occur only in exceptional circumstances (<10% probability)

**Risk Levels:**
- **Critical Risk:** Unacceptable - Change should not proceed without significant mitigation
- **High Risk:** Requires extensive evaluation, validation, and mitigation measures
- **Medium Risk:** Requires standard change control with appropriate controls
- **Low Risk:** Acceptable with minimal controls

**Additional Risk Assessment Tools:**
- **FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis):** For complex process or equipment changes
- **HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Points):** For process changes affecting product safety
- **What-If Analysis:** For brainstorming potential failure scenarios
- **Fishbone Diagram:** To identify root causes and contributing factors

See **Annex A02: Risk Assessment Templates** for detailed forms and examples.

### 5.2 Change Control Process Flow

#### 5.2.1 Step 1: Change Identification and Initiation

**Who:** Any personnel identifying need for change

**Actions:**
1. **Identify Change Need:**
   - Proactive improvement opportunity
   - Regulatory requirement
   - Equipment failure or obsolescence
   - Process optimization
   - Deviation or CAPA-driven
   - Customer or supplier notification

2. **Complete Change Control Request Form** (**Annex A01**)
   - Describe current state vs. proposed change
   - Justify reason for change
   - Identify affected areas (products, processes, equipment, documents)
   - Preliminary risk assessment
   - Proposed implementation timeline

3. **Submit to Quality Assurance Manager**
   - Email submission or hand-delivery
   - Include all supporting documentation (vendor notifications, deviation reports, technical data)

**Timeline:** Complete within 5 working days of identifying change need

---

#### 5.2.2 Step 2: Change Control Number Assignment and Initial Review

**Who:** Quality Assurance Manager

**Actions:**
1. **Assign Change Control Number (CCN):**
   - Format: CC-YYYY-### (sequential)
   - Example: CC-2026-015
   - Log in Change Control Register

2. **Initial Completeness Review:**
   - Verify all required fields completed
   - Check supporting documentation attached
   - Assess if change is truly required (vs. optional enhancement)

3. **Preliminary Classification:**
   - Assign preliminary risk level (Critical/Major/Minor)
   - Determine if full CCB review required or can be expedited
   - Identify SMEs needed for evaluation

4. **Route for Evaluation:**
   - **Critical/Major:** Schedule for CCB meeting
   - **Minor:** Route to Department Manager for approval
   - **Emergency:** Flag for expedited CCB review (within 48 hours)

**Timeline:** Complete within 2 working days of receipt

---

#### 5.2.3 Step 3: Technical Evaluation and Impact Assessment

**Who:** Change Initiator, SMEs, Department Managers

**Actions:**

**A. Detailed Impact Assessment (Complete Impact Assessment Form - Annex A03)**

**Product Quality Impact:**
- Effect on Critical Quality Attributes (CQAs)
- Impact on product specifications
- Effect on product safety, efficacy, or purity
- Shelf-life or stability implications

**Process Impact:**
- Changes to critical process parameters (CPPs)
- Effect on process capability or yield
- Impact on process control strategy
- Changes to in-process controls

**Equipment/Facility Impact:**
- Equipment qualification status
- Cleanroom or environmental classification
- Utility system effects (HVAC, water, power)
- Equipment train or flow considerations

**Materials Impact:**
- Raw material specifications
- Material compatibility
- Supplier qualification status
- Packaging materials or labels

**Documentation Impact:**
- SOPs requiring update
- Batch records and packaging instructions
- Specifications
- Validation protocols and reports
- Training materials

**Regulatory Impact:**
- Marketing Authorization variation required?
- Notification to regulatory authority required?
- GMP or GACP compliance implications
- Inspection readiness

**Validation/Qualification Impact:**
- Does change affect validated system or process?
- Is revalidation required?
- Can change be implemented under protocol deviation?
- New validation required?
- Concurrent or retrospective validation approach?

**Training Impact:**
- Personnel requiring training
- Training content and delivery method
- Training timeline

**Supply Chain Impact:**
- Effect on material availability
- Lead time changes
- Supplier qualifications
- Inventory management

**B. Risk Assessment (See Section 5.1.2)**
- Complete Risk Assessment Matrix
- Document potential failure modes
- Identify mitigation measures
- Assign residual risk level

**C. Technical Review by SMEs**
- Engineering review (for equipment/facility changes)
- QC review (for analytical or specification changes)
- Production review (for process changes)
- Validation review (for changes to validated systems)

**D. Cost-Benefit Analysis**
- Implementation costs (materials, labor, downtime)
- Validation/qualification costs
- Training costs
- Long-term benefits (efficiency, quality improvement, compliance)
- Return on investment timeline

**Timeline:** Complete within 10 working days of CCN assignment (Critical changes: 20 days if complex)

---

#### 5.2.4 Step 4: Change Control Board Review and Decision

**Who:** Change Control Board (QP, Quality Manager, QA Manager, relevant Dept Managers, SMEs)

**Meeting Preparation:**
- QA Manager distributes change package to CCB members 5 days before meeting
- Package includes: CCR form, impact assessment, risk assessment, supporting documentation

**CCB Meeting Agenda:**

1. **Review Change Request:**
   - Initiator presents rationale and technical details
   - SMEs present evaluation findings
   - Q&A and discussion

2. **Review Risk Assessment:**
   - Assess risk level and mitigation measures
   - Determine if risk is acceptable
   - Identify additional controls needed

3. **Review Impact Assessment:**
   - Discuss all impacts (quality, compliance, operational)
   - Verify completeness of impact analysis

4. **Determine Validation/Qualification Requirements:**
   - Revalidation needed?
   - New validation protocol required?
   - Installation/Operational/Performance Qualification (IQ/OQ/PQ)?
   - Concurrent vs. retrospective validation?

5. **Review Implementation Plan:**
   - Timeline feasibility
   - Resource availability
   - Critical path items
   - Dependencies

6. **Make Decision:**

**CCB Decision Options:**

**APPROVED:**
- Change approved as submitted
- Implementation may proceed per approved plan
- QP signature (for Critical changes)
- Assign implementation owner and timeline

**APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS:**
- Change approved with specific requirements or modifications
- Conditions clearly documented
- Revised plan required before implementation
- Re-review may be required if conditions significantly alter proposal

**DEFERRED:**
- Insufficient information to make decision
- Additional evaluation or data required
- Specify what additional information needed
- Reschedule for future CCB meeting

**REJECTED:**
- Change not approved
- Risk too high or not justified
- Alternative approaches recommended
- Documented justification for rejection

**CCB Meeting Documentation:**
- Minutes recorded by QA Manager
- Attendance, decisions, rationale documented
- Action items and owners assigned
- Minutes distributed within 3 working days

**Timeline:** CCB meeting within 15 working days of impact assessment completion (expedited: 5 days for urgent changes)

---

#### 5.2.5 Step 5: Implementation Planning (For Approved Changes)

**Who:** Change Owner (assigned by CCB), Implementation Team

**Actions:**

1. **Develop Detailed Implementation Plan:**

**Plan Components:**
- **Scope of Work:** Detailed tasks and activities
- **Resources Required:** Personnel, equipment, materials, budget
- **Timeline:** Gantt chart or timeline with milestones
- **Responsibilities:** RACI matrix (Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed)
- **Dependencies:** Prerequisite activities, external dependencies
- **Communication Plan:** Stakeholder notifications, updates
- **Risk Mitigation:** Controls to minimize implementation risks
- **Rollback Plan:** How to reverse change if implementation fails
- **Success Criteria:** How to verify successful implementation
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Criteria for sign-off and closure

2. **Prepare Validation/Qualification Documentation (if required):**
- Write validation protocol per VAL_08.01_v1 Validation Master Plan
- Define acceptance criteria
- Specify testing approach
- Obtain protocol approval before implementation

3. **Update Documentation:**
- Draft revised SOPs, batch records, specifications
- Prepare training materials
- Update equipment manuals or drawings
- Create as-built documentation (for facilities/equipment)

4. **Schedule Activities:**
- Coordinate with production schedule
- Minimize operational impact
- Plan for downtime if required
- Communicate schedule to stakeholders

5. **Obtain Final Approval of Implementation Plan:**
- QA Manager reviews plan
- Quality Manager approves plan
- QP approves for Critical changes

**Timeline:** Complete within 10 working days of CCB approval

---

#### 5.2.6 Step 6: Change Implementation

**Who:** Change Owner, Implementation Team

**Pre-Implementation Checklist:**
- [ ] Implementation plan approved
- [ ] All required materials and resources available
- [ ] Validation protocol approved (if applicable)
- [ ] Training completed (if required before implementation)
- [ ] Production schedule coordinated
- [ ] Stakeholders notified
- [ ] Rollback plan in place

**Implementation Activities:**

1. **Execute Implementation Plan:**
   - Follow approved plan step-by-step
   - Document all activities in real-time
   - Record any deviations from plan
   - Take photographs for as-built documentation (facilities/equipment)

2. **Conduct Installation/Operational Qualification (if required):**
   - Execute IQ protocol (verify installation per specifications)
   - Execute OQ protocol (verify equipment operates per specifications)
   - Document all test results
   - Address any failures or deviations

3. **Update Documentation:**
   - Finalize revised SOPs and submit for approval per QA_00.02_v1
   - Update batch records and specifications
   - Update equipment lists, facility drawings, etc.

4. **Conduct Training:**
   - Train all affected personnel on changes
   - Document training per HRM-02-001 Training Management SOP
   - Verify competency before personnel use new process/equipment

5. **Communicate Implementation:**
   - Notify all stakeholders of completion
   - Provide summary of what changed
   - Inform when new procedures take effect

**Implementation Documentation:**
- Implementation Log (activities, dates, personnel)
- Photos or videos (where applicable)
- As-built drawings or schematics
- Training records
- Validation/qualification reports
- Updated SOPs and documents

**Timeline:** Per approved implementation plan (typically 30-180 days depending on complexity)

---

#### 5.2.7 Step 7: Performance Verification and Effectiveness Review

**Who:** Change Owner, QA Manager, CCB

**Actions:**

**A. Performance Qualification (PQ) - For Equipment/Process Changes:**
- Execute PQ protocol under actual operating conditions
- Verify change achieves intended objectives
- Demonstrate process capability and reproducibility
- Minimum 3 consecutive successful batches (or per protocol)

**B. Effectiveness Review:**

**Timing:**
- **Immediate:** Within 30 days of implementation (initial check)
- **Follow-up:** 3-6 months post-implementation (sustained effectiveness)

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Did change achieve intended objective?
- Was implementation completed per plan?
- Any unexpected issues or side effects?
- Product quality maintained or improved?
- Process performance stable?
- No new deviations related to change?

**Data Review:**
- Batch records from first 3-10 batches post-change
- Deviation data
- In-process control data
- QC test results
- Process performance indicators (yield, cycle time, etc.)
- Customer complaints (if change affects finished product)

**C. Documentation:**
- Effectiveness Review Report
- Conclusion: Effective / Partially Effective / Not Effective
- Recommendations for further action (if needed)
- CAPA initiation if issues identified

**Approval:**
- QA Manager reviews effectiveness review
- Quality Manager approves
- CCB reviews at next meeting

**Timeline:**
- Immediate review: 30 days post-implementation
- Follow-up review: 3-6 months post-implementation

---

#### 5.2.8 Step 8: Change Control Closure

**Who:** QA Manager, Quality Manager, QP (for Critical changes)

**Closure Criteria (All must be satisfied):**
- [ ] Change fully implemented per approved plan
- [ ] All required validation/qualification complete and approved
- [ ] All documentation updated and approved
- [ ] All training completed and documented
- [ ] Effectiveness review complete and acceptable
- [ ] No open action items or CAPAs related to implementation
- [ ] No outstanding regulatory notifications (if applicable)

**Closure Process:**
1. **QA Manager Verifies Closure Criteria:**
   - Reviews change control file for completeness
   - Verifies all documentation in order

2. **Prepare Closure Summary:**
   - Original change description
   - Implementation summary
   - Validation/qualification results
   - Effectiveness review results
   - Lessons learned
   - Recommendations for future similar changes

3. **Obtain Closure Approvals:**
   - QA Manager signs closure
   - Quality Manager approves closure
   - QP approves closure (for Critical changes)

4. **Update Change Control Register:**
   - Status: CLOSED
   - Closure date
   - Actual implementation time vs. planned

5. **Archive Change Control File:**
   - Assemble complete change control package
   - File per QAS-03-001 Document Retention SOP
   - Retention period: 10 years minimum

**Timeline:** Close within 30 days of effectiveness review completion

### 5.3 Emergency Changes

#### 5.3.1 Definition

Emergency changes are unplanned changes required immediately to:
- Prevent or mitigate product quality or patient safety risk
- Respond to equipment failure affecting GMP operations
- Comply with urgent regulatory requirement
- Address critical supply disruption

#### 5.3.2 Emergency Change Procedure

**Immediate Actions (0-24 hours):**

1. **Notify Key Personnel:**
   - QA Manager (immediately)
   - Quality Manager (within 1 hour)
   - QP (within 2 hours)

2. **Document Emergency:**
   - What happened (incident description)
   - Why emergency action needed
   - What emergency action taken
   - Immediate risk assessment
   - Product/batch impact

3. **Implement Emergency Change:**
   - Take minimum action necessary to mitigate immediate risk
   - Document all actions in real-time
   - Quarantine affected product/batches if applicable

4. **Verbal Approval:**
   - Obtain verbal approval from QP and Quality Manager
   - Document who gave approval and when

**Follow-Up Actions (24-72 hours):**

1. **Initiate Formal Change Control:**
   - Complete Change Control Request form
   - Classify change and conduct risk assessment
   - Document retrospective justification for emergency action

2. **CCB Review (Expedited):**
   - Emergency CCB meeting within 72 hours
   - Review emergency action taken
   - Approve temporary change OR
   - Require permanent solution (follow standard change control for permanent fix)

3. **Document and Investigate:**
   - Deviation report if emergency resulted from incident
   - CAPA if systematic issue identified

**Maximum Duration of Emergency Change:**
- 90 days (must convert to permanent solution or revert to original state)

**Retrospective Validation:**
- If emergency change affects validated process, retrospective validation may be acceptable
- Requires QP approval and documented justification

### 5.4 Temporary Changes

#### 5.4.1 Definition

Temporary changes are planned changes implemented for a limited defined period while permanent solution is developed.

**Examples:**
- Alternative supplier while primary supplier is qualified
- Interim equipment while permanent equipment is being qualified
- Temporary facility arrangement during renovation

#### 5.4.2 Temporary Change Requirements

- **Maximum Duration:** 90 days (extension requires QP approval with justification)
- **Approval:** Same approval level as permanent change (Critical/Major/Minor)
- **Validation:** Required if change affects validated system
- **Documentation:** Temporary change clearly marked on all documentation
- **Expiry Management:** QA Manager tracks expiry dates, sends reminders 30 days before expiry
- **Product Labeling:** Batches produced under temporary change tracked separately

**At Expiry:**
- Temporary change MUST either:
  - Be converted to permanent change (requires full change control), OR
  - Be reverted to original state, OR
  - Be extended (requires QP approval with strong justification)

### 5.5 Change Control for Validated Systems

#### 5.5.1 Changes Requiring Revalidation

**Critical Parameters:** Changes to critical parameters outside validated ranges always require revalidation.

**Examples Requiring Revalidation:**
- HVAC system modification affecting cleanroom classification
- Process parameter changes outside validated ranges
- Equipment replacement with different technology
- Software upgrade for validated computerized system
- Sterilization process changes
- Analytical method changes affecting accuracy/precision

#### 5.5.2 Change Evaluation Process (Per ICH Q10)

**Step 1: Change Assessment**
- Is the change within the validated design space?
- Does change affect Critical Quality Attributes (CQAs)?
- Does change affect Critical Process Parameters (CPPs)?

**Step 2: Determine Validation Approach**

**If Change is WITHIN Design Space:**
- No revalidation required
- Continued process verification may be sufficient
- Document rationale in change control file

**If Change is OUTSIDE Design Space but within Equipment/Process Capabilities:**
- Protocol deviation OR
- Validation protocol amendment OR
- Limited revalidation (e.g., 1-3 batches vs. full 3-batch validation)

**If Change is OUTSIDE Design Space and Process Capabilities:**
- Full revalidation required
- New validation protocol
- Minimum 3 consecutive successful batches
- Full characterization and acceptance

**Step 3: Select Validation Strategy**

**Prospective Validation (Preferred):**
- Validate before implementation
- Proves capability before routine use
- Required for Critical changes

**Concurrent Validation:**
- Validation concurrent with routine production
- Acceptable for low-risk changes
- Requires QP approval

**Retrospective Validation:**
- Validation after implementation using historical data
- Only for emergency changes or low-risk changes
- Requires strong justification and QP approval

#### 5.5.3 Integration with Validation Master Plan

- All changes requiring validation documented in Validation Master Plan (VAL_08.01_v1)
- Validation schedule updated
- Resources allocated

### 5.6 Regulatory Notifications

#### 5.6.1 Changes Requiring Regulatory Notification

**Variations to Marketing Authorization (MA):**

**Type IA (Minor Changes):**
- Notification within 12 months
- Examples: Manufacturing site name/address change (cosmetic), editorial changes

**Type IB (Minor Changes requiring notification before implementation):**
- Notification 30 days before implementation (can proceed if no objection)
- Examples: New packaging site, minor analytical method change

**Type II (Major Changes):**
- Prior approval required (90 days review)
- Examples: Change in synthesis route, new manufacturing site

**GMP Certificate Holder Notifications:**
- Site Master File updates
- Significant facility or equipment changes
- Organizational changes (QP change)

#### 5.6.2 Notification Process

1. **QA Manager Identifies Regulatory Impact** during change evaluation
2. **Consult Regulatory Affairs/QP** on notification requirements
3. **Prepare Variation Application or Notification** per local regulations
4. **Submit to Regulatory Authority** with required documentation
5. **Track Approval Status** in Change Control Register
6. **Implement Change ONLY After Approval** (for Type II) or after notification period (for Type IB)

### 5.7 Post-Implementation Monitoring

#### 5.7.1 Ongoing Monitoring Requirements

**For all Critical and Major changes, monitor for minimum 6 months post-implementation:**

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**
- Batch release rate (% of batches released first time)
- Deviation frequency (related to changed process/equipment)
- OOS/OOE investigation rate
- Process capability indices (Cpk)
- Yield
- Cycle time
- Quality test results (stability, purity, potency)

**Trending and Review:**
- Monthly trending for first 3 months
- Quarterly review at 6 months
- Annual review inclusion in Product Quality Review (PQR)

**Escalation:**
- If negative trends observed, initiate CAPA investigation
- CCB may require additional controls or reversion to original state

---

### 5.8 Change Control Metrics and Reporting

#### 5.8.1 Monthly Metrics (QA Manager Reports)

- Number of change controls initiated (by category: Critical/Major/Minor)
- Number pending CCB review (aging)
- Number approved, rejected, deferred
- Average cycle time (initiation to closure)
- Number of emergency changes
- Number of temporary changes and expiry status
- Implementation delays and reasons

#### 5.8.2 Quarterly Review (Quality Manager)

- Trending of change control data
- Identification of repeat changes (same system/process)
- Effectiveness review outcomes
- Validation burden (number requiring validation)
- Regulatory notifications submitted
- Lessons learned and process improvements

#### 5.8.3 Annual Review (QP and CCB)

- Review of change control system effectiveness
- Changes to products, processes, facilities (comprehensive)
- Integration with CAPA system
- Recommendations for change control procedure improvements
- Presented at Management Review meeting

## APPENDIX: CHANGE CONTROL PROCESS FLOWCHART

```
START: Change Identified
  ↓
Complete Change Control Request Form (Annex A01)
  ↓
Submit to QA Manager
  ↓
QA Manager assigns CCN, logs in register
  ↓
Preliminary Classification:
  - Critical (Level 1)
  - Major (Level 2)
  - Minor (Level 3)
  ↓
Minor Change? → YES → Route to Dept Manager + QA Manager → Approved? → YES → Implement → Effectiveness Review → Close
  ↓ NO                                                          ↓ NO → Return to Initiator with feedback → END
Critical or Major Change
  ↓
Conduct Detailed Impact Assessment (Annex A03)
  ↓
Conduct Risk Assessment (Annex A02)
  ↓
Technical Evaluation by SMEs
  ↓
Schedule CCB Meeting
  ↓
CCB Reviews Change Request
  ↓
CCB Decision:
  - APPROVED → Proceed to Implementation Planning
  - APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS → Revise Plan → Re-review → Proceed
  - DEFERRED → Additional Info Required → Loop back to Evaluation
  - REJECTED → Document Rationale → END
  ↓ APPROVED
Develop Implementation Plan
  - Tasks, timeline, resources
  - Validation requirements
  - Documentation updates
  - Training plan
  ↓
QA Manager/Quality Manager/QP Approve Implementation Plan
  ↓
Implement Change per Approved Plan
  - Execute tasks
  - Conduct IQ/OQ (if applicable)
  - Update documentation
  - Conduct training
  ↓
Validation/Qualification (if required)
  - Execute PQ protocol
  - 3 consecutive successful batches (typical)
  - Generate validation report
  ↓
Effectiveness Review (30 days + 3-6 months)
  - Verify change achieved objective
  - Review process performance data
  - Check for unexpected issues
  ↓
Effectiveness Acceptable? → NO → Initiate CAPA → Corrective Actions → Re-evaluate
  ↓ YES
Change Control Closure
  - Verify all closure criteria met
  - QA Manager/Quality Manager/QP sign closure
  - Update register status: CLOSED
  - Archive change control file
  ↓
END
```
