SECTION 1: PURPOSE & SCOPE
================================================================================

### 1.1 PURPOSE

The purpose of this Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is to establish a systematic 
and controlled process for the release of batches of medical cannabis products (dried 
flowers, extracts, infusions, and derived preparations) for distribution to customers, 
healthcare institutions, and regulatory authorities. This procedure ensures that only 
batches meeting all quality, safety, efficacy, and regulatory requirements are released 
to the market, and that the Qualified Person (QP) fulfills their legal responsibility 
under EU GMP regulations to certify that each batch complies with the specifications 
and manufacturing authorization.

This SOP establishes:
  • The decision-making criteria for batch release
  • The role and responsibilities of QC, QA, and the Qualified Person
  • The documentation requirements for each batch release
  • The archival and traceability records
  • Emergency procedures for blocked or quarantined batches

Regulatory Alignment:
  ✓ EU GMP Annex 7 (Herbal Preparations) - Section 2 (Personnel), Section 6 (Facilities)
  ✓ EudraLex Volume 4 Chapter 3 (Batch Release) and Chapter 7 (Quality Overall Summary)
  ✓ EU Directive 2001/83/EC - Article 51 (Qualified Person certification)
  ✓ ICH Q10 (Pharmaceutical Quality System) - Release of Batches
  ✓ North Macedonia Medical Cannabis Act & Regulations on GMP Compliance

### 1.2 SCOPE

This SOP covers all activities related to the release and certification of batches:

**INCLUSIONS:**
  ✓ Batch receipt and verification of production documentation
  ✓ Quality control testing results review
  ✓ Stability data assessment
  ✓ Batch assessment checklist completion
  ✓ Batch quarantine hold/release decision
  ✓ QP review and release certification
  ✓ Batch release record archival
  ✓ Post-release monitoring and stability tracking
  ✓ Batch blocking procedures for non-conforming batches

**EXCLUSIONS:**
  ✗ Product development and stability protocols (see SOP QAS-00-001 Product Development)
  ✗ Manufacturing/production procedures (see SOP PRO-03-001 Production Control)
  ✗ Quality control testing methods (see SOP QC-01-002 Analytical Testing Methods)
  ✗ Deviation and change control management (see SOP QAS-00-005 Change Control)
  ✗ Nonconformity investigation (see SOP QAS-00-006 CAPA System)

### 1.3 APPLICABILITY

Personnel Required to Follow This Procedure:
  • Quality Control (QC) Manager and QC Analysts
  • Quality Assurance (QA) Manager
  • Qualified Person (QP) – legally responsible individual
  • QC Head / Department Manager
  • Batch/Production Owner (Cultivation or Manufacturing Team)
  • Facility Manager
  • Records/Documentation personnel

SECTION 2: DEFINITIONS & ABBREVIATIONS
================================================================================

### 2.1 KEY DEFINITIONS

**Batch (Lot)**
A specific quantity of a product manufactured in a single production cycle and 
intended to have uniform character and quality within specified limits.

**Batch Release**
The formal decision and documentation by the Quality Control function to approve 
a batch for distribution to customers, following successful completion of all 
in-process and finished product quality tests, stability assessments, and 
regulatory compliance checks.

**Qualified Person (QP)**
A natural person who meets the qualifications specified in EU GMP regulations 
(Article 51 of Directive 2001/83/EC) and is responsible for certifying that each 
batch has been manufactured in accordance with the specifications and authorization, 
and that it is safe and suitable for human use.

**Batch Assessment Checklist**
A comprehensive form documenting all quality, testing, stability, and compliance 
data required for batch release decision-making; serves as the primary tool for 
QC and QA review before QP certification.

**Out-of-Specification (OOS)**
A test result that falls outside the acceptable limits defined in the product 
specification; requires investigation and determination of batch acceptability.

**Stability Data**
Temperature and humidity-controlled storage test results demonstrating product 
integrity, potency retention, and safety over the intended shelf life period.

**Quarantine Hold**
A temporary status applied to a batch during production and testing, preventing 
release until all results are reviewed and approval is granted.

**Batch Blocking**
A formal action to prevent release and distribution of a batch due to detected 
non-conformities, OOS results, or regulatory violations; includes investigation, 
customer notification, and corrective action.

**Amendment History Record**
Documentation of all changes to batch records, including reason, authorization, 
and approval signatures.

SECTION 3: RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

### 3.1 BATCH OWNER / PRODUCTION TEAM

**Responsibilities:**
  • Submit batch documentation package to QC within 24 hours of production completion
  • Provide accurate batch number, product identity, quantity produced, and production dates
  • Ensure all in-process testing data are included and verified
  • Respond to QC/QA requests for additional information or documentation
  • Maintain batch records in traceable format (electronic and/or physical)

**Authority:**
  • Does NOT approve batch release
  • May present operational or production context during QC review
  • May request expedited review only with QC Head approval

---

### 3.2 QUALITY CONTROL (QC) MANAGER & ANALYSTS

**Responsibilities:**
  • Receive batch documentation package upon production completion
  • Initiate Batch Release Assessment Form (Annex A01)
  • Conduct comprehensive review of all QC testing results (potency, microbiology, 
    heavy metals, pesticides, moisture, foreign material, etc.)
  • Verify that all test results meet product specification limits
  • Investigate any out-of-specification (OOS) or suspect results; document findings
  • Assess batch stability data against shelf-life requirements
  • Verify proper labeling, packaging, and sample retention requirements are met
  • Complete Batch Release Checklist (Annex A02) documenting all checks performed
  • Provide preliminary release recommendation to QA Manager
  • Document any deviations, discrepancies, or holds in batch record
  • Maintain confidentiality and traceability of all batch records

**Authority:**
  • May place batch on quarantine hold for further investigation
  • May recommend batch blocking if critical defects are identified
  • Does NOT approve final batch release (that is QP responsibility)
  • May escalate complex decisions to QA Manager or QP immediately

---

### 3.3 QUALITY ASSURANCE (QA) MANAGER

**Responsibilities:**
  • Receive QC preliminary recommendation and supporting documentation
  • Conduct independent secondary review of all testing, stability, and compliance data
  • Verify regulatory compliance (labeling, claims, advertising, traceability)
  • Review batch record for completeness, accuracy, and signature authority
  • Assess any deviations or investigations documented during QC review
  • Approve or reject QC preliminary recommendation (with written justification if different)
  • Prepare QP Release Certification Form (Annex A03) for QP signature
  • Ensure batch release documentation is complete before QP review
  • Provide guidance on regulatory requirements and batch hold/release decisions
  • Document QA approval in batch record with date and signature

**Authority:**
  • May reject QC preliminary recommendation and request additional investigation
  • May place batch on hold pending additional stability data or regulatory review
  • Does NOT approve final batch release (that is QP responsibility)
  • Acts as acting department manager for QC decisions if QC Head is absent

---

### 3.4 QUALIFIED PERSON (QP)

**Responsibilities & Legal Authority (EU GMP Annex 1, Article 51 Directive 2001/83/EC):**
  • Conduct final comprehensive review of batch documentation package, including:
    - QC test results and method validations
    - Stability data supporting shelf-life claims
    - Batch record completeness and regulatory compliance
    - Manufacturing and packaging records from production team
    - Any deviations, investigations, or corrective actions
  • Certify in writing that each batch meets all specifications and is safe for distribution
  • Sign and date the Batch Release Certification Form (Annex A03) as legal evidence of 
    accountability
  • Determine batch acceptability independent of other personnel recommendations
  • Request additional data or investigations if batch status is unclear
  • Document any concerns or conditional approvals in the batch record
  • Maintain records of all batch certifications for audit trail

**Authority:**
  • SOLE authority to approve final batch release and authorize distribution
  • May reject batch release and require corrective action or batch blocking
  • May impose conditional release requirements (e.g., additional testing, stability 
    monitoring, customer notification)
  • May delegate review authority ONLY to another appropriately qualified Responsible Person 
    (not to QC or QA personnel without QP qualifications)

---

### 3.5 FACILITY MANAGER

**Responsibilities:**
  • Acknowledge receipt of batch release documentation and approval
  • Authorize release to warehouse/distribution based on QP certification
  • Ensure released batches are transferred to appropriate storage location
  • Notify Regulatory Affairs of released batches requiring regulatory notification
  • Oversee post-release stability monitoring and shelf-life tracking
  • Approve batch blocking decisions and ensure customer/regulatory notification

**Authority:**
  • Does NOT approve batch content or quality (QC/QA responsibility)
  • May authorize distribution logistics only after QP release certification

### 4.1 BATCH RELEASE PROCESS OVERVIEW

The batch release process consists of six sequential phases to ensure comprehensive 
quality assessment and regulatory compliance before the Qualified Person issues 
final certification:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PHASE 1: BATCH DOCUMENTATION RECEIPT & INITIAL VERIFICATION     │
│ (QC Team, 24 hours post-production)                              │
└──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                       ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PHASE 2: QUALITY CONTROL TESTING REVIEW & ASSESSMENT            │
│ (QC Manager/Analysts, 2-3 working days)                          │
└──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                       ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PHASE 3: STABILITY DATA & SHELF-LIFE VERIFICATION              │
│ (QC Lead, concurrent with Phase 2)                              │
└──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                       ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PHASE 4: QUALITY ASSURANCE SECONDARY REVIEW & APPROVAL          │
│ (QA Manager, 1-2 working days after QC recommendation)          │
└──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                       ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PHASE 5: QUALIFIED PERSON FINAL REVIEW & CERTIFICATION          │

└──────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                       ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PHASE 6: BATCH RELEASE AUTHORIZATION & ARCHIVAL                │
```

### 4.2 PHASE 1: BATCH DOCUMENTATION RECEIPT & INITIAL VERIFICATION

**Timing:** Within 24 hours of batch completion of production

**Procedure:**

1. **Batch Package Submission**
   - Batch Owner submits complete batch documentation package to QC Manager
   - Package includes: batch number, product identity, quantity produced (kg/units), 
     production dates, storage conditions, and all in-process test results
   - QC Manager verifies package completeness using Batch Submission Checklist (internal document)
   - If documentation is incomplete, QC Manager notifies Batch Owner and requests 
     missing data with a deadline

2. **Batch Quarantine Assignment**
   - Upon receipt of complete package, batch is assigned "Quarantine Hold" status
   - Batch number is recorded in Quality Control Log (reference: SOP-Logbooks)
   - Physical batch samples are transferred to QC Sample Storage under quarantine label
   - Batch is prevented from distribution pending QC approval

3. **Preliminary Batch Assessment**
   - QC Manager initiates Batch Release Assessment Form (Annex A01)
   - Batch identity, quantities, production dates, and storage conditions are recorded
   - Review timeline is assigned: target 2–3 working days for QC assessment

---

### 4.3 PHASE 2: QUALITY CONTROL TESTING REVIEW & ASSESSMENT

**Timing:** 2–3 working days after batch documentation receipt

**Procedure:**

1. **Testing Results Review**
   - QC Analyst reviews all analytical test results (CoA and HPLC/GC-MS reports)
   - Each test result is verified for:
     - Analytical method validity (proper validation, qualification)
     - Specification limits compliance (all results within spec)
     - Reference standards integrity (proper storage, expiration)
     - Test documentation completeness (dates, analyst signatures, instrument logs)
   - If any test is OOS (out-of-specification):
     - Investigation is initiated and documented
     - Root cause analysis determines batch acceptability
     - See Section 4.9 for OOS/Deviation Handling

2. **Microbiology & Safety Assessment**
   - Pathogenic bacteria, mold, and contaminant test results are reviewed
   - Results are verified against specification limits (THC <0.2% for food products, 
     as per North Macedonia regulations)
   - Microbial contamination trends are assessed for process control
   - If results indicate contamination, corrective action is recommended

3. **Potency & Identity Testing**
   - THC/CBD content verified against specification labels and regulatory claims
   - Test methods (HPLC) are confirmed to be validated and properly maintained
   - Identity testing (visual, chromatographic) confirmed
   - Deviation from label claims triggers investigation and potential batch blocking

4. **Completion of Batch Release Checklist**
   - QC Analyst completes Batch Release Checklist (Annex A02) documenting:
     - Each test performed (name, result, specification limit, pass/fail status)
     - Equipment used and calibration status
     - Any deviations, OOS results, or investigations initiated
     - Analyst signature and date
   - All findings are documented in the batch record

5. **Preliminary QC Recommendation**
   - QC Manager prepares written preliminary recommendation:
     - **APPROVE FOR RELEASE:** Batch meets all specifications, no deviations, 
       ready for QA review
     - **APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS:** Minor deviations resolved, stability pending, 
       or post-release monitoring recommended
     - **HOLD FOR INVESTIGATION:** OOS result, deviation, or incomplete testing; 
       investigation timeline provided
     - **RECOMMEND BLOCKING:** Critical defect identified; batch cannot be released
   - Recommendation is documented in Batch Release Assessment Form (Annex A01)
   - QC Manager signs and dates the recommendation

---

### 4.4 PHASE 3: STABILITY DATA & SHELF-LIFE VERIFICATION

**Timing:** Concurrent with Phase 2 (2–3 working days)

**Procedure:**

1. **Stability Study Review**
   - QC Lead retrieves stability study data from Stability Database (reference: SOP-Logbooks)
   - Batch-specific stability results are located (matching batch number, storage condition)
   - Stability data must support the shelf-life claim on the product label
   - For new batches without historical stability: provisional shelf-life may be assigned 
     with commitment to ongoing monitoring (post-release stability tracking)

2. **Shelf-Life Assignment**
   - Stability results are assessed to determine maximum acceptable storage duration
   - Shelf-life is assigned conservatively based on passing quality attributes:
     - Potency retention (minimum 90% of initial THC/CBD at end of shelf-life)
     - Microbial stability (no growth, no exceeding limits)
     - Physical integrity (no moisture change >2%, no discoloration)
   - Assigned shelf-life is documented in Batch Release Assessment Form (Section: Shelf-Life)

3. **Storage Condition Verification**
   - Storage condition recommendation is documented (e.g., 15–25°C, <60% RH)
   - Batch samples are confirmed to be stored under specified conditions during testing
   - Deviation from storage conditions is documented and impact on shelf-life assessed
   - If storage deviation occurred, shorter shelf-life may be assigned or investigation triggered

4. **Stability Data Deficiency Management**
   - If insufficient stability data exists, batch may be released with:
     - Reduced shelf-life (e.g., 6 months vs. 12 months claim)
     - Post-release stability monitoring requirement
     - Customer communication on temporary shelf-life pending confirmation
   - Extended shelf-life claims require FDA/regulatory approval before implementation

---

### 4.5 PHASE 4: QUALITY ASSURANCE SECONDARY REVIEW & APPROVAL

**Timing:** 1–2 working days after QC preliminary recommendation

**Procedure:**

1. **Independent QA Review**
   - QA Manager receives complete batch package and QC preliminary recommendation
   - QA Manager conducts **independent** secondary review (not merely confirming QC decision)
   - QA review includes:
     - Verification of all QC testing and analytical data
     - Confirmation of stability data supporting shelf-life claim
     - Verification of batch record completeness (all signatures, dates, amendments)
     - Assessment of any deviations or investigations
     - Regulatory compliance verification (labeling claims, regulatory notifications)

2. **Regulatory Compliance Assessment**
   - Batch labeling is verified against approved specifications
   - THC/CBD content and health claims are reviewed for regulatory compliance with:
     - North Macedonia Medical Cannabis Act
     - EU advertising and labeling regulations
     - Customer contract specifications (if healthcare institution buyer)
   - Any discrepancy triggers investigation or batch blocking

3. **Deviation & Corrective Action Review**
   - All documented deviations during manufacturing are reviewed
   - Corrective actions are assessed for effectiveness
   - Impact on batch quality and releasability is determined
   - If corrective action is incomplete, batch is held pending resolution

4. **QA Approval Decision**
   - QA Manager provides written approval decision:
     - **APPROVE:** All findings support QC recommendation; ready for QP review
     - **APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS:** Additional documentation or post-release monitoring required
     - **REJECT:** QA assessment contradicts QC recommendation; investigation required
   - Decision is documented in Batch Release Assessment Form (Annex A01)
   - QA Manager signs and dates the approval

5. **Preparation for QP Review**
   - QA Manager compiles comprehensive QP Release Certification Form (Annex A03)
   - All supporting documentation is organized in batch file (physical or electronic)
   - File is marked "READY FOR QP REVIEW" and transmitted to QP with target timeline

---

### 4.6 PHASE 5: QUALIFIED PERSON FINAL REVIEW & CERTIFICATION

**Timing:** 1 working day after QA approval (EU GMP standard)

**Procedure:**

1. **QP Comprehensive Review**
   - Qualified Person receives batch package with complete documentation
   - QP conducts **final independent review** of:
     - Manufacturing and packaging record (completeness, compliance with authorization)
     - QC and QA assessments (findings, recommendations, supporting data)
     - Stability data supporting shelf-life claims
     - Batch record amendments and investigation outcomes
     - Regulatory compliance and labeling accuracy
   - QP is NOT bound by QC/QA recommendations; QP makes independent determination of 
     batch acceptability

2. **QP Decision Authority**
   - QP determines one of four outcomes:

   **OUTCOME A: APPROVE & RELEASE**
     - Batch meets all specifications and quality standards
     - Manufacturing process complies with authorization
     - All testing and stability data are acceptable
     - Batch is suitable for distribution and use

   **OUTCOME B: APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS**
     - Batch is releasable with specific requirements, such as:
       - Post-release stability monitoring (ongoing testing with timeline)
       - Reduced shelf-life pending confirmation of stability claim
       - Customer notification regarding specific quality parameter
       - Additional labeling or documentation requirement

   **OUTCOME C: CONDITIONAL HOLD – REQUEST ADDITIONAL DATA**
     - Batch releasability is unclear pending additional information
     - Specific data request is documented with timeline (e.g., extended stability study, 
       additional microbiology testing, root cause analysis of deviation)
     - Batch remains in quarantine until data is submitted and reviewed

   **OUTCOME D: REJECT & RECOMMEND BLOCKING**
     - Critical defect identified (OOS result, specification deviation, manufacturing 
       deviation, stability failure)
     - Batch cannot be released; blocking procedure is initiated (see Section 4.8)
     - Written justification is provided to Facility Manager

3. **QP Release Certification Form**
   - QP completes and signs Batch Release Certification Form (Annex A03)
   - Form documents:
     - Batch number, product identity, quantity, production date
     - QP assessment summary (what was reviewed, any concerns)
     - QP decision (one of four outcomes above)
     - QP signature and date (legal certification of accountability)
   - Signature is original (not electronic) or digitally authenticated per facility IT policy

4. **QP Signature Authority & Legal Standing**
   - QP signature on Batch Release Certification Form represents legal certification that:
     - Batch has been manufactured in accordance with authorization and GMP regulations
     - Batch meets quality specifications and is safe for distribution
     - QP has personally reviewed all critical data and quality parameters
     - QP takes legal responsibility for batch safety and efficacy
   - QP signature creates audit trail for regulatory inspection and product liability

---

### 4.7 PHASE 6: BATCH RELEASE AUTHORIZATION & ARCHIVAL

**Timing:** Within 24 hours of QP certification (Outcome A or B)

**Procedure:**

1. **Facility Manager Authorization**
   - Facility Manager receives QP Release Certification Form with QP signature
   - Facility Manager verifies QP decision is APPROVE (Outcome A or B)
   - Facility Manager authorizes transfer of batch from quarantine to released inventory
   - Authorization is documented in Batch Release Log (reference: SOP-Logbooks)
   - Facility Manager signature is affixed to batch release authorization

2. **Batch Status Update**
   - Batch status is updated from "Quarantine Hold" to "Released"
   - Batch is labeled with Release Label (barcode, batch number, shelf-life, storage condition)
   - Physical samples are transferred from QC quarantine storage to Archive Storage
   - Electronic batch record status is updated in Quality Management System (QMS)

3. **Distribution Authorization**
   - Released batch is transferred to Finished Product Warehouse
   - Warehouse Manager receives batch with complete documentation (CoA, Batch Record summary)
   - Warehouse barcode system is updated to allow batch release for order fulfillment
   - Batch is made available for customer distribution

4. **Batch Record Archival**
   - Complete batch record (original or electronic copy) is archived in Records Storage
   - Batch Release Archival Checklist (Annex A06) is completed, documenting:
     - All supporting documents filed (testing, stability, deviations, investigations)
     - Document completeness verification
     - Archival location and format (physical folder, electronic folder, microfilm)
     - Archival date and responsible person signature
   - Batch file is organized for ease of retrieval during audits or investigations

5. **Post-Release Monitoring**
   - If Outcome B includes post-release stability monitoring, monitoring schedule is 
     initiated in Stability Database
   - Monitoring timeline (e.g., 6 months post-release, 12 months post-release) is set
   - Results will be reviewed against stability criteria; findings are documented
   - Facility Manager is notified if any post-release monitoring results fail specification

6. **Regulatory Notification**
   - If batch is released to healthcare institution or regulated customer, Regulatory 
     Affairs team is notified
   - Batch information (number, product, quantity, shelf-life, lot number) is transmitted 
     per regulatory requirements
   - Customer CoA (Certificate of Analysis) is prepared and distributed

---

### 4.8 BATCH BLOCKING & QUARANTINE PROCEDURES

**Applicable When:** QP Outcome D (Reject & Recommend Blocking) or QC holds batch 
for critical defect

**Procedure:**

1. **Blocking Decision Documentation**
   - Blocking decision is documented in writing by QP or QC manager
   - Reason for blocking is clearly stated (e.g., "OOS microbiology result," 
     "Manufacturing deviation," "Stability failure")
   - Impact assessment is provided: potential safety risk, effectiveness concern, 
     or regulatory non-compliance

2. **Internal Notification**
   - Facility Manager is notified of batch blocking decision within 24 hours
   - Batch Owner is notified and requested not to use or distribute batch
   - Warehouse Manager is notified; batch is physically separated and labeled "BLOCKED"
   - Records Team initiates blocking documentation in Batch Blocking Log 
     (reference: SOP-Logbooks)

3. **Investigation Initiation**
   - Quality Assurance Manager initiates deviation investigation per SOP QAS-00-005 
     (CAPA System)
   - Investigation determines root cause and assesses impact:
     - Is batch safety compromised? Is customer notification required?
     - Are other batches potentially affected?
     - What corrective action is needed?
   - Investigation results are documented and corrective action plan is approved

4. **Customer Notification (if applicable)**
   - If blocked batch was distributed to customers, Regulatory Affairs and Facility 
     Manager determine if customer notification is required
   - Customer notification includes:
     - Batch number(s) affected
     - Reason for blocking (safety, efficacy, regulatory non-compliance)
     - Recommended action (return batch, do not use, hold for further instruction)
     - Company contact for questions or adverse event reporting

5. **Batch Disposition**
   - After investigation and corrective action, batch disposition is determined:
     - **RELEASE AFTER CORRECTION:** Additional testing or re-processing; re-submit 
       for QP review
     - **REWORK:** Return batch to production for blending or re-processing; 
       treated as new batch post-rework
     - **DESTRUCTION:** Batch is destroyed; waste disposal documented per 
       environmental regulations
   - Disposition decision is approved by QP and Facility Manager

6. **Regulatory Reporting**
   - Batch blocking and resolution is documented for regulatory reporting
   - Quality Overall Summary (PMCF - Post-Market Compliance File) is updated
   - Any deviation requiring regulatory notification (serious adverse events, 
     safety issues) is reported per legal timeline

---

### 4.9 OUT-OF-SPECIFICATION (OOS) RESULTS & INVESTIGATION

**When OOS Result is Discovered:**

1. **Immediate Actions**
   - QC Analyst places batch on immediate quarantine hold
   - OOS result is documented with test method, result value, specification limit, date, 
     and analyst signature
   - QC Manager is notified immediately (same working day)
   - Batch Owner is notified; batch is prevented from further processing or distribution

2. **Investigation Initiation**
   - QC Manager documents the OOS result in the Batch Record
   - If OOS involves a potential safety concern (microbiology, heavy metals, potency 
     verification failure):
     - QA Manager is notified immediately
     - Investigation is escalated to deviation protocol
     - Other batches with same ingredients or process are flagged for review
   
3. **Analytical Investigation Steps**
   - **Re-analysis verification:** Aliquot is retested by independent analyst using 
     same method (if sufficient sample exists)
   - **Method validation review:** Analytical method is verified to be properly validated 
     and in-control (check reference standards, instrument calibration, environment)
   - **Instrument investigation:** Instrument that generated OOS result is checked for 
     malfunction, calibration drift, or maintenance issues
   - **Root cause analysis:** Root cause of OOS is determined (sample degradation, 
     analytical error, true product variation)

4. **Batch Acceptability Determination**
   - If re-analysis or investigation confirms OOS is a true product defect:
     - Batch is recommended for blocking; batch owner is notified
     - Deviation investigation is initiated per SOP QAS-00-005
     - Customer notification is assessed if batch was distributed
   
   - If investigation determines OOS was analytical error:
     - Result is invalidated; original specification result stands
     - Corrective action is documented (re-training, instrument repair, etc.)
     - Batch proceeds to QA/QP review with investigation documentation attached

5. **Documentation in Batch Record**
   - Complete OOS investigation (including re-analysis results, root cause, corrective 
     action) is filed in batch record
   - Investigation conclusion and batch disposition (RELEASE or BLOCK) is clearly 
     documented
   - All signatures and dates are affixed by QC manager, QA manager, and (if blocking 
     is recommended) QP

### 5.2 RETENTION & ARCHIVAL

**Retention Period:** Minimum **10 years** after batch expiration date

**Rationale:**
  - EU GMP Annex 7 requires batch records be retained for minimum duration of 
    product shelf-life + 1 year minimum
  - North Macedonia medical cannabis regulations align with EU GMP standard
  - Product liability and adverse event traceability requires long-term availability

**Archival Locations:**
  - **Physical Records:** Climate-controlled records room (15–25°C, <60% RH)
  - **Electronic Records:** Secure QMS database with electronic signature integrity 
    protection
  - **Sample Retention:** Per SOP-Logbooks; samples archived for future testing if 
    adverse events arise

**Access Control:**
  - Only QC, QA, Facility Manager, and QP have authorized access
  - Audit trail logs all access to batch records
  - Physical records room has entry control (key card, visitor log)

---

### 5.3 BATCH RECORD AMENDMENTS

If corrections or amendments are needed to batch record after release:

1. **Amendment Authorization**
   - Amendment must be authorized by QA Manager or QP
   - Reason for amendment is clearly documented (e.g., "Corrected calculation error," 
     "Additional stability data added")
   - Amendment is NOT permitted to change release decision retroactively unless 
     investigation determines release was inappropriate

2. **Amendment Documentation**
   - Original entry is retained (not erased or deleted)
   - Amendment is recorded with date, reason, and authorizing signature
   - Amendment is traceable to decision-maker
   - Electronic systems retain audit trail showing original and amended values

3. **Post-Release Amendments**
   - Amendments made after QP release certification require special approval:
     - Minor correction (e.g., typo, clerical error) = QA Manager approval
     - Significant amendment (e.g., additional stability data, revised shelf-life) = 
       QP review and re-certification
     - Safety-related amendment (e.g., unexpected OOS discovered post-release) = 
       Immediate QA/QP review; customer notification assessed

SECTION 7: EMERGENCY & EXCEPTIONAL PROCEDURES
================================================================================

### 7.1 EXPEDITED BATCH RELEASE

**When Permitted:**
  - Healthcare institution requires immediate supply for medical use
  - Supply chain emergency or shortage
  - Regulatory exemption approved by authorities

**Process:**
  1. QC Manager and QA Manager jointly document reason for expedited release
  2. Batch must still meet ALL specification limits; no testing can be waived
  3. If testing is incomplete (e.g., 6-month stability data pending), conditional 
     release with post-release monitoring is approved by QP only
  4. Facility Manager and Regulatory Affairs are notified
  5. Customer is informed of any conditional status (if applicable)
  6. Expedited release is documented in batch record with approvals

---

### 7.2 BATCH RECALL POST-RELEASE

If a batch released to customers is later determined to be unsafe or non-compliant:

1. **Recall Trigger:** Adverse event report, regulatory non-compliance discovery, 
   or quality investigation revealing released batch defect

2. **Immediate Actions:**
   - Facility Manager and Regulatory Affairs are notified immediately
   - Legal determination of recall scope (national, regional, full)
   - Customer notification is prepared with batch identification and return/destruction 
     instructions

3. **Documentation:**
   - Batch record is amended with recall decision and supporting investigation
   - Batch is flagged in QMS as "RECALLED"
   - Corrective action to prevent future occurrences is implemented and verified
