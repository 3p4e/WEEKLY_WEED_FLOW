## PURPOSE

This annex provides standardized risk assessment tools and templates for evaluating proposed changes to ensure risks to product quality, patient safety, and GMP compliance are identified, assessed, and mitigated.

---

## 1. RISK ASSESSMENT OVERVIEW

### 1.1 When to Perform Risk Assessment

**ALL Critical (Level 1) changes:** Mandatory comprehensive risk assessment

**ALL Major (Level 2) changes:** Mandatory standard risk assessment

**Minor (Level 3) changes:** Risk assessment optional but recommended

### 1.2 Risk Assessment Principles (ICH Q9 Quality Risk Management)

**Risk** = **Severity** × **Probability**

**Risk Assessment Process:**
1. **Risk Identification:** What could go wrong?
2. **Risk Analysis:** How likely? How severe?
3. **Risk Evaluation:** Is the risk acceptable?
4. **Risk Control:** How to reduce or eliminate risk?
5. **Risk Communication:** Document and communicate findings
6. **Risk Review:** Monitor effectiveness of controls

## 2. RISK ASSESSMENT MATRIX (Primary Tool)

### 2.1 Risk Matrix Table

| **Severity →** | **Negligible (1)** | **Minor (2)** | **Moderate (3)** | **Major (4)** | **Catastrophic (5)** |
|----------------|--------------------|---------------|------------------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Probability ↓** |  |  |  |  |  |
| **Almost Certain (5)** | **Medium (5)** | **High (10)** | **High (15)** | **Critical (20)** | **Critical (25)** |
| **Likely (4)** | **Low (4)** | **Medium (8)** | **High (12)** | **High (16)** | **Critical (20)** |
| **Possible (3)** | **Low (3)** | **Medium (6)** | **Medium (9)** | **High (12)** | **High (15)** |
| **Unlikely (2)** | **Low (2)** | **Low (4)** | **Medium (6)** | **Medium (8)** | **High (10)** |
| **Rare (1)** | **Low (1)** | **Low (2)** | **Low (3)** | **Medium (4)** | **Medium (5)** |

### 2.2 Severity Scoring Criteria

| Score | Severity Level | Product Quality Impact | Patient Safety Impact | GMP Compliance Impact |
|-------|----------------|------------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **5** | **Catastrophic** | Critical Quality Attribute severely compromised | Life-threatening or permanent harm | Major GMP violation, regulatory action likely |
| **4** | **Major** | CQA significantly affected, product may not meet specification | Serious adverse effect requiring medical intervention | Significant GMP non-compliance |
| **3** | **Moderate** | Quality attribute affected but within specification limits | Moderate adverse effect, no medical intervention | Moderate GMP gap, correctable |
| **2** | **Minor** | Minor quality variation, no spec impact | Mild transient adverse effect | Minor GMP observation |
| **1** | **Negligible** | No measurable quality impact | No patient safety impact | No GMP impact |

### 2.3 Probability Scoring Criteria

| Score | Probability Level | Description | Approximate Frequency |
|-------|-------------------|-------------|----------------------|
| **5** | **Almost Certain** | Expected to occur in most circumstances | > 90% chance, > 1 per month |
| **4** | **Likely** | Will probably occur in most circumstances | 60-90% chance, 1-4 per quarter |
| **3** | **Possible** | Might occur at some time | 30-60% chance, 1-4 per year |
| **2** | **Unlikely** | Could occur but not expected | 10-30% chance, < 1 per year |
| **1** | **Rare** | May occur only in exceptional circumstances | < 10% chance, < 1 in 3 years |

### 2.4 Risk Level Interpretation and Action Required

| Risk Score | Risk Level | Color Code | Action Required |
|------------|------------|------------|-----------------|
| **20-25** | **CRITICAL** | 🔴 RED | **UNACCEPTABLE** - Change should not proceed unless significant risk mitigation implemented. QP approval mandatory. |
| **12-19** | **HIGH** | 🟠 ORANGE | **EXTENSIVE CONTROLS REQUIRED** - Detailed risk mitigation plan, validation/qualification, enhanced monitoring. QP review required. |
| **6-11** | **MEDIUM** | 🟡 YELLOW | **STANDARD CONTROLS REQUIRED** - Appropriate change control procedures, validation if applicable, routine monitoring. |
| **1-5** | **LOW** | 🟢 GREEN | **ACCEPTABLE** - Standard change control, routine documentation and verification sufficient. |

**FMEA Scoring:**

**Severity (S):** 1 = No effect, 10 = Catastrophic

**Probability (P):** 1 = Rare, 10 = Almost certain

**Detection (D):** 1 = Almost certain to detect, 10 = Undetectable

**RPN (Risk Priority Number):** S × P × D (Range: 1-1000)

**Action Thresholds:**
- **RPN > 200:** Immediate action required (Critical risk)
- **RPN 100-200:** Action required (High risk)
- **RPN 50-100:** Action recommended (Medium risk)
- **RPN < 50:** Monitor (Low risk)

## 9. RISK ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

### Risk Acceptance Decision Matrix

| Change Category | Maximum Acceptable Risk Level | Approval Authority |
|----------------|------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Critical Changes** | Medium (after mitigation) | QP Final Approval |
| **Major Changes** | Medium (after mitigation) | Quality Manager / CCB |
| **Minor Changes** | Low (after mitigation) | Department Manager |

**Risk Mitigation Requirements:**
- **Critical Risks (RPN 20-25):** Mandatory mitigation to reduce to High or lower
- **High Risks (RPN 12-19):** Mitigation required, residual risk Medium acceptable
- **Medium Risks (RPN 6-11):** Standard controls, residual risk Low-Medium acceptable
- **Low Risks (RPN 1-5):** Routine controls adequate

**Unacceptable Scenarios:**
- Any Critical risk that cannot be mitigated to High or lower → Change REJECTED
- Multiple High risks without adequate mitigation → Change DEFERRED
- Patient safety Critical risk with any probability > Rare → IMMEDIATE ESCALATION
