# ANNEX A03: EFFECTIVENESS REVIEW PROCEDURES

**Parent SOP:** QA_00.06_A03_v1 Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) System
**Annex Code:** QA_00.06_A03_v1
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-22

---

## PURPOSE

This annex provides comprehensive procedures and forms for conducting 30-day and 6-month effectiveness reviews of Corrective and Preventive Actions. The effectiveness review verifies that implemented actions achieved their intended purpose and that root causes have been eliminated, not just symptoms treated.

---

# EFFECTIVENESS REVIEW PROCEDURES AND FORMS

---

## SECTION 1: EFFECTIVENESS REVIEW OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Purpose of Effectiveness Review

The Effectiveness Review answers critical questions:

✓ **Was the action implemented as planned?** (Implementation Verification)
✓ **Is the problem resolved?** (Effectiveness Verification)
✓ **Has root cause been eliminated?** (Root Cause Elimination Verification)
✓ **Is the improvement sustained?** (Sustained Effectiveness)
✓ **Can the CAPA be closed?** (Closure Decision)

### 1.2 Review Timing

**Corrective Actions (Addressing Identified Problems):**

| Review Type | Timing | Purpose | Status Determination |
|---|---|---|---|
| **30-Day Effectiveness Check** | 30 days after action completion | Verify implementation, check for immediate recurrence | Implementation Verified or Implementation Issues |
| **6-Month Sustained Effectiveness Review** | 6 months after action completion | Verify problem has not recurred, confirm sustained improvement | Effectiveness Confirmed or Effectiveness Not Confirmed |
| **Closure Decision** | After 6-month review | Determine if CAPA can be closed or requires extension | CAPA Closed or CAPA Reopened |

**Preventive Actions (Addressing Potential Problems):**

| Review Type | Timing | Purpose | Status Determination |
|---|---|---|---|
| **30-Day Effectiveness Check** | 30 days after action completion | Verify action implemented correctly, system functioning as designed | Implementation Verified or Implementation Issues |
| **Effectiveness Validation** | When risk condition occurs | Verify preventive action is effective under actual risk exposure | Effectiveness Confirmed or Not Confirmed |
| **Closure Decision** | After validation or specified period | Determine if preventive measure is effective and sustainable | CAPA Closed or Requires Modification |

### 1.3 Effectiveness Review Participants

**Required Participants:**
- **Quality Assurance Manager** (facilitator/coordinator)
- **Responsible Person** (who implemented the action)
- **Quality Manager** (for high-risk CAPAs, approves closure)
- **Subject Matter Experts** (as applicable, to provide technical input)

**Optional Participants:**
- Department Manager (for operational actions)
- QP (for critical actions)
- Relevant personnel with product/process knowledge

## SECTION 2: 30-DAY EFFECTIVENESS CHECK PROCEDURES

### 2.1 Pre-Meeting Preparation (5-7 days before meeting)

**Quality Assurance Manager:**

1. **Schedule Meeting**
   - Send meeting notice 1-2 weeks in advance
   - Include: CAPAN, issue description, meeting objective
   - Attendees: Responsible person, QA Manager, SMEs
   - Suggested duration: 30-60 minutes

2. **Gather Implementation Evidence**
   - Request from Responsible Person all evidence of action completion:
     - Training records with attendance and competency assessment results
     - Updated procedure or SOP with approval documentation
     - Equipment maintenance records, calibration certificates
     - System configuration changes with screenshots or technical documentation
     - Physical improvements with photos and inspection records
   - Deadline: 3 working days before meeting

3. **Review Evidence for Completeness**
   - Check: Was action completed as described in CAPA plan?
   - Check: Is evidence objective and verifiable?
   - Flag: Any gaps or incomplete evidence for discussion at meeting

4. **Prepare Meeting Agenda**
   - Review original problem and root cause
   - Review planned corrective action
   - Present evidence of implementation
   - Discuss: Any barriers encountered during implementation?
   - Verify: Is action sustainable without close supervision?

### 2.2 30-Day Effectiveness Check Meeting

**Meeting Objective**: Verify that corrective/preventive action was implemented as planned

**Meeting Agenda** (30-60 minutes):

#### Opening (5 min)
- Welcome and introductions
- Review meeting objective: Implementation Verification
- Confirm attendees understand their roles

#### Review of CAPA and Planned Action (5 min)
- **Facilitator summarizes**:
  - Original problem identified
  - Root cause(s) identified
  - Planned corrective/preventive action(s)
  - Responsible person and target completion date

#### Implementation Evidence Presentation (20-30 min)
- **Responsible Person presents**:
  - How action was implemented
  - Evidence of implementation (documents, records, changes made)
  - Timeline: When was action completed? (On-time, early, or delayed?)
  - Any barriers encountered during implementation?
  - Any modifications to the planned approach?

**Questions to address during presentation:**
- ☐ Was action completed as planned or modified?
- ☐ If modified, why was modification necessary?
- ☐ Is evidence of completion objective and verifiable?
- ☐ Were target dates met?
- ☐ What was the implementation effort required?

#### Initial Effectiveness Assessment (10-15 min)
- **Facilitator/Quality Manager assess**:
  - ☐ Is action implemented completely (100% completion)?
  - ☐ Is implementation correct (meets original specifications)?
  - ☐ Is implementation sustainable without temporary/emergency measures?
  - ☐ Are there any early signs of problem recurrence?
  - ☐ Have related processes been affected negatively?

**Data to review if available:**
- If product quality action: Latest test results for affected product
- If process action: Process data since implementation
- If equipment action: Equipment records since implementation
- If training action: Personnel performance since training

#### Implementation Status Determination (5 min)
- **Green (Implementation Verified)**
  - ☑ Action fully implemented as planned
  - ☑ Evidence of completion objective and complete
  - ☑ No immediate barriers or problems identified
  - **Next step**: Continue to 6-month effectiveness review

- **Yellow (Implementation Issues)**
  - ⚠ Action partially implemented or modified
  - ⚠ Missing evidence or unclear completeness
  - ⚠ Minor barriers identified
  - **Next step**: Extend implementation timeline, address issues, recheck in 15 days

- **Red (Implementation Not Complete)**
  - ✗ Action not completed or not implemented correctly
  - ✗ Implementation blocked by unexpected barriers
  - ✗ Approach requires major revision
  - **Next step**: Revise action plan, extend timeline, reschedule check-in within 2 weeks

#### Closing (5 min)
- Summarize status determination
- Next steps and timeline
- Confirm responsible person understands next actions

## SECTION 3: 6-MONTH SUSTAINED EFFECTIVENESS REVIEW

### 3.1 Pre-Meeting Preparation (1-2 weeks before meeting)

**Quality Assurance Manager:**

1. **Schedule Meeting**
   - Send meeting notice 3-4 weeks in advance
   - Include: CAPAN, timeline for review, data requirements
   - Attendees: Responsible person, QA Manager, Quality Manager (if high-risk CAPA), SMEs
   - Suggested duration: 60-90 minutes

2. **Gather 6-Month Performance Data**

   **For Product Quality CAPAs:**
   - 6 months of test results for affected product(s)
   - Out-of-specification report for past 6 months (should show none or resolution)
   - Customer complaint log for affected product (should show no recurrence)
   - Batch release data showing stable acceptance rate
   - Trend analysis: Has parameter been stable and within specification?

   **For Process CAPAs:**
   - 6 months of batch records for affected process
   - Critical Process Parameters (CPP) data for past 6 months
   - In-Process Controls (IPC) results for past 6 months
   - Yield and productivity data (should show improvement or stability)
   - Process capability analysis (Cpk, if applicable)
   - Environmental monitoring (if applicable) - should show no new excursions

   **For Equipment CAPAs:**
   - Equipment maintenance records for past 6 months
   - Equipment qualification status (still valid?)
   - Equipment validation status (still current?)
   - Calibration history (all within schedule?)
   - Equipment-related deviation log (any new issues?)
   - Equipment performance metrics (if applicable)

   **For Training CAPAs:**
   - Training completion records for all trained personnel
   - Competency assessment results
   - Performance evaluations or observations since training
   - Error/incident reports for trained personnel (should show improvement)
   - Any refresher training needed?

   **For Regulatory CAPAs:**
   - Regulatory inspection results (if any) for affected area
   - Internal audit findings for affected area
   - Regulatory correspondence (any new observations?)

3. **Compile Data Summary**
   - Organize 6 months of performance data chronologically
   - Calculate statistics: Mean, trend, standard deviation, out-of-spec rate
   - Create charts/graphs showing: Baseline (before action) vs. Current (after action)
   - Summarize: Is there objective evidence of improvement or sustained performance?

4. **Prepare Analysis Summary**
   - What does the data show? (Improvement? Stable? Degradation?)
   - Has problem recurred? (Any OOS, deviation, error since implementation?)
   - Has root cause been eliminated? (Would same failure occur without controls?)
   - Are other processes/products affected? (Systemic improvement?)

### 3.2 6-Month Sustained Effectiveness Review Meeting

**Meeting Objective**: Verify that corrective action achieved its intended purpose and that improvement is sustained

**Meeting Agenda** (60-90 minutes):

#### Opening (5-10 min)
- Welcome and introductions
- Review meeting objective: Sustained Effectiveness Verification
- Confirm scope: What period is being reviewed? (6 months post-implementation)

#### Review of CAPA and Implemented Action (10 min)
- **Facilitator summarizes**:
  - Original problem and root cause
  - Planned corrective action
  - 30-day implementation verification results
  - Expected outcome/success criteria

#### 6-Month Performance Data Presentation (25-35 min)
- **QA Manager presents comprehensive data analysis**:

  **Data Presentation Components:**
  - Timeline: Charts showing data before and after action implementation
  - Baseline vs. Current: Comparison of performance pre/post action
  - Statistical Summary: Mean, range, variation, out-of-spec occurrences
  - Trend Analysis: Is improvement trending, stable, or degrading?
  - Breakdown by factor: Any variation by product, batch, equipment, personnel, time?

  **Key Charts/Data to Present:**
  - **Before/After Comparison Table**: Shows clear improvement or sustained performance
  - **Control Chart**: Shows process stability with no excursions
  - **Histogram/Distribution**: Shows clustering around target, within specification
  - **Trend Line**: Shows no upward or downward drift

  **Data-Specific Questions Addressed:**
  - **Product Quality**: "Have there been any OOS results in the past 6 months?"
  - **Process**: "Have Critical Process Parameters remained in control?"
  - **Equipment**: "Has equipment maintained calibration and functionality?"
  - **Training**: "Have trained personnel demonstrated sustained competency?"
  - **Regulatory**: "Have there been any new observations or non-conformances?"

#### Effectiveness Assessment (20-30 min)
- **Team assesses effectiveness against predefined criteria**:

  **Effectiveness Criteria for Corrective Actions:**
  - ☑ No recurrence of original problem in 6-month period
  - ☑ Root cause has been eliminated (not just masked)
  - ☑ Related processes/products not negatively affected
  - ☑ Improvement is sustainable with current controls
  - ☑ No new problems introduced by the corrective action
  - ☑ Data trends show stability or improvement (no degradation)

  **Effectiveness Questions:**
  - "If we removed the corrective action today, would the problem recur?" (If YES → Action still needed)
  - "Is the improvement due to the action or other factors?" (Confirm causation)
  - "Does similar problem exist in other areas?" (Identify systemic issues)
  - "Are the controls adequate to prevent future recurrence?" (Verify sustainability)

#### Systemic Issue Evaluation (10-15 min)
- **Evaluate whether issue was unique or systemic**:
  - "Is this problem occurring elsewhere?" (Other products, processes, areas?)
  - "Should this corrective action be applied facility-wide?" (Systemic improvement?)
  - "Are there similar products/processes at risk?" (Preventive action needed?)
  - "Does management review need to address systemic issues?" (Policy/procedure change?)

**If systemic issue identified:**
- **New CAPA may be initiated**: "SYSTEMIC ISSUE" label, broader investigation scope
- **Document systemic impact**: Include in effectiveness review meeting minutes

#### Effectiveness Status Determination (5-10 min)

**GREEN (Effectiveness Confirmed)**
- ☑ Problem has NOT recurred in 6-month period
- ☑ Root cause has been ELIMINATED (confirmed by data)
- ☑ Improvement is SUSTAINED and STABLE
- ☑ No new problems introduced by corrective action
- ☑ Controls are adequate to prevent recurrence
- **Recommendation**: APPROVE CAPA CLOSURE

**YELLOW (Effectiveness Uncertain)**
- ⚠ Data is mixed or inconclusive
- ⚠ Partial improvement but not complete resolution
- ⚠ Trend is stable but data period short
- ⚠ Root cause may only be partially addressed
- **Recommendation**: EXTEND MONITORING 3 months, RECHECK, or TAKE ADDITIONAL ACTION

**RED (Effectiveness Not Confirmed)**
- ✗ Problem HAS recurred or partially recurred
- ✗ Root cause NOT eliminated (problem returning without action)
- ✗ Improvement not sustained (degrading trend)
- ✗ New problems introduced by corrective action
- ✗ Systemic issues identified affecting multiple areas
- **Recommendation**: REVISE ACTION PLAN, RETURN TO ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS, REIMPLEMENT

#### Closing and Next Steps (5-10 min)
- **Summarize effectiveness determination**
- **Explain closure decision**:
  - If GREEN: "CAPA is CLOSED - Action was successful, no further action needed"
  - If YELLOW: "CAPA remains OPEN - Extended monitoring for _____ months, recheck by 2026-01-22"
  - If RED: "CAPA remains OPEN - Revise action plan, investigate deeper root causes, reimplement"
- **Assign next step responsibilities**
- **Confirm closure timeline or extended monitoring plan**

## SECTION 5: COMMON EFFECTIVENESS REVIEW SCENARIOS

### Scenario 1: Straightforward Corrective Action - Effective

**Issue**: OOS test result for microbial count
**Root Cause**: Cleaning procedure inadequate
**Action**: Revised cleaning procedure with enhanced validation
**6-Month Data**: No OOS results, microbial counts stable within spec
**Result**: ☑ **GREEN** - EFFECTIVENESS CONFIRMED → CAPA CLOSED

### Scenario 2: Training-Related Action - Requires Extended Review

**Issue**: Personnel errors in batch record completion
**Root Cause**: Training gap, procedure ambiguity
**Action**: Comprehensive training, revised SOP with more detailed instructions
**6-Month Data**: No training-related errors observed, personnel demonstrating competency
**Result**: ☑ **GREEN** - EFFECTIVENESS CONFIRMED → CAPA CLOSED

### Scenario 3: Equipment Change - Needs Validation

**Issue**: Equipment malfunction causing batch upset
**Root Cause**: Equipment age, inadequate maintenance
**Action**: Replacement with new equipment, upgraded maintenance schedule
**6-Month Data**: Equipment functioning properly but only limited batch history
**Complication**: Limited data due to recent installation
**Result**: ☑ **YELLOW** - Provisional effectiveness → Extended monitoring to 12 months

### Scenario 4: Systemic Issue Identified During Effectiveness Review

**Issue**: Multiple OOS results
**Root Cause**: Environmental monitoring not catching early excursions
**Action**: Enhanced environmental monitoring protocol
**6-Month Data**: Monitoring improved, no OOS, BUT similar issues found in adjacent area
**Result**: ☑ **GREEN** for original CAPA (effectiveness confirmed) BUT ☑ **NEW SYSTEMIC CAPA** initiated for facility-wide environmental control

### Scenario 5: Effectiveness Not Confirmed - Reopened CAPA

**Issue**: Recurrent deviation in document review process
**Root Cause**: Inadequate document control procedure
**Action**: Implemented new electronic document system
**6-Month Data**: System implemented BUT still seeing similar deviations, suggesting root cause not addressed
**Result**: ☑ **RED** - Effectiveness NOT Confirmed → CAPA REOPENED for deeper investigation
