SECTION 7: DEVIATIONS / PROBLEM-SOLVING
================================================================================

7.1 WHAT CONSTITUTES A DEVIATION?

A deviation is any departure from this procedure, including:
  • Not following steps in order
  • Using different materials or equipment
  • Not obtaining required signatures
  • Skipping quality checkpoints
  • Obtaining incorrect results

7.2 HOW TO HANDLE A DEVIATION

If a deviation occurs during this procedure:

STEP 1: STOP the procedure immediately
  • Do not proceed if something is wrong
  • Do not attempt to cover it up

STEP 2: DOCUMENT what happened
  • Write down what went wrong
  • Note when it happened
  • Identify who was involved
  • Describe the deviation clearly

STEP 3: NOTIFY your supervisor immediately
  • Inform [Job title] of the deviation
  • Provide written documentation
  • Do not release any product affected

STEP 4: COMPLETE a Deviation Report
  • Use Form: [Reference deviation report form]
  • Submit to: Quality Assurance Manager
  • Attach documentation

STEP 5: INVESTIGATION & CORRECTIVE ACTION
  • Quality Assurance will investigate
  • Root cause will be determined
  • Corrective action will be assigned
  • Follow-up verification will confirm effectiveness

For detailed CAPA procedures, see: QAS-05-002 (CAPA SOP)
