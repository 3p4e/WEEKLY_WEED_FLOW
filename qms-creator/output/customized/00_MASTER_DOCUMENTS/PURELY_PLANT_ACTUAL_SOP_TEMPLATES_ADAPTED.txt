5. DEFINITIONS

                         (Reflects optimal final moisture content)

Critical Process Parameter The temperature, humidity, and time parameters
(CPP):                   that directly affect final product quality

Control Tray:            Representative sample of material distributed
                         throughout drying cycle for moisture checks

Wet Weight:              Weight of freshly trimmed material
                         (higher moisture content)

Dry Weight:              Weight of material after drying cycle
                         (target moisture <10%)

Homogenization:          Process of mixing dried material for uniformity

Batch:                   All cannabis material from single harvest
                         processed together with unique batch number

7. DRYING PROCEDURE - STEP BY STEP

7.1 PRE-DRYING PREPARATION (Day Before Drying)

Step 1: Verify Environmental Conditions
  ☐ Check drying room temperature: 22-24°C
  ☐ Check drying room humidity: <40%RH
  ☐ Verify HVAC system operational
  ☐ Verify Testo data loggers operational
  ☐ Check weather forecast (no unexpected humidity change expected)
  ☐ Document conditions in Drying Logbook - Annex 04/02

Step 2: Prepare Drying Equipment (CDS24 Machine)
  ☐ Inspect machine for any residual material from previous cycle
  ☐ Clean all internal surfaces per EQU-02-XXX (Equipment SOP)
  ☐ Verify all shelves/trays are clean and dry
  ☐ Verify carts move freely and lock in place
  ☐ Verify door seals properly and alarm system functional
  ☐ Verify temperature and humidity sensors calibrated and responsive
  ☐ Run empty cycle test: Verify machine reaches target temperature/humidity
