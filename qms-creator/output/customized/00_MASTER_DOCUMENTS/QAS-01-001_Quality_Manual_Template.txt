1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

This Quality Manual (QM) describes the Quality Management System (QMS) 
implemented at Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility, a medical cannabis production facility located 
in 123 Cannabis Boulevard, Skopje, 1000, Skopje Region, North Macedonia, North Macedonia.

This manual serves as the primary governance document establishing:
- Quality policy and objectives
- Organizational structure and responsibilities
- Commitment to regulatory compliance and GMP standards
- Documentation and record management procedures
- Communication and escalation pathways

This Quality Manual is mandatory reading for all personnel with access to 
quality-related processes and must be reviewed annually or when significant 
changes occur in the organization or regulatory requirements.

5. QUALITY POLICY
================================================================================

QUALITY POLICY STATEMENT

Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility is committed to producing medical cannabis products of the 
highest quality, safety, and consistency while maintaining full compliance 
with EU GMP standards (Annex 7 - Herbal Preparations) and all applicable 
North Macedonian regulations.

Our quality policy includes:

→ SAFETY FIRST
  We prioritize product safety above all other considerations. Every product 
  released meets rigorous testing standards for microbial contamination, 
  pesticide residues, heavy metals, and cannabinoid potency.

→ REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
  We maintain full compliance with:
  • EU GMP Annex 7 (Herbal Preparations)
  • North Macedonia Medical Cannabis Regulations
  • International standards for herbal products (Ph. Eur., USP)
  • All licensing requirements and conditions

→ CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
  We systematically evaluate and improve our processes through:
  • Regular audits and self-inspections
  • Root cause analysis of deviations
  • Implementation of corrective and preventive actions
  • Evaluation of customer feedback and complaints
  • Training and competency development

→ TRANSPARENCY & TRACEABILITY
  We maintain complete documentation traceability from propagation through 
  final product delivery. All records are maintained in a secure, accessible 
  format suitable for both digital and paper-based systems.

→ QUALITY CULTURE
  We foster a quality-conscious culture where every team member understands 
  their role in maintaining product quality and regulatory compliance.

7. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

KEY POSITIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

Facility Manager
- Overall responsibility for facility operations and QMS effectiveness
- Ensures adequate resources and personnel
- Approves all quality-related decisions above specified thresholds
- Communicates with regulatory authorities
- Conducts quarterly management reviews

Quality Assurance Manager
- Oversees development and maintenance of QMS procedures
- Responsible for internal audits and self-inspections
- Manages deviation, CAPA, and change control processes
- Reviews batch releases and product quality data
- Ensures training compliance and records maintenance

Production Manager
- Oversees all cultivation and production activities
- Ensures compliance with SOPs and work instructions
- Conducts daily facility evaluations
- Manages batch documentation and production records
- Implements corrective actions for production deviations

Sanitation & Hygiene Manager
- Develops and implements cleaning and sanitation procedures
- Conducts environmental monitoring (microbiological sampling)
- Manages pest control and facility cleanliness
- Maintains hygiene training records

Security Manager
- Oversees access control and visitor management
- Maintains security systems and incident logs
- Ensures chain of custody documentation
- Manages regulatory compliance reporting

HR Manager
- Manages recruitment, training, and personnel records
- Maintains competency assessments and training matrices
- Conducts health and safety reviews
- Manages terminations and personnel files

Document/Records Manager
- Maintains master document list and distribution
- Manages records retention and archival
- Conducts document control reviews
- Ensures data integrity and record security

Equipment Manager
- Manages equipment qualification and maintenance
- Maintains calibration schedules and logs
- Oversees equipment change control and replacement
- Ensures spare parts inventory

All Personnel
- Understand and follow applicable SOPs and work instructions
- Report deviations, concerns, and improvement suggestions
- Participate in required training
- Maintain confidentiality and data security
- Support facility quality objectives

RECORDS MANAGEMENT:

All records are maintained according to SOP-REC-02-001 (Records Management)
with the following requirements:

Retention Period:        [RETENTION_PERIODS BY RECORD TYPE]
Storage Location:        Primary & Daily automated backup at 23:00 CET to off-site cloud storage + local backup server
Archive Location:        Dedicated Archive Room (Climate Controlled)
Retrieval Process:       See REC-02-003 (Record Retrieval Form)
Destruction Process:     Cannabis waste is rendered unrecognizable and unusable by:
1. Mixing with non-hazardous waste (coffee grounds, soil) at 50/50 ratio
2. Placement in locked waste containers
3. Disposal via licensed waste management contractor
4. Documented with destruction records (FM-SEC-003)
5. Witnessed by Security Manager and documented

Data Security:           - 24/7 CCTV surveillance with 90-day retention
- Access control system with biometric authentication
- Secure vault with dual-lock system
- Perimeter fencing and motion sensors
- On-site security personnel
- Visitor log and escort requirements

11. COMMUNICATION & MANAGEMENT REVIEW
================================================================================

INTERNAL COMMUNICATION:

Quality meetings are conducted:
  • Daily: Production team briefings
  • Weekly: Quality assurance meeting
  • Monthly: Management review meeting
  • Quarterly: Comprehensive QMS review

Issues & Escalation:
  Level 1: Department Manager (Issues resolved at department level)
  Level 2: Facility Manager (Quality/Regulatory issues)
  Level 3: Executive Management (Major deviations, regulatory matters)

See SOP-QAS-XX for detailed communication procedures

MANAGEMENT REVIEW:

Management conducts comprehensive QMS reviews quarterly, evaluating:

✓ Results of internal audits and self-inspections
✓ Status of product quality data and batch releases
✓ Deviation, CAPA, and change control status
✓ Personnel training and competency assessments
✓ Equipment qualification and maintenance status
✓ Regulatory compliance and communications
✓ Customer feedback and complaints
✓ Effectiveness of document control
✓ Achievement of quality objectives
✓ Recommended improvements and actions

Management Review meetings are documented with:
  • Attendees
  • Discussions and findings
  • Decisions and actions
  • Responsible persons and deadlines

See QAS-07-002 (Annual Product Quality Review)

REGULATORY COMMUNICATION:

All regulatory communications are:
  • Documented and maintained on file
  • Authorized by Facility Manager and Quality Manager
  • Submitted to Ministry of Health of North Macedonia
  • Tracked in Regulatory Communication Log

Regulatory inspections are:
  • Planned and scheduled with appropriate preparation
  • Conducted with complete transparency
  • Documented with formal reports and findings
  • Responded to with corrective action plans
