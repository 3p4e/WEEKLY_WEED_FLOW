Notes:
- Microbiological sample handling: 2–8°C transport; deliver to contracted lab ≤ 6 h. Use sterile containers (0.5–1 L) with sodium thiosulfate for chlorine neutralization where applicable.
- Physico-chemical samples: rinse bottles 3×, store at ambient or per method; pH measurements to be performed per lab instruction (pH test result expiry: 2 h for some methods).
- Sampling point sanitisation: flame metal faucets 30–60s, cool 30–60s, do NOT re-flush after sanitisation for microbiological samples (see SOP A08).
