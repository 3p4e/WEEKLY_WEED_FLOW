2. **Documentation Handling**: Any documents generated as a result of the activities performed under this SOP must be handled according to the standard documentation practices. 5. **Archiving**: Completed documents must be archived in accordance with the retention period of 5 years. Ensure that documents are stored securely and in a manner that they can be readily retrieved for review or audit purposes.

- **Supervisor Checkpoints**: After the completion of each major section of the activity, a supervisor or designated quality representative should verify the work performed. This includes checking for adherence to SOP procedures and GMP principles.

#### 4.4 Completion Criteria

- **Documentation Approval**: All documents related to the activities must be signed and dated by the person performing the activity and reviewed and approved by a supervisor or quality assurance representative.

- **Archival Confirmation**: The final step to signal the completion of activities under this SOP is the confirmation that all related documents have been archived according to the specified retention period and are easily retrievable.

This procedure ensures that activities related to the New SOP are conducted in a manner that is compliant with GMP principles and internal quality standards, ensuring consistency, traceability, and integrity in our operations.

7. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

The purpose of this section is to outline the essential documentation requirements necessary for ensuring compliance, traceability, and quality assurance in our processes. This involves the meticulous recording, maintenance, and retention of Batch Records, Logs, and adherence to a strict Retention Policy.

7.1 Batch Records:
Batch Records are critical documents that provide a detailed account of the production history of each batch of products. These records should include but are not limited to:

- The batch identification number.
- Date of production.
- Detailed description of the product, including specifications.
- Quantities of raw materials used.
- Processing instructions and operations performed.
- Names of personnel involved in the production.
- Any deviations or anomalies observed and corrective actions taken.
- Final inspection and release information.

Batch Records must be created contemporaneously with the operations and must be clear, accurate, and complete.

7.2 Logs:
Logs are utilized to record various operational, maintenance, and quality control activities. These include equipment logs, cleaning logs, calibration logs, and environmental monitoring logs. Logs should contain:

- Date and time of the activity.
- Detailed description of the activity, including equipment or area involved.
- Name of the individual(s) performing the activity.
- Observations and results of the activity.
- Any deviations from standard procedures and corrective actions taken.

Logs should be maintained in a manner that is orderly and easy to review for compliance and audit purposes.

7.3 Retention Policy:
All Batch Records and Logs must be retained for a minimum period of 5 years from the date of manufacture or as required by applicable regulatory requirements, whichever is longer. The retention period is established to ensure that a comprehensive history of all batches, including their production, control, and distribution, is available for review in the case of product recalls, investigations, or compliance verifications.

Retention Policy must ensure that:
- Documents are stored securely in a manner that prevents unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.
- Documents are stored in conditions that preserve their readability and integrity.
- Access to documents is available for regulatory inspection or audit purposes.

In conclusion, adherence to these documentation requirements is essential for ensuring the integrity, safety, and quality of our products. It facilitates effective tracking, traceability, and accountability at all stages of production and distribution.

1. **Overview and Objectives:** A session will be conducted to provide an overview of the new SOP, highlighting its objectives, scope, and the improvements or changes from the previous version.

2. **Detailed Walkthrough:** Detailed training sessions, including a walkthrough of the new SOP, will be provided. This will cover all steps, expected outcomes, and the roles and responsibilities of all involved personnel.

3. **Hands-on Sessions:** Practical, hands-on sessions will be arranged to allow employees to practice the new procedures under supervision, ensuring they understand and can apply the SOP in their daily tasks.

4. **Assessment and Competency Checks:** Post-training, an assessment will be conducted to evaluate the understanding and competency of the employees in implementing the new SOP. This may include quizzes, practical demonstrations, or both.

5. **Feedback Collection:** Feedback will be collected from all participants to identify any areas of confusion or difficulty. This will help in refining the training process and addressing any gaps.

#### 8.2 Retraining

1. **Updates to the SOP:** If the SOP is revised or updated, retraining sessions will be held to acquaint all relevant personnel with the changes.
