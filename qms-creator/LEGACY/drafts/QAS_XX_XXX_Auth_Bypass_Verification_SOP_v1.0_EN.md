#### 4.2 Process Steps

1. **Initiation of Verification Process:**
   - Begin the process by logging into the system using an administrator or equivalent account that has the necessary permissions to perform Auth Bypass Verification.
   
2. **Selection of Bypass Criteria:**
   - Select the specific criteria or conditions under which the authentication bypass will be verified. This could involve scenarios such as system recovery, emergency access, or other predefined conditions warranting bypass.

3. **Execution of Bypass:**
   - Perform the authentication bypass under the selected criteria, ensuring to document the start time, end time, and any other relevant parameters of the bypass execution.

4. **Verification of Bypass Functionality:**
   - Verify that the bypass functions as intended, providing the required access or privileges without compromising security or operational integrity.

5. **Logging and Reporting:**
   - Log all actions taken during the process, including any deviations from expected outcomes. Ensure comprehensive documentation of the verification process, outcome, and any observations noted.

6. **Review and Approval:**
   - The completed verification process and its documentation should be reviewed and approved by authorized personnel. This serves to validate the effectiveness and appropriateness of the Auth Bypass Verification.

#### 4.3 In-Process Controls

- **Documentation Accuracy:** Throughout the process, ensure that all actions, observations, and outcomes are accurately recorded. This documentation should be clear, legible, and free of any errors or omissions.
  
- **Adherence to SOP:** At each step of the process, verify that the actions taken are in strict adherence to the defined SOP. Any deviations should be noted and addressed accordingly.
  
- **Security Checks:** Ensure that the verification process does not inadvertently compromise system security. Special attention should be paid to ensuring that the Auth Bypass does not remain active beyond the verification period.

#### 4.4 Completion Criteria

The Auth Bypass Verification SOP is considered complete when the following criteria are met:
