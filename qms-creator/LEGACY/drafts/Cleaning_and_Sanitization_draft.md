## 1. PURPOSE

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes the systematic approach for Cleaning and Sanitization
in compliance with EudraLex Volume 4 - EU GMP, EudraLex Annex 15 - Qualification and Validation, WHO GMP Guidelines, Good Agricultural and Collection Practices to ensure product quality, safety, and regulatory compliance.

The procedure aims to:
- Provide clear, reproducible procedures for all personnel
- Ensure complete documentation and traceability

This SOP supports the Quality Management System by ensuring consistent quality, compliance, and continuous improvement.



## 2. SCOPE

### 2.1 Applicability
This procedure applies to All relevant facilities and covers Activities related to Cleaning and Sanitization.

## 4. PROCEDURE

### 4.1 Prerequisites
Before beginning this procedure, ensure:
- All required documentation is available

### 4.2 Process Steps

#### 4.2.1 Preparation
Prepare the work area and gather required materials.

**Critical Parameters:**
- Temperature: 20-25°C
- Humidity: 45-65% RH

**Documentation:**
Record preparation activities in the batch record

#### 4.2.2 Execution
Perform the main process steps as defined.

**Quality Checks:**
- Verify parameters are within specification
- Document all observations

### 4.3 In-Process Controls
N/A

### 4.4 Completion Criteria
The process is complete when:
- All process steps have been completed
- All documentation is complete and accurate
- Quality checks have passed
- Area has been cleaned and cleared
