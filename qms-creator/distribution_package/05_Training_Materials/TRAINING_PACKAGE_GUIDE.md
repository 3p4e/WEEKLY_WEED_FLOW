## 1. Purpose

This guide accompanies the GMP Training Distribution Package for cannabis facility personnel. It provides instructions for trainers and trainees on using the QMS documentation for qualification and ongoing competency development.

## 3. Training Sequence

### 3.1 New Employee Onboarding (Week 1-2)

**Day 1-2: Orientation**
- Company overview and facility tour
- QA_00.01 Quality Manual overview
- HRM_05.01 Personnel Management SOP

**Day 3-5: GMP Fundamentals**
- HRM_05.04 GMP Training Program
- HRM_05.05 Employee Hygiene
- HRM_05.07 Gowning Procedures
- Practical gowning assessment

**Week 2: Role-Specific Training**
- Department-specific SOPs
- Supervised practical tasks
- Initial competency assessment

### 3.2 Ongoing Training Requirements

| Frequency | Training Type | Documentation |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| Initial | All assigned SOPs | Training Record Form |
| Annual | GMP Refresher | Requalification Record |
| As needed | New/Revised SOPs | Training Acknowledgment |
| Quarterly | Competency Checks | Assessment Forms |

## 5. Training Records

### 5.1 Required Documentation

For each training session, complete:
1. **Training Attendance Log** - Date, trainer, attendees
2. **Training Record Form** - Individual employee record
3. **Assessment Results** - Test scores or practical evaluation
4. **Competency Certificate** - Upon successful completion

### 5.2 Record Retention

| Record Type | Retention Period |
|-------------|------------------|
| Training Records | Employment + 5 years |
| Assessment Results | Employment + 5 years |
| Competency Certificates | Employment + 5 years |
| Training Materials | Current version + 2 years |

---

## 6. Assessment Standards

### 6.1 Written Assessments
- Minimum passing score: 80%
- Retake allowed after 1 week additional training
- Maximum 3 attempts before remedial action

### 6.2 Practical Assessments
- Observed performance by qualified trainer
- Checklist-based evaluation
- Must demonstrate all critical steps correctly

### 6.3 Competency Verification
- Annual requalification required
- Unannounced competency checks quarterly
- Documented observation of routine tasks

## 8. Trainer Qualifications

### 8.1 Internal Trainers Must Have:
- Minimum 2 years GMP experience
- Completed Train-the-Trainer program
- Current qualification in subject area
- Approved by QA Manager

### 8.2 External Trainers Must Provide:
- CV demonstrating expertise
- References from similar facilities
- Training materials for QA review
- Compliance with facility procedures

## 9. Training Effectiveness Evaluation

### 9.1 Metrics Tracked

| Metric | Target | Review Frequency |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| First-time pass rate | >90% | Monthly |
| Training completion rate | 100% | Monthly |
| Deviation reduction | Year-over-year | Quarterly |
| Competency check pass rate | >95% | Quarterly |

### 9.2 Continuous Improvement
- Training effectiveness reviewed in Management Review
- Feedback collected from trainees
- SOPs updated based on training gaps identified
- Annual training program review
