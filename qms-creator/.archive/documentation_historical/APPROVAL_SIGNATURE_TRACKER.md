## Instructions

This tracking sheet records the approval status of all QMS documents. Each document requires signatures from:
1. **Prepared by**: Document author/creator
2. **Reviewed by**: Technical reviewer (typically Department Manager)
3. **Approved by**: Final approver (QP or QA Manager)

**Status Codes:**
- ⬜ Pending - Not yet signed
- 🟡 In Review - Under review
- ✅ Approved - All signatures complete
- ❌ Rejected - Requires revision
