## 1. PURPOSE

This SOP establishes procedures for the vegetative growth phase of cannabis production plants from transplant through transition to flowering at <span class="var">[FACILITY_NAME]</span>.

---

## 2. SCOPE

Applies to:
- Transplanting rooted clones from propagation
- Vegetative room management
- Plant training and canopy management
- Nutrition and irrigation during vegetative phase
- Transition to flowering

## 4. PROCEDURE

### 4.1 Clone Receipt and Transplanting

#### 4.1.1 Receiving Clones from Propagation

1. Verify transfer documentation <span class="var">[FM-PROP-003]</span>
2. Inspect clones:
   - Root development adequate
   - No pest or disease signs
   - Healthy foliage
3. Record receipt in Vegetative Batch Record <span class="var">[FM-VEG-001]</span>

#### 4.1.2 Container Preparation

| Container Size | Vegetative Duration | Plant Spacing |
|----------------|---------------------|---------------|
| <span class="var">[4 L (1 gal)]</span> | <span class="var">[2-3 weeks]</span> | <span class="var">[30 x 30 cm]</span> |
| <span class="var">[11 L (3 gal)]</span> | <span class="var">[4-5 weeks]</span> | <span class="var">[45 x 45 cm]</span> |
| <span class="var">[19 L (5 gal)]</span> | <span class="var">[5-6 weeks]</span> | <span class="var">[60 x 60 cm]</span> |

**Preparation Steps:**
1. Clean containers per <span class="var">[PRO_05.02]</span>
2. Fill with growing medium (<span class="var">[coco coir/perlite 70:30]</span>)
3. Pre-moisten with dilute nutrient solution (EC <span class="var">[0.8-1.0]</span>)
4. Label with batch/plant ID

#### 4.1.3 Transplanting Procedure

1. Create hole in medium sized for root ball
2. Remove clone from propagation container gently
3. Place at same depth as original (crown at surface)
4. Fill around root ball, firm gently
5. Initial watering:
   - pH: <span class="var">[5.8]</span>
   - EC: <span class="var">[0.8-1.0 mS/cm]</span>
   - Volume: <span class="var">[Until 10% runoff]</span>
6. Label plant with:
   - Batch ID
   - Plant number
   - Cultivar
   - Transplant date

### 4.2 Vegetative Growth Management

#### 4.2.1 Week 1-2: Establishment Phase

**Environmental:**
- Slightly higher humidity: <span class="var">[65-70%]</span>
- Reduced light intensity: <span class="var">[400-500 PPFD]</span>
- CO₂: <span class="var">[ambient or 800 ppm]</span>

**Nutrition:**
- Week 1: <span class="var">[EC 1.0-1.2 mS/cm]</span>
- Week 2: <span class="var">[EC 1.2-1.4 mS/cm]</span>
- Full vegetative nutrient formula

**Monitoring:**
- Check for transplant stress
- Monitor for wilting
- Observe root establishment (water uptake)

#### 4.2.2 Week 3-4: Active Growth Phase

**Environmental:**
- Standard vegetative conditions
- Full light intensity: <span class="var">[600 PPFD]</span>
- CO₂ supplementation: <span class="var">[1000 ppm]</span>

**Nutrition:**
- EC: <span class="var">[1.4-1.6 mS/cm]</span>
- Increase frequency as plants grow
- Monitor runoff EC (should be <span class="var">[<2.0 mS/cm]</span>)

**Plant Training:**
- Begin topping when <span class="var">[5-6 nodes]</span> present
- Initiate Low Stress Training (LST)
- Remove lower growth (lollipopping) as needed

#### 4.2.3 Week 5+: Pre-Flower Preparation

**Environmental:**
- Maintain all parameters
- Begin reducing nitrogen ratio in nutrition
- Consider pre-transition root flush

**Plant Management:**
- Final training adjustments
- Canopy leveling
- Remove lower <span class="var">[1/3]</span> growth
- Assess readiness for flip

### 4.3 Plant Training Techniques

#### 4.3.1 Topping

**Purpose:** Create multiple main colas, bushier growth

**Procedure:**
1. Wait until plant has <span class="var">[5-6 nodes]</span>
2. Sterilize cutting tool
3. Cut main stem above <span class="var">[4th or 5th node]</span>
4. Clean cut, remove apical meristem
5. Allow <span class="var">[3-5 days]</span> recovery before further training

#### 4.3.2 Low Stress Training (LST)

**Purpose:** Horizontal canopy development, even light distribution

**Procedure:**
1. Once stems are pliable (post-topping)
2. Gently bend branches horizontally
3. Secure with soft ties or clips
4. Do NOT break stems
5. Reposition weekly as growth continues

#### 4.3.3 SCROG (Screen of Green) - If Used

**Setup:**
- Install trellis net at <span class="var">[30-40 cm]</span> above containers
- Net opening: <span class="var">[10 x 10 cm]</span> squares

**Training:**
1. Allow plants to grow through net
2. Tuck branches below net, spread horizontally
3. Continue until <span class="var">[70-80%]</span> of net filled
4. Transition to flower when canopy even

#### 4.3.4 Defoliation

**Timing:** <span class="var">[Days 1-7 of veg week 3+]</span>

**Guidelines:**
- Remove only large fan leaves blocking light
- Never remove more than <span class="var">[20-30%]</span> foliage at once
- Focus on lower, shaded leaves
- Allow <span class="var">[5-7 days]</span> recovery between sessions

### 4.4 Daily Monitoring Checklist

| Parameter | Check | Record |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Temperature | Verify within range | Log if excursion |
| Humidity | Verify within range | Log if excursion |
| CO₂ | Verify supplementation | Log reading |
| Plants visual | Inspect each plant | Note abnormalities |
| Irrigation system | Confirm operation | Log any issues |
| Pest/disease | Look for signs | Report immediately |
| Growth progress | Assess development | Weekly measurement |

Document in Daily Vegetative Room Log <span class="var">[FM-VEG-002]</span>

### 4.5 Transition to Flowering

#### 4.5.1 Readiness Criteria

| Criterion | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Plant height | <span class="var">[30-50 cm]</span> (cultivar dependent) |
| Node count | <span class="var">[8-12 nodes]</span> |
| Canopy development | Even, fills allocated space |
| Health status | No pest/disease, no deficiencies |
| Root development | Established, healthy (check drainage) |
| Vegetative duration | <span class="var">[4-6 weeks minimum]</span> |

#### 4.5.2 Pre-Transition Procedure

1. Complete Vegetative Phase Completion Form <span class="var">[FM-VEG-003]</span>
2. Final plant count and health assessment
3. Verify all training complete
4. Coordinate with Flowering Room for transfer
5. Prepare transfer documentation

#### 4.5.3 Transfer Protocol

1. Move plants during dark period or dim lighting
2. Minimize environmental stress
3. Immediately adjust to flowering schedule:
   - Photoperiod: <span class="var">[12/12]</span>
4. Complete transfer documentation <span class="var">[FM-VEG-004]</span>
5. Update batch tracking system
