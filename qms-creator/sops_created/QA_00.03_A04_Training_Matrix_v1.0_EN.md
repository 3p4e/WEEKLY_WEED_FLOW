# TRAINING MATRIX

## Purpose

This Training Matrix defines the mandatory training requirements for each job role at <span class="var">[FACILITY_NAME]</span>. It identifies which training modules are required (R), recommended (O), or not applicable (N/A) for each position to ensure personnel are qualified to perform their assigned duties in compliance with EU GMP requirements.

## Legend

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **R** | Required - Mandatory training before performing job duties |
| **O** | Optional - Recommended but not mandatory |
| **N/A** | Not Applicable - Training not relevant to role |
| **I** | Initial - Required within first <span class="var">[30 days]</span> of employment |
