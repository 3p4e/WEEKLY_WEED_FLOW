## 1. PURPOSE

This SOP establishes procedures for microbiological testing of cannabis products at <span class="var">[FACILITY_NAME]</span> to ensure compliance with Ph. Eur. 5.1.4 and 5.1.8 microbial quality requirements for herbal medicinal products.

---

## 2. SCOPE

Applies to:
- Total Aerobic Microbial Count (TAMC)
- Total Yeast and Mold Count (TYMC)
- Bile-tolerant Gram-negative bacteria
- *Salmonella* spp. detection
- *Escherichia coli* detection
- *Staphylococcus aureus* detection
- *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* detection

## 5. MEDIA AND REAGENTS

### 5.1 Culture Media

| Medium | Use | Incubation |
|--------|-----|------------|

| Violet Red Bile Glucose Agar (VRBGA) | Bile-tolerant gram-neg | <span class="var">[30-35°C, 24-48 hours]</span> |

| Buffered Peptone Water (BPW) | Dilution/pre-enrichment | - |
| Tryptone Soya Broth (TSB) | Enrichment | <span class="var">[30-35°C, 24 hours]</span> |

### 5.2 Media Preparation

1. Prepare according to manufacturer instructions
2. Autoclave at <span class="var">[121°C for 15 minutes]</span>
3. Cool in water bath to <span class="var">[45-50°C]</span> before pouring
4. Pour plates aseptically (<span class="var">[~20 mL per plate]</span>)
5. Store prepared plates at <span class="var">[2-8°C]</span>
6. Use within <span class="var">[2 weeks]</span> of preparation

### 5.3 Media Quality Control

| Test | Requirement | Frequency |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Sterility | No growth after 48h incubation | Each batch |
| Growth promotion | Positive control organisms grow | Each batch |
| Selectivity | Negative control organisms inhibited | Each batch |
| pH verification | Within specification | Each batch |

## 6. SAMPLE PREPARATION

### 6.1 Sample Receipt

1. Verify sample identification and integrity
2. Record receipt in Microbiology Log <span class="var">[FM-MICRO-001]</span>
3. Store at <span class="var">[2-8°C]</span> until testing
4. Test within <span class="var">[24 hours]</span> of receipt

### 6.2 Test Sample Preparation

#### 6.2.1 Initial Suspension (1:10 Dilution)

1. Work in laminar flow hood using aseptic technique
2. Weigh <span class="var">[10.0 g]</span> sample into sterile stomacher bag
3. Add <span class="var">[90 mL]</span> sterile buffered peptone water
4. Homogenize in stomacher for <span class="var">[2 minutes]</span>
5. Allow to settle for <span class="var">[5 minutes]</span>
6. This is the <span class="var">[10⁻¹ dilution]</span>

#### 6.2.2 Serial Dilutions

| Dilution | Preparation |
|----------|-------------|
| 10⁻¹ | Initial suspension |
| 10⁻² | 1 mL of 10⁻¹ + 9 mL BPW |
| 10⁻³ | 1 mL of 10⁻² + 9 mL BPW |
| 10⁻⁴ | 1 mL of 10⁻³ + 9 mL BPW |

Use new sterile pipette for each dilution step.

## 7. TEST PROCEDURES

### 7.1 Total Aerobic Microbial Count (TAMC)

**Method:** Pour plate (Ph. Eur. 2.6.12)

**Procedure:**
1. Pipette <span class="var">[1 mL]</span> from each dilution (10⁻¹ to 10⁻⁴) into sterile petri dishes (duplicate)
2. Add <span class="var">[15-20 mL]</span> molten TSA (45-50°C) to each plate
3. Mix gently by swirling
4. Allow to solidify
5. Invert plates and incubate at <span class="var">[30-35°C for 3-5 days]</span>
6. Count colonies on plates with <span class="var">[30-300 colonies]</span>

**Calculation:**
$$TAMC\ (CFU/g) = \frac{\sum Colonies}{(n_1 + 0.1n_2) × d} $$

Where:
- n₁ = number of plates counted at first dilution
- n₂ = number of plates counted at second dilution
- d = dilution factor of first dilution counted

### 7.2 Total Yeast and Mold Count (TYMC)

**Method:** Pour plate (Ph. Eur. 2.6.12)

**Procedure:**
1. Pipette <span class="var">[1 mL]</span> from dilutions 10⁻¹ to 10⁻³ into petri dishes (duplicate)
2. Add <span class="var">[15-20 mL]</span> molten SDA (with antibiotics if specified)
3. Mix and solidify
4. Incubate at <span class="var">[20-25°C for 5-7 days]</span>
5. Count colonies (yeasts appear within 2-3 days, molds within 5-7 days)
6. Count plates with <span class="var">[30-300 colonies]</span>

### 7.3 Bile-Tolerant Gram-Negative Bacteria

**Method:** Pour plate with selective medium

**Procedure:**
1. Pipette <span class="var">[1 mL]</span> from dilutions 10⁻¹ to 10⁻³ into petri dishes (duplicate)
2. Add <span class="var">[15-20 mL]</span> molten VRBGA
3. Allow to solidify
4. Overlay with <span class="var">[5 mL]</span> additional VRBGA
5. Incubate at <span class="var">[30-35°C for 24-48 hours]</span>
6. Count characteristic purple-red colonies (bile precipitation zone)

### 7.4 *Escherichia coli* Detection

**Method:** Selective enrichment and confirmation (Ph. Eur. 2.6.13)

**Procedure:**

*Enrichment:*
1. Inoculate <span class="var">[10 mL]</span> of 10⁻¹ dilution into <span class="var">[100 mL]</span> MacConkey broth
2. Incubate at <span class="var">[42-44°C for 24-48 hours]</span>

*Isolation:*
3. Streak from turbid broth onto MacConkey agar
4. Incubate at <span class="var">[30-35°C for 24 hours]</span>
5. *E. coli* colonies: Pink/red, may have bile precipitation

*Confirmation:*
6. Subculture suspect colonies
7. Perform biochemical identification:
   - Indole test: Positive
   - MR-VP: MR positive, VP negative
   - Citrate: Negative
   - Or use API/VITEK identification

**Result:** Report as "Detected" or "Not Detected" per g tested

### 7.5 *Salmonella* Detection

**Method:** Enrichment and selective isolation (Ph. Eur. 2.6.13)

**Procedure:**

*Pre-enrichment:*
1. Add <span class="var">[25 g]</span> sample to <span class="var">[225 mL]</span> buffered peptone water
2. Incubate at <span class="var">[30-35°C for 18-24 hours]</span>

*Selective enrichment:*
3. Transfer <span class="var">[0.1 mL]</span> to <span class="var">[10 mL]</span> Rappaport-Vassiliadis broth
4. Transfer <span class="var">[1 mL]</span> to <span class="var">[10 mL]</span> Tetrathionate broth
5. Incubate RV at <span class="var">[30-35°C]</span>, TT at <span class="var">[42-44°C]</span> for <span class="var">[24 hours]</span>

*Selective isolation:*
6. Streak from each enrichment onto XLD and Brilliant Green agar
7. Incubate at <span class="var">[30-35°C for 24-48 hours]</span>
8. *Salmonella* on XLD: Red colonies with black centers

*Confirmation:*
9. Biochemical identification and/or serological confirmation

**Result:** Report as "Absent" or "Detected" per 25 g

### 7.6 *Staphylococcus aureus* Detection

**Method:** Enrichment and selective isolation (Ph. Eur. 2.6.13)

**Procedure:**
1. Inoculate <span class="var">[1 g]</span> sample (from 10⁻¹ dilution) into <span class="var">[100 mL]</span> Tryptone Soya Broth
2. Incubate at <span class="var">[30-35°C for 24 hours]</span>
3. Streak onto Mannitol Salt Agar and Baird-Parker Agar
4. Incubate at <span class="var">[30-35°C for 24-48 hours]</span>
5. *S. aureus*: Yellow colonies on MSA, black with halo on BP
6. Confirm with coagulase test

**Result:** Report as "Absent" or "Detected" per 1 g

### 7.7 *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* Detection

**Procedure:**
1. Enrich as for *S. aureus*
2. Streak onto Cetrimide agar
3. Incubate at <span class="var">[30-35°C for 24-48 hours]</span>
4. *P. aeruginosa*: Blue-green pigmented colonies
5. Confirm with oxidase test (positive) and biochemical ID

**Result:** Report as "Absent" or "Detected" per 1 g

## 9. METHOD SUITABILITY (Validation)

Per Ph. Eur. 2.6.12/2.6.13, verify product does not interfere with microbial recovery.

### 9.1 Growth Promotion Test

| Test Organism | Inoculum Level | Recovery Requirement |
|---------------|----------------|----------------------|
| Test organisms per method | <span class="var">[<100 CFU]</span> | <span class="var">[50-200% of control]</span> |

If recovery <50%, use validated neutralization or dilution method.

---

## 10. RESULTS INTERPRETATION

### 10.1 Counting Rules

- Count plates with <span class="var">[30-300 colonies]</span> (TAMC) or <span class="var">[15-150 colonies]</span> (TYMC)
- If all plates <30, count lowest dilution
- If all plates >300, count highest dilution (estimate)
- Report results to 2 significant figures

### 10.2 Reporting

| Result | Report As |
|--------|-----------|
| Enumeration | <span class="var">[X × 10ⁿ CFU/g]</span> |
| Below detection | <span class="var">[<10 CFU/g (or limit of method)]</span> |
| Absence test | <span class="var">[Absent in X g]</span> or <span class="var">[Detected in X g]</span> |

---

## 11. OUT-OF-SPECIFICATION RESULTS

1. Do NOT repeat test without investigation
2. Verify sample identity and handling
3. Review QC results and technique
4. If laboratory error confirmed, document and retest
5. If confirmed OOS, report to QA immediately
6. Product remains quarantine pending disposition
