## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure establishes validated methods for the detection and quantification of mycotoxins in cannabis and cannabis-derived products using Liquid Chromatography-Tandem Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) and immunoassay screening methods. The procedure ensures compliance with European Commission regulations, European Pharmacopoeia requirements, and cannabis-specific safety limits to protect consumers from toxic fungal metabolites.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

- **Cannabis flower** (dried inflorescence)
- **Cannabis trim** and leaf material
- **Cannabis extracts** (oils, concentrates, tinctures)
- **Cannabis-infused products**
- **Intermediate materials** during processing
- **Starting materials** (incoming testing)
- **Process environment** samples

### 2.1 Target Mycotoxins

| Mycotoxin Class | Specific Compounds | Producing Organisms |
|-----------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Aflatoxins** | B₁, B₂, G₁, G₂ | *Aspergillus flavus*, *A. parasiticus* |
| **Ochratoxin A** | OTA | *Aspergillus*, *Penicillium* spp. |

## 6. Sample Preparation

### 6.1 Sample Receipt and Storage

1. **Receive** sample with completed chain of custody form
2. **Verify** sample identification matches documentation
3. **Inspect** for visible mold growth (record observations)
4. **Store** at <span class="var">[-20°C]</span> until analysis
5. **Maximum hold time**: <span class="var">[60 days]</span> from receipt
6. **Protect** from light (aflatoxins are photosensitive)

### 6.2 Sample Homogenization

**Dried Cannabis Flower:**

1. **Use** clean, dedicated laboratory mill
2. **Grind** at <span class="var">[15,000 rpm]</span> for <span class="var">[30 seconds]</span>
3. **Target particle size**: <span class="var">[<1 mm]</span>
4. **Clean** grinder between samples:
   - Brush residue
   - Wipe with 70% ethanol
   - Run blank grinding
5. **Mix** thoroughly for homogeneity
6. **Store** in amber glass at <span class="var">[-20°C]</span>

**Cannabis Extracts/Oils:**

1. **Warm** viscous samples to <span class="var">[40°C]</span>
2. **Mix** thoroughly by vortexing
3. **Sample directly** without grinding

### 6.3 Extraction Procedure - QuEChERS Modified

**Step 1: Weigh Sample**

| Matrix | Sample Weight |
|--------|--------------|
| Dried flower | <span class="var">[2.0 g ± 0.1 g]</span> |
| Fresh plant | <span class="var">[5.0 g ± 0.2 g]</span> |
| Extracts/oils | <span class="var">[1.0 g ± 0.05 g]</span> |

**Step 2: Internal Standard Addition**

1. Add <span class="var">[100 µL]</span> IS working solution
2. Vortex briefly to distribute

**Step 3: Hydration (dried samples)**

1. Add <span class="var">[10 mL]</span> ultrapure water
2. Vortex <span class="var">[30 seconds]</span>
3. Let stand <span class="var">[15 minutes]</span>

**Step 4: Extraction**

1. Add <span class="var">[10 mL]</span> extraction solvent:
   - **Option A**: <span class="var">[ACN:H₂O:Acetic Acid (79:20:1)]</span>
   - **Option B**: <span class="var">[ACN:H₂O:Formic Acid (80:19:1)]</span>
2. Shake vigorously <span class="var">[10 minutes]</span> on orbital shaker
3. Add QuEChERS extraction salts:
   - <span class="var">[4 g]</span> MgSO₄ (anhydrous)
   - <span class="var">[1 g]</span> NaCl
4. Shake immediately <span class="var">[1 minute]</span>
5. Centrifuge at <span class="var">[4000 rpm]</span> for <span class="var">[10 minutes]</span>

**Step 5: Cleanup (Option A - MycoSep)**

1. Transfer <span class="var">[8 mL]</span> supernatant to MycoSep 226 column
2. Push through column at steady rate
3. Collect purified extract
4. Proceed to concentration

**Step 5: Cleanup (Option B - dSPE)**

1. Transfer <span class="var">[6 mL]</span> supernatant to dSPE tube containing:
   - <span class="var">[400 mg]</span> C18
   - <span class="var">[150 mg]</span> PSA
   - <span class="var">[900 mg]</span> MgSO₄
2. Vortex <span class="var">[30 seconds]</span>
3. Centrifuge <span class="var">[5 minutes]</span> at <span class="var">[4000 rpm]</span>
4. Transfer supernatant

**Step 6: Concentration**

1. Transfer <span class="var">[4 mL]</span> cleaned extract to glass tube
2. Evaporate under nitrogen at <span class="var">[40°C]</span> to <span class="var">[~0.5 mL]</span>
3. Reconstitute in <span class="var">[1 mL]</span> mobile phase A:B (50:50)
4. Vortex <span class="var">[30 seconds]</span>
5. Filter through <span class="var">[0.22 µm]</span> PTFE
6. Transfer to amber autosampler vial

### 6.4 Immunoaffinity Column Cleanup (Aflatoxins - Reference Method)

**For Regulatory Compliance Testing per Ph. Eur. 2.8.18:**

**Step 1: Extraction**

1. Weigh <span class="var">[25 g]</span> homogenized sample
2. Add <span class="var">[5 g]</span> NaCl
3. Add <span class="var">[125 mL]</span> MeOH:H₂O (80:20)
4. Blend at high speed <span class="var">[2 minutes]</span>
5. Filter through fluted filter paper

**Step 2: Dilution**

1. Dilute <span class="var">[15 mL]</span> filtrate with <span class="var">[30 mL]</span> PBS
2. Filter through glass microfiber filter

**Step 3: IAC Cleanup**

1. Condition AflaTest® column with <span class="var">[10 mL]</span> PBS
2. Load <span class="var">[20 mL]</span> diluted extract at <span class="var">[1-2 drops/second]</span>
3. Wash with <span class="var">[20 mL]</span> water at <span class="var">[2 mL/min]</span>
4. Air dry column <span class="var">[1 minute]</span>
5. Elute with <span class="var">[1.5 mL]</span> methanol at <span class="var">[1 drop/second]</span>
6. Collect in amber vial

**Step 4: Final Preparation**

1. Evaporate to dryness under nitrogen at <span class="var">[40°C]</span>
2. Reconstitute in <span class="var">[500 µL]</span> mobile phase
3. Vortex and transfer to autosampler vial

## 8. ELISA Screening Method

### 8.1 Purpose of ELISA Screening

- Rapid screening for presumptive positive/negative
- Cost-effective batch release screening
- Confirmation by LC-MS/MS for positives or near-limit results

### 8.2 ELISA Procedure (Aflatoxin Total)

**Kit**: <span class="var">[Validated commercial ELISA kit for Total Aflatoxins]</span>

**Sample Preparation:**

1. Weigh <span class="var">[5 g]</span> homogenized sample
2. Add <span class="var">[25 mL]</span> MeOH:H₂O (70:30)
3. Shake <span class="var">[3 minutes]</span>
4. Filter through Whatman No. 1
5. Dilute filtrate as per kit instructions

**ELISA Procedure:**

1. Bring all reagents to <span class="var">[room temperature]</span>
2. Prepare standards according to kit protocol
3. Add <span class="var">[50 µL]</span> standard/sample to wells
4. Add <span class="var">[50 µL]</span> conjugate to all wells
5. Add <span class="var">[50 µL]</span> antibody to all wells
6. Mix plate gently <span class="var">[30 seconds]</span>
7. Incubate at <span class="var">[20-25°C]</span> for <span class="var">[15-30 minutes]</span> (per kit)
8. Wash wells <span class="var">[3 times]</span> with wash buffer
9. Add <span class="var">[100 µL]</span> substrate to all wells
10. Incubate <span class="var">[5-15 minutes]</span> in dark
11. Add <span class="var">[100 µL]</span> stop solution
12. Read at <span class="var">[450 nm]</span> within <span class="var">[15 minutes]</span>

**Data Analysis:**

1. Calculate %B/B₀ for each well
2. Plot standard curve (logit-log or 4-parameter)
3. Determine sample concentrations from curve
4. Apply dilution factors

**ELISA Interpretation:**

| Result | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **< 50% of limit** | Pass - no confirmation needed |
| **50-150% of limit** | Confirm by LC-MS/MS |
| **> 150% of limit** | Presumptive Fail - confirm by LC-MS/MS |

## 10. Data Analysis and Reporting

### 10.1 Identification Criteria

| Criterion | LC-MS/MS Specification |
|-----------|------------------------|
| **Retention Time** | <span class="var">[±0.1 min]</span> vs. standard |
| **Qualifier/Quantifier Ratio** | <span class="var">[±30%]</span> of standard |
| **Signal/Noise** | <span class="var">[≥3:1]</span> for identification |
| **Minimum 2 MRM Transitions** | Both present |

### 10.2 Quantification

**Internal Standard Method:**

$$C_{sample} = \frac{(Response_{analyte}/Response_{IS})_{sample}}{(Response_{analyte}/Response_{IS})_{standard}} × C_{standard} × \frac{V_{final}}{W_{sample}} × DF$$

Where:
- C = Concentration (µg/kg)
- V_final = Final extract volume (mL)
- W_sample = Sample weight (g)
- DF = Dilution factor

## 11. Out-of-Specification Procedures

### 11.1 OOS Investigation

**Phase 1 - Immediate Actions:**

1. **Stop** batch processing
2. **Quarantine** affected material
3. **Review** QC data (blanks, spikes, IS recovery)
4. **Check** for cross-contamination

**Phase 2 - Laboratory Investigation:**

1. Re-extract from retained sample
2. Confirm by alternative method (IAC cleanup + LC-MS/MS)
3. Verify against reference material
4. Check standard integrity

**Phase 3 - Extended Investigation:**

1. Sample re-collection if homogeneity suspect
2. Environmental investigation (storage conditions)
3. Method validation review

### 11.2 OOS Documentation

Complete Mycotoxin OOS Investigation Form (FM-QC-037) including:
- Original and confirmation results
- QC data review
- Root cause determination
- Corrective actions
- Batch impact assessment

### 11.3 OOS Notification

| Mycotoxin | Notification Timeline |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Aflatoxins** (carcinogens) | Immediate verbal + same-day written |
| **Other mycotoxins** | Within 24 hours |

## 14. Safety Considerations

### 14.1 Mycotoxin Hazards

| Mycotoxin | Primary Hazard | IARC Classification |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| **Aflatoxin B₁** | Carcinogen | Group 1 (Carcinogenic to humans) |
| **Ochratoxin A** | Nephrotoxin, possible carcinogen | Group 2B |
| **Fumonisins** | Possible carcinogen | Group 2B |
| **Others** | Various toxicity | Monitor literature |

### 14.2 Handling Precautions

1. **Work in designated area** for mycotoxin handling
2. **Use fume hood** for all standard preparation
3. **Double-glove** when handling concentrated standards
4. **Avoid creating aerosols or dust**
5. **Decontaminate** spills immediately with 5% sodium hypochlorite
6. **Never** eat, drink, or store food in mycotoxin work areas

### 14.3 Required PPE

- Laboratory coat (disposable recommended)
- Safety glasses
- Nitrile gloves (double-gloving for standards)
- Face mask (N95 when grinding samples)

### 14.4 Waste Disposal

| Waste Type | Disposal Method |
|------------|----------------|
| **Mycotoxin standards** | Decontaminate with 5% NaOCl, hazardous waste |
| **Contaminated consumables** | Autoclave or chemical decontamination |
| **Solvent waste** | Halogenated or non-halogenated collection |
| **ELISA waste** | Chemical waste stream |
