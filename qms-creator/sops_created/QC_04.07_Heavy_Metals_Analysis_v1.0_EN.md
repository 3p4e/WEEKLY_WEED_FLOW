## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure establishes validated methods for the determination of elemental impurities (heavy metals) in cannabis and cannabis-derived products using Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry (ICP-MS) and Inductively Coupled Plasma Optical Emission Spectroscopy (ICP-OES). The procedure ensures compliance with ICH Q3D guidelines, European Pharmacopoeia requirements, and specific cannabis regulatory limits.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

- **Cannabis flower** (dried inflorescence)
- **Cannabis extracts** (oils, concentrates, tinctures)
- **Cannabis-infused products**
- **Intermediate materials**
- **Starting materials** (incoming testing)
- **Process water** and rinse samples
- **Packaging materials** (extractable metals)

## 6. Sample Preparation

### 6.1 Sample Receipt and Storage

1. **Receive** sample with chain of custody documentation
2. **Verify** sample ID, weight, and integrity
3. **Store** at <span class="var">[room temperature]</span> for dried material
4. **Store** at <span class="var">[2-8°C]</span> for liquid samples
5. **Maximum hold time**: <span class="var">[6 months]</span> for dried, <span class="var">[30 days]</span> for liquids

### 6.2 Sample Homogenization

**Dried Cannabis Flower:**

1. **Use** ceramic or agate mortar/pestle (avoid metal contamination)
2. **Grind** to <span class="var">[<0.5 mm]</span> particle size
3. **Alternatively**: Use cryogenic grinding with liquid nitrogen
4. **Mix** thoroughly to ensure homogeneity
5. **Store** in acid-washed glass container

**Cannabis Extracts/Oils:**

1. **Warm** viscous samples to <span class="var">[40°C]</span>
2. **Mix** thoroughly by gentle stirring
3. **Sample directly** - no grinding required

### 6.3 Microwave-Assisted Acid Digestion

**Equipment Setup:**

| Parameter | Setting |
|-----------|---------|
| **Vessel Type** | PTFE or PFA, <span class="var">[100 mL]</span> capacity |
| **Maximum Pressure** | <span class="var">[40 bar]</span> |
| **Maximum Temperature** | <span class="var">[220°C]</span> |
| **Power** | <span class="var">[1500 W]</span> maximum |

**Digestion Procedure:**

**Step 1: Sample Weighing**

| Matrix | Sample Weight |
|--------|--------------|
| Dried flower | <span class="var">[0.5 g ± 0.01 g]</span> |
| Extracts/oils | <span class="var">[0.3 g ± 0.01 g]</span> |
| Aqueous samples | <span class="var">[10 mL]</span> |

**Step 2: Acid Addition**

1. **Add** <span class="var">[7 mL]</span> concentrated HNO₃ (trace metal grade)
2. **Add** <span class="var">[1 mL]</span> concentrated HCl (trace metal grade)
3. **Add** <span class="var">[1 mL]</span> H₂O₂ (30%)
4. **Allow** to stand <span class="var">[15 minutes]</span> (pre-digestion)
5. **Cap** vessels loosely, then seal

**Step 3: Microwave Program**

| Stage | Temperature (°C) | Ramp (min) | Hold (min) | Power |
|-------|------------------|------------|------------|-------|
| 1 | <span class="var">[120]</span> | <span class="var">[5]</span> | <span class="var">[5]</span> | <span class="var">[800 W]</span> |
| 2 | <span class="var">[160]</span> | <span class="var">[5]</span> | <span class="var">[5]</span> | <span class="var">[1000 W]</span> |
| 3 | <span class="var">[200]</span> | <span class="var">[5]</span> | <span class="var">[15]</span> | <span class="var">[1200 W]</span> |
| Cool | <span class="var">[50]</span> | - | <span class="var">[20]</span> | <span class="var">[0 W]</span> |

**Step 4: Post-Digestion**

1. **Allow** vessels to cool to <span class="var">[<50°C]</span>
2. **Open** vessels carefully in fume hood
3. **Verify** complete digestion (clear solution, no particles)
4. **If incomplete**: Add 1 mL H₂O₂, repeat step 3

**Step 5: Dilution**

1. **Transfer** digest quantitatively to volumetric flask
2. **Rinse** vessel with <span class="var">[3 × 5 mL]</span> ultrapure water
3. **Dilute** to <span class="var">[50 mL]</span> with ultrapure water
4. **Final acid concentration**: Approximately <span class="var">[2% HNO₃]</span>
5. **Add** internal standard to final concentration: <span class="var">[10 µg/L]</span>

### 6.4 Alternative: Hot Block Digestion

For laboratories without microwave digestion:

1. **Weigh** sample into PTFE digestion tube
2. **Add** <span class="var">[5 mL]</span> HNO₃ + <span class="var">[1 mL]</span> HCl
3. **Heat** at <span class="var">[95°C]</span> for <span class="var">[2 hours]</span> with watch glass cover
4. **Add** <span class="var">[1 mL]</span> H₂O₂, continue heating <span class="var">[1 hour]</span>
5. **Cool** and dilute as above

**Note**: Hot block method has lower digestion efficiency; validate for specific matrices.

## 9. Data Analysis and Reporting

### 9.1 Calculations

**Concentration in Sample:**

$$C_{sample} = \frac{(C_{measured} - C_{blank}) × V_{final} × D}{W_{sample}}$$

Where:
- C_sample = Concentration in sample (µg/g or mg/kg)
- C_measured = Measured concentration (µg/L)
- C_blank = Method blank concentration (µg/L)
- V_final = Final digest volume (L)
- D = Any additional dilution factor
- W_sample = Sample weight (g)

**Internal Standard Correction:**

$$C_{corrected} = C_{measured} × \frac{IS_{expected}}{IS_{measured}}$$

### 9.2 Detection and Quantification Limits

| Parameter | Definition | Calculation |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| **IDL** (Instrument Detection Limit) | 3σ of blank | <span class="var">[7 replicates × 3.14 × SD]</span> |
| **MDL** (Method Detection Limit) | 3σ through method | <span class="var">[7 spikes × t(n-1,0.99) × SD]</span> |
| **LOQ** (Limit of Quantification) | 10σ or lowest validated | <span class="var">[LOQ = 3.3 × MDL]</span> |
| **Reporting Limit** | ≥ LOQ | Per element |

## 10. Interferences and Corrections

### 10.1 Spectral Interferences (ICP-MS)

| Element | Mass | Interference | Correction |
|---------|------|--------------|------------|
| **As (75)** | 75 | ⁴⁰Ar³⁵Cl⁺ | He collision mode |
| **Se (78)** | 78 | ⁴⁰Ar³⁸Ar⁺ | He collision, use 82Se |
| **Cr (52)** | 52 | ⁴⁰Ar¹²C⁺, ³⁵Cl¹⁶OH⁺ | He collision mode |
| **V (51)** | 51 | ³⁵Cl¹⁶O⁺ | He collision mode |
| **Fe (56)** | 56 | ⁴⁰Ar¹⁶O⁺ | He collision mode |
| **Cu (63)** | 63 | ⁴⁰Ar²³Na⁺ | He collision mode |

### 10.2 Non-Spectral Interferences

| Interference Type | Cause | Mitigation |
|------------------|-------|------------|
| **Matrix suppression** | High dissolved solids | Internal standards, matrix matching |
| **Memory effects** | Hg, Au, B | Extended rinse times, gold in rinse |
| **Space charge** | High mass, high concentration | Internal standard correction |
| **Carbon enhancement** | Organic carbon | Match organic content |

### 10.3 Mercury-Specific Considerations

1. **Memory effect**: Use <span class="var">[5% Au in 10% HCl]</span> in rinse solution
2. **Volatility**: Add <span class="var">[0.5 mL]</span> BrCl oxidizer post-digestion
3. **Stability**: Analyze samples within <span class="var">[48 hours]</span> of digestion
4. **Rinse time**: <span class="var">[≥60 seconds]</span> between samples

## 11. Out-of-Specification Procedures

### 11.1 OOS Investigation

**Phase 1 - Immediate Actions:**

1. **Stop** batch processing
2. **Review** QC data (blanks, spikes, IS recovery)
3. **Check** for contamination sources
4. **Verify** calculations and dilutions

**Phase 2 - Laboratory Investigation:**

1. Re-digest from retained sample
2. Analyze with enhanced QC
3. Verify against second method (ICP-OES or alternative mass)
4. Check SRM recovery

**Phase 3 - Extended Investigation:**

1. Sample re-collection if homogeneity suspect
2. Full method validation review
3. Instrument qualification verification

### 11.2 OOS Documentation

Complete Heavy Metals OOS Investigation Form (FM-QC-031) including:
- Original and retest data
- QC data review
- Root cause analysis
- Corrective actions
- Impact assessment

### 11.3 OOS Notification

| Element | Notification Timeline |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Pb, As, Cd, Hg** (Class 1) | Immediate verbal + same-day written |
| **Other elements** | Within 24 hours |
