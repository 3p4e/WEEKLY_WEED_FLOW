## Observation Codes and Actions

### Internal RH Guidelines

| RH Reading | Interpretation | Action Required |
|------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **>70%** | Too wet - risk of mold | Leave open 30-60 min, increase frequency |
| **65-70%** | Slightly elevated | Extended burping, monitor closely |
| **60-65%** | Target range (early cure) | Normal procedure |
| **55-60%** | Target range (late cure) | Normal procedure |
| **<55%** | Getting dry | Reduce burping frequency, check seal |
| **<50%** | Too dry | Add humidity pack, seal immediately |
