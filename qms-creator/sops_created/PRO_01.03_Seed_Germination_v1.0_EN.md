## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 This SOP establishes procedures for the controlled germination of cannabis seeds to establish new genetics, create new mother plants, or for breeding programs at <span class="var">[FACILITY_NAME]</span>.

1.2 Objectives:
- Achieve consistent germination rates (<span class="var">[≥85%]</span> for viable seed)
- Maintain genetic traceability from seed to plant
- Ensure compliance with applicable regulations
- Establish healthy seedlings for phenotype selection

---

## 2. SCOPE

2.1 Applies to:
- Seed receipt and documentation
- Seed storage conditions
- Germination procedures
- Seedling care until transplant
- Phenotype selection activities

2.2 Does **not** apply to:
- Vegetative cloning (see <span class="var">[PRO_01.02]</span>)
- Mother plant management (see <span class="var">[PRO_01.01]</span>)

## 4. DEFINITIONS

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Viable Seed** | Seed capable of germinating under appropriate conditions |
| **Scarification** | Process of weakening seed coat to promote germination |
| **Radicle** | First root emerging from germinating seed |
| **Cotyledon** | First embryonic leaves emerging from seed |
| **Hypocotyl** | Stem of germinating seedling below cotyledons |
| **Phenotype** | Observable characteristics of individual plant |

## 7. PROCEDURE

### 7.1 Seed Receipt and Documentation

1. Verify seed lot documentation:
   - Certificate of origin/authenticity
   - Cultivar/strain identification
   - Seed count and lot number
   - Viability test results (if available)

2. Record in Seed Inventory Log <span class="var">[FM-SEED-001]</span>:
   - Receipt date
   - Supplier information
   - Lot number
   - Quantity received
   - Storage location assigned

3. Obtain authorization for germination from Cultivation Manager

### 7.2 Seed Storage

Prior to germination, store seeds in:
- Temperature: <span class="var">[4-8°C]</span> (refrigerated)
- Humidity: <span class="var">[20-30% RH]</span> (desiccant in container)
- Container: <span class="var">[Airtight, light-proof]</span>
- Maximum storage: <span class="var">[24 months from receipt]</span>

### 7.3 Germination Procedure

#### 7.3.1 Paper Towel Method (Primary)

**Day 0 - Setup:**

1. Initiate Germination Batch Record <span class="var">[FM-SEED-002]</span>
2. Prepare sterile work surface
3. Pre-soak seeds (optional, for older seeds):
   - <span class="var">[12-24 hours]</span> in room temperature water
   - Add <span class="var">[1 drop H₂O₂ (3%)]</span> per <span class="var">[100 mL water]</span> (optional scarification)

4. Prepare germination setup:
   - Fold paper towels to fit container
   - Moisten with pH-adjusted water (<span class="var">[pH 6.0-6.5]</span>)
   - Paper should be damp, not dripping

5. Place seeds:
   - Space <span class="var">[2-3 cm]</span> apart
   - Cover with second moistened paper towel
   - Place in petri dish or covered container

6. Label with:
   - Batch number
   - Cultivar
   - Date
   - Quantity

7. Place in dark, warm environment:
   - Temperature: <span class="var">[25°C]</span>
   - Use heating mat if needed

**Day 1-3 - Monitoring:**

| Day | Action | Expected Development |
|-----|--------|---------------------|
| 1 | Check moisture, maintain damp | Seeds swelling |
| 2 | Check moisture, look for taproots | Some taproots emerging |
| 3 | Check for <span class="var">[1-2 cm]</span> taproot | Most viable seeds sprouted |

**Transplant when:**
- Taproot reaches <span class="var">[1-2 cm]</span>
- Handle carefully by seed shell only
- Plant taproot down, <span class="var">[1 cm]</span> deep

#### 7.3.2 Direct Sow Method (Alternative)

1. Prepare starter cubes:
   - Pre-soak rockwool or peat pellet
   - pH adjust soak water: <span class="var">[5.5-5.8]</span>

2. Create small hole: <span class="var">[0.5-1 cm]</span> depth

3. Place seed:
   - Pointed end down (if identifiable)
   - Cover lightly with medium

4. Place under dome:
   - Maintain <span class="var">[85-95% humidity]</span>
   - Temperature: <span class="var">[25°C]</span>
   - Dark until emergence, then provide light

5. Expected emergence: <span class="var">[3-7 days]</span>

### 7.4 Seedling Care

#### 7.4.1 Post-Emergence Care (Day 3-14)

**First True Leaves Stage:**

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Light | <span class="var">[18 hours at 150-200 PPFD]</span> |
| Temperature | <span class="var">[22-26°C]</span> |
| Humidity | <span class="var">[65-75%]</span> (gradually reduce) |
| Watering | <span class="var">[Light, as needed when surface dry]</span> |
| Nutrition | <span class="var">[None until 2nd set true leaves]</span> |

**Daily Monitoring:**
- Check for stretching (indicates insufficient light)
- Monitor for damping off (fungal disease)
- Ensure adequate air circulation
- Record observations in Seedling Log <span class="var">[FM-SEED-003]</span>

#### 7.4.2 Early Vegetative (Day 14-28)

1. Transplant to larger container when roots fill starter:
   - Container: <span class="var">[0.5-1 L pot]</span>
   - Medium: <span class="var">[Same as vegetative production]</span>

2. Begin light nutrition:
   - <span class="var">[1/4 strength vegetative nutrients]</span>
   - EC: <span class="var">[0.6-0.8 mS/cm]</span>
   - pH: <span class="var">[5.8-6.2]</span>

3. Increase light gradually:
   - Target: <span class="var">[250-300 PPFD]</span>

4. Monitor for:
   - Growth vigor
   - Leaf morphology (phenotype indicators)
   - Any abnormalities

### 7.5 Phenotype Selection

#### 7.5.1 Selection Criteria

For breeding or mother selection, evaluate:

| Criterion | Desirable Traits |
|-----------|------------------|
| Growth rate | Vigorous, consistent |
| Structure | Strong stems, good branching |
| Leaf health | No deficiencies or abnormalities |
| Pest resistance | No susceptibility indicators |
| Uniformity | Consistent with cultivar expectations |

#### 7.5.2 Selection Process

1. Grow seedlings to <span class="var">[4-6 weeks]</span> vegetative
2. Document characteristics in Phenotype Evaluation Form <span class="var">[FM-SEED-004]</span>
3. Take clones from promising individuals
4. Flower original seedlings for full evaluation (if breeding)
5. Select based on complete evaluation
6. Qualified selections → Mother Plant qualification per <span class="var">[PRO_01.01]</span>
