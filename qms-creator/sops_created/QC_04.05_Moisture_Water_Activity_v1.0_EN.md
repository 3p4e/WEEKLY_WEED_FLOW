## 1. PURPOSE

This SOP establishes procedures for determining moisture content and water activity in cannabis flower and cannabis products at <span class="var">[FACILITY_NAME]</span> to ensure product quality, stability, and compliance with specifications.

---

## 2. SCOPE

Applies to:
- Loss on Drying (LOD) determination
- Karl Fischer titration (if applicable)
- Water activity measurement
- In-process monitoring during drying/curing
- Finished product testing

---

## 3. BACKGROUND

### 3.1 Importance of Moisture Control

| Parameter | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| Too high moisture | Microbial growth, mold, degradation |
| Too low moisture | Product degradation, loss of terpenes, brittleness |
| Optimal moisture | Product stability, quality preservation |

### 3.2 Specifications for Cannabis Flower

| Parameter | Specification | Reference |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Moisture Content (LOD) | <span class="var">[8-12% w/w]</span> | Internal specification |
| Water Activity | <span class="var">[0.55-0.65 aw]</span> | Internal specification |
| Ph. Eur. LOD limit | <span class="var">[≤10%]</span> (herbal drugs) | Ph. Eur. 2.8.17 |
| Mold prevention threshold | <span class="var">[<0.65 aw]</span> | Literature |

## 4. METHOD A: LOSS ON DRYING (LOD)

### 4.1 Principle

Moisture content is determined gravimetrically by measuring weight loss upon heating the sample under defined conditions. Water and other volatile substances are evaporated.

### 4.2 Equipment

| Equipment | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Drying oven | <span class="var">[Forced air circulation, 100-110°C ±2°C]</span> |
| Moisture analyzer (alternative) | <span class="var">[Halogen or infrared, ±0.01% readability]</span> |
| Analytical balance | <span class="var">[0.1 mg readability, calibrated]</span> |
| Weighing dishes | <span class="var">[Glass or aluminum, pre-dried]</span> |
| Desiccator | <span class="var">[With active desiccant]</span> |
| Tongs/forceps | <span class="var">[For handling hot dishes]</span> |

### 4.3 Procedure - Oven Method (Ph. Eur. 2.2.32)

#### 4.3.1 Preparation

1. Pre-dry weighing dishes:
   - Heat at <span class="var">[105°C for 1 hour]</span>
   - Cool in desiccator to room temperature
   - Weigh immediately (W₀)

2. Grind sample to homogeneous powder (<span class="var">[0.5-1.0 mm particles]</span>)

#### 4.3.2 Analysis

1. Weigh accurately <span class="var">[1.000-2.000 g]</span> sample into pre-dried dish
2. Record weight of dish + sample (W₁)
3. Place dish (uncovered) in oven at <span class="var">[105°C]</span>
4. Dry for <span class="var">[2 hours]</span>
5. Remove, cover, cool in desiccator (<span class="var">[30-45 minutes]</span>)
6. Weigh (W₂)
7. Return to oven for <span class="var">[1 hour]</span>
8. Cool and reweigh
9. Repeat until constant weight achieved:
   - Successive weights differ by <span class="var">[≤0.5 mg]</span> or <span class="var">[≤0.05%]</span>

#### 4.3.3 Calculation

$$LOD\ (\%) = \frac{(W_1 - W_2)}{(W_1 - W_0)} × 100$$

Where:
- W₀ = Weight of empty dish (g)
- W₁ = Weight of dish + sample before drying (g)
- W₂ = Weight of dish + sample after drying (g)

### 4.4 Procedure - Moisture Analyzer (Rapid Method)

#### 4.4.1 Instrument Setup

1. Power on analyzer, allow warm-up (<span class="var">[15 minutes]</span>)
2. Verify calibration with check weight
3. Set parameters:
   - Temperature: <span class="var">[105°C]</span> (or <span class="var">[103°C]</span> for standard method correlation)
   - End point: <span class="var">[Automatic (≤0.05%/minute change)]</span> or <span class="var">[timed 15 minutes]</span>
   - Display: % moisture

#### 4.4.2 Analysis

1. Tare sample pan
2. Add <span class="var">[2.0-3.0 g]</span> ground sample
3. Spread evenly across pan
4. Close lid, start analysis
5. Record result when end point reached

#### 4.4.3 Notes

- Rapid method for routine/in-process testing
- Correlate with oven method during validation
- Document any correlation factor applied

### 4.5 Acceptance Criteria

| Criterion | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Duplicate agreement | <span class="var">[RSD ≤5%]</span> or <span class="var">[difference ≤0.5%]</span> |
| Constant weight | <span class="var">[≤0.05% change]</span> between weighings |
| Product specification | <span class="var">[8-12% moisture]</span> |

## 5. METHOD B: WATER ACTIVITY (aw)

### 5.1 Principle

Water activity is the ratio of vapor pressure of water in a sample to the vapor pressure of pure water at the same temperature. It indicates the amount of water available for microbial growth and chemical reactions.

$$a_w = \frac{p}{p_0}$$

Where p = vapor pressure of water in sample, p₀ = vapor pressure of pure water

### 5.2 Equipment

| Equipment | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Water activity meter | <span class="var">[Decagon AquaLab, Rotronic, or equivalent]</span> |
| Accuracy | <span class="var">[±0.003 aw]</span> |
| Sample cups | <span class="var">[Manufacturer-specific, disposable or cleaned]</span> |
| Calibration standards | <span class="var">[Saturated salt solutions, certified aw values]</span> |

### 5.3 Calibration Standards

| Salt Solution | aw at 25°C |
|---------------|------------|
| Lithium chloride (LiCl) | <span class="var">[0.113]</span> |
| Magnesium chloride (MgCl₂) | <span class="var">[0.328]</span> |
| Sodium chloride (NaCl) | <span class="var">[0.753]</span> |
| Potassium chloride (KCl) | <span class="var">[0.843]</span> |
| Potassium sulfate (K₂SO₄) | <span class="var">[0.973]</span> |

### 5.4 Procedure

#### 5.4.1 Instrument Calibration

1. Turn on instrument, allow equilibration (<span class="var">[30 minutes]</span>)
2. Verify temperature stability (<span class="var">[25.0 ±0.2°C]</span>)
3. Calibrate with standards:
   - Use <span class="var">[0.328 (MgCl₂)]</span> and <span class="var">[0.753 (NaCl)]</span> or per manufacturer
4. Verify calibration:
   - Measured vs. expected within <span class="var">[±0.003 aw]</span>
5. Document in Calibration Log <span class="var">[FM-CAL-001]</span>

#### 5.4.2 Sample Analysis

1. Prepare sample:
   - Grind to expose surface area
   - If whole flower, break into small pieces
2. Fill sample cup:
   - Cover bottom to <span class="var">[~half full]</span>
   - Do not compress
   - Use consistent fill level
3. Wipe cup exterior clean
4. Place sample cup in measurement chamber
5. Close chamber, initiate measurement
6. Wait for equilibration (typically <span class="var">[5-10 minutes]</span>)
7. Record:
   - Water activity (aw)
   - Temperature (°C)
   - Sample ID
   - Date/time
8. Remove sample, clean chamber if needed

### 5.5 Acceptance Criteria

| Criterion | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Calibration verification | <span class="var">[±0.003 aw]</span> from certified value |
| Duplicate precision | <span class="var">[±0.01 aw]</span> |
| Sample temperature | <span class="var">[25.0 ±1.0°C]</span> |
| Product specification | <span class="var">[0.55-0.65 aw]</span> |

### 5.6 Interpretation

| Water Activity | Interpretation |
|----------------|----------------|
| <span class="var">[<0.60]</span> | No microbial growth possible |
| <span class="var">[0.60-0.65]</span> | Most molds inhibited, some xerophilic molds possible |
| <span class="var">[0.65-0.70]</span> | Mold growth risk, unacceptable for long-term storage |
| <span class="var">[>0.70]</span> | High microbial growth risk |

## 6. IN-PROCESS MONITORING

### 6.1 Drying Phase Monitoring

| Timing | Test | Target |
|--------|------|--------|
| Start of drying | Moisture | <span class="var">[~75-80%]</span> (fresh) |
| Day 3-5 | Moisture | <span class="var">[~30-50%]</span> |
| Day 7-10 | Moisture | <span class="var">[~15-20%]</span> |
| End of drying | Moisture + aw | <span class="var">[10-12%, aw <0.65]</span> |
| After curing | Moisture + aw | <span class="var">[8-12%, aw 0.55-0.65]</span> |

### 6.2 Sampling for In-Process

- Sample from multiple locations in drying room
- Sample from different parts of plant (tip, middle, stem)
- Use rapid moisture analyzer for quick decisions
- Confirm with aw measurement before transfer

## 8. TROUBLESHOOTING

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Erratic LOD results | Sample not homogeneous | Improve grinding/mixing |
| LOD too variable | Inconsistent sample size | Use consistent weight |
| aw reading unstable | Temperature not equilibrated | Allow longer equilibration |
| aw reading high | Sample too fresh | Allow sample to equilibrate to room conditions |
| Constant weight not achieved | Decomposition occurring | Use lower temperature, shorter time |
| Moisture analyzer differs from oven | Different endpoints | Correlate methods, apply factor |
