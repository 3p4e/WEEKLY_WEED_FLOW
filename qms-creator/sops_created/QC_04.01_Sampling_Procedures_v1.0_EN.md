## 1. PURPOSE

This SOP establishes standardized procedures for sampling of cannabis raw materials, in-process materials, and finished products at <span class="var">[FACILITY_NAME]</span> to ensure representative samples are obtained for quality testing in compliance with EU GMP requirements.

---

## 2. SCOPE

Applies to:
- Incoming raw material sampling
- In-process sampling during production
- Finished product sampling
- Stability sampling
- Retention sample collection
- Environmental sampling
- Water sampling

---

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| QC Manager | Sampling plan approval, procedure oversight |

| QA | Sampling plan review, compliance verification |

---

## 4. DEFINITIONS

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Representative Sample** | A sample that accurately reflects the composition of the entire batch |
| **Composite Sample** | A sample formed by combining multiple sub-samples |
| **Aliquot** | A measured portion of a sample |
| **Sampling Plan** | Document specifying sample size, locations, and frequency |
| **Chain of Custody** | Documented transfer of sample possession |
| **Quarantine** | Status of material awaiting testing/disposition |

---

## 5. GENERAL SAMPLING PRINCIPLES

### 5.1 Core Requirements

1. Samples must be **representative** of the batch
2. Sampling must not compromise product quality or contaminate the batch
3. Sampler must be trained and qualified
4. All sampling must be documented contemporaneously
5. Chain of custody must be maintained
6. Aseptic technique required for microbial samples

### 5.2 Sample Integrity

| Requirement | Standard |
|-------------|----------|
| Container cleanliness | Sterile/clean as appropriate |
| Container material | Non-reactive, appropriate for sample type |
| Labeling | Complete, indelible, secure |
| Storage | Appropriate conditions immediately after collection |
| Transport | Controlled conditions, timely delivery |

## 7. SAMPLING PROCEDURES BY MATERIAL TYPE

### 7.1 Cannabis Flower (Dried) - Finished Product

#### 7.1.1 Sampling Plan

| Batch Size | Number of Units to Sample | Sample Quantity per Unit |
|------------|---------------------------|--------------------------|
| <span class="var">[≤10 kg]</span> | <span class="var">[All containers]</span> | <span class="var">[5 g from each]</span> |
| <span class="var">[10-50 kg]</span> | <span class="var">[√n + 1 containers]</span> | <span class="var">[5 g from each]</span> |
| <span class="var">[>50 kg]</span> | <span class="var">[√n + 1 containers (min 10)]</span> | <span class="var">[5 g from each]</span> |

**Total Composite Sample Required:** <span class="var">[Minimum 50 g]</span>

#### 7.1.2 Sampling Locations

For each container selected:
- Top layer: <span class="var">[1 sub-sample]</span>
- Middle layer: <span class="var">[1 sub-sample]</span>
- Bottom layer: <span class="var">[1 sub-sample]</span>

Combine sub-samples to form composite for that container.

#### 7.1.3 Procedure

1. Don appropriate PPE (gown, gloves, hair cover)
2. Verify batch identification matches documentation
3. Record environmental conditions
4. Open container in clean area
5. Using sterile sampling tool:
   - Collect sub-samples from designated locations
   - Avoid crushing or damaging material
   - Select representative material (not just large buds or shake)
6. Combine sub-samples in clean container
7. Weigh sample and record
8. Transfer to labeled sample containers:
   - Testing sample: <span class="var">[30 g minimum]</span>
   - Retention sample: <span class="var">[20 g minimum]</span>
9. Seal containers
10. Complete Sample Collection Form <span class="var">[FM-QC-001]</span>
11. Transport to laboratory

#### 7.1.4 Sample Allocation

| Test | Approximate Quantity |
|------|---------------------|
| Identity (macroscopic/microscopic) | <span class="var">[2 g]</span> |
| Cannabinoid content (HPLC) | <span class="var">[1 g]</span> |
| Terpene profile (GC) | <span class="var">[1 g]</span> |
| Loss on drying/moisture | <span class="var">[2 g]</span> |
| Water activity | <span class="var">[3 g]</span> |
| Microbial testing | <span class="var">[10 g]</span> |
| Pesticide residues | <span class="var">[5 g]</span> |
| Heavy metals | <span class="var">[2 g]</span> |
| Mycotoxins | <span class="var">[5 g]</span> |
| Foreign matter | <span class="var">[5 g]</span> |
| **Total testing** | <span class="var">[~36 g]</span> |
| Retention sample | <span class="var">[20 g]</span> |
| **Total required** | <span class="var">[~56 g]</span> |

### 7.2 In-Process Sampling - Drying

#### 7.2.1 Sampling Points and Frequency

| Sampling Point | Frequency | Purpose |
|----------------|-----------|---------|
| Start of drying | <span class="var">[Day 0]</span> | Baseline moisture |
| During drying | <span class="var">[Days 3, 7, 10]</span> | Monitor moisture loss |
| End of drying | <span class="var">[When target reached]</span> | Confirm specification |

#### 7.2.2 Sample Quantity

| Test | Quantity |
|------|----------|
| Moisture content | <span class="var">[5 g]</span> |
| Water activity | <span class="var">[3 g]</span> |

#### 7.2.3 Procedure

1. Select representative plants/branches from different locations in drying room
2. Sample from: <span class="var">[minimum 3 different locations]</span>
3. Cut small sample portion (<span class="var">[~2 g per location]</span>)
4. Combine to form composite sample
5. Immediately transfer to sealed container
6. Label with batch ID, date, time, location
7. Transport to lab for immediate testing

### 7.3 In-Process Sampling - Trimming

#### 7.3.1 Sampling Points

| Material | Frequency | Quantity |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Trimmed flower | <span class="var">[Every 2 hours during production]</span> | <span class="var">[10 g composite]</span> |
| End of batch | <span class="var">[At completion]</span> | <span class="var">[50 g composite]</span> |

#### 7.3.2 Procedure

1. Collect samples from output at regular intervals
2. Composite for shift/batch
3. Visual inspection for quality
4. Record sample weight and observations

### 7.5 Environmental Sampling

#### 7.5.1 Air Sampling (Microbial)

| Area | Frequency | Method |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Processing rooms | <span class="var">[Weekly]</span> | Active air sampling (<span class="var">[1000 L]</span>) |
| Packaging area | <span class="var">[Weekly]</span> | Active air sampling |
| QC laboratory | <span class="var">[Weekly]</span> | Active air sampling |
| Critical operations | <span class="var">[During production]</span> | Settle plates (<span class="var">[4-hour exposure]</span>) |

#### 7.5.2 Surface Sampling

| Surface Type | Frequency | Method |
|--------------|-----------|--------|
| Product contact surfaces | <span class="var">[After cleaning, before use]</span> | Contact plates or swabs |
| Non-product contact | <span class="var">[Weekly]</span> | Swabs |
| Equipment | <span class="var">[After cleaning validation]</span> | Swabs |

**Swab Procedure:**
1. Pre-moisten swab with sterile saline
2. Swab <span class="var">[25 cm² area]</span> in horizontal strokes
3. Rotate swab 90°, repeat in vertical strokes
4. Place in transport medium
5. Label and transport to lab within <span class="var">[4 hours]</span>

### 7.6 Water Sampling

#### 7.6.1 Sampling Points and Frequency

| Point | Parameter | Frequency |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Source water inlet | Full analysis | <span class="var">[Quarterly]</span> |
| Post-treatment (RO) | Conductivity, pH, micro | <span class="var">[Weekly]</span> |
| Points of use | Conductivity, micro | <span class="var">[Weekly rotation]</span> |
| Storage tank | Full analysis | <span class="var">[Monthly]</span> |

#### 7.6.2 Procedure

**Chemical Analysis:**
1. Flush outlet for <span class="var">[2-3 minutes]</span>
2. Collect in clean container
3. Fill to top, minimize headspace
4. Cap and label immediately

**Microbial Analysis:**
1. Flush outlet for <span class="var">[2-3 minutes]</span>
2. Flame outlet if accessible (optional)
3. Collect in sterile container with sodium thiosulfate (if chlorinated)
4. Do NOT rinse container
5. Cap and label immediately
6. Transport on ice, test within <span class="var">[6 hours]</span>

## 9. SAMPLE HANDLING AND TRANSPORT

### 9.1 Handling Requirements

| Sample Type | Handling | Time Limit |
|-------------|----------|------------|
| Microbial samples | Refrigerate immediately | <span class="var">[<24 hours to testing]</span> |
| Cannabinoid/terpene | Protect from light and heat | <span class="var">[<72 hours to testing]</span> |
| Moisture samples | Sealed, test immediately | <span class="var">[<4 hours to testing]</span> |
| Pesticide samples | Refrigerate or freeze | <span class="var">[<7 days to testing]</span> |
| Heavy metals | Room temperature | <span class="var">[Stable]</span> |

### 9.2 Transport

1. Place samples in clean transport container
2. Include appropriate temperature control
3. Secure to prevent damage
4. Complete Chain of Custody form <span class="var">[FM-QC-002]</span>
5. Hand-deliver to laboratory or receiving area
6. Obtain receiving signature

---

## 10. SAMPLE RECEIPT AND STORAGE

### 10.1 Laboratory Receipt

Upon receipt in laboratory:

1. Verify sample integrity (container sealed, label intact)
2. Check sample against Chain of Custody
3. Verify storage conditions maintained during transport
4. Log receipt in Sample Receipt Log <span class="var">[FM-QC-003]</span>
5. Assign laboratory sample number
6. Store appropriately pending analysis

### 10.2 Storage Conditions

| Sample Type | Storage Condition | Maximum Hold Time |
|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Routine testing | <span class="var">[15-25°C, protected from light]</span> | <span class="var">[7 days]</span> |
| Microbial testing | <span class="var">[2-8°C]</span> | <span class="var">[24 hours]</span> |
| Long-term/retention | <span class="var">[15-25°C, controlled humidity]</span> | <span class="var">[Product shelf life + 1 year]</span> |
| Reference samples | <span class="var">[Per specification]</span> | <span class="var">[As required]</span> |

## 11. RETENTION SAMPLES

### 11.1 Requirements

Per EU GMP Annex 19:
- Retain samples from each batch of finished product
- Quantity sufficient for <span class="var">[2 full analyses]</span>
- Store in final packaging or simulated packaging
- Retain for <span class="var">[product shelf life + 1 year]</span>

### 11.2 Retention Sample Procedure

1. Collect during batch sampling
2. Package in final market packaging (or equivalent)
3. Label as "RETENTION SAMPLE"
4. Store in dedicated retention sample storage
5. Log in Retention Sample Register <span class="var">[FM-QC-004]</span>
6. Review annually for expired samples
7. Dispose of expired samples per procedure
