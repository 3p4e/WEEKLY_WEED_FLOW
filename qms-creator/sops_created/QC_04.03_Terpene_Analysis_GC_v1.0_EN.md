## 1. PURPOSE

This SOP establishes the procedure for qualitative and quantitative determination of terpenes in cannabis flower using Gas Chromatography with Flame Ionization Detection (GC-FID) or Mass Spectrometry detection (GC-MS) at <span class="var">[FACILITY_NAME]</span>.

---

## 2. SCOPE

### 2.1 Applicable Materials
- Cannabis flos (dried flower)
- Cannabis trim
- Cannabis extracts

|---------|-------|---------------|
| α-Pinene | Monoterpene | Pine, forest |

| Limonene | Monoterpene | Citrus, lemon |

## 3. PRINCIPLE

Terpenes are extracted from cannabis using organic solvent, then analyzed by gas chromatography. Volatile terpenes are separated on a capillary column and detected by FID (quantification) or MS (identification/quantification). Quantification is performed against certified reference standards.

## 6. STANDARD PREPARATION

### 6.1 Stock Standard Solution

1. Allow reference standard vials to equilibrate to room temperature
2. Weigh or pipette accurately (volumetric if liquid)
3. Prepare stock at <span class="var">[1000 µg/mL]</span> in methanol
4. Store at <span class="var">[-20°C]</span> in amber vials with minimal headspace
5. Stability: <span class="var">[3 months]</span>

### 6.2 Working Standard Solution

1. Dilute stock to <span class="var">[100 µg/mL]</span> in methanol
2. Store at <span class="var">[2-8°C]</span>
3. Stability: <span class="var">[1 month]</span>

## 7. SAMPLE PREPARATION

### 7.1 Cannabis Flower Extraction

1. Weigh accurately <span class="var">[200.0 ± 2.0 mg]</span> ground, homogenized sample
2. Transfer to <span class="var">[20 mL]</span> headspace vial or extraction tube
3. Add <span class="var">[10.0 mL]</span> methanol
4. Cap immediately (terpenes are volatile!)
5. Vortex <span class="var">[30 seconds]</span>
6. Sonicate <span class="var">[10 minutes]</span> at <span class="var">[room temperature]</span>
7. Centrifuge at <span class="var">[3000 rpm for 5 minutes]</span>
8. Transfer <span class="var">[1.0 mL]</span> supernatant to GC vial
9. Cap immediately
10. Analyze within <span class="var">[24 hours]</span>

**Dilution factor:** <span class="var">[50× (200 mg in 10 mL)]</span>

### 7.2 Alternative Headspace Method (Qualitative)

For rapid screening:
1. Weigh <span class="var">[100 mg]</span> sample into headspace vial
2. Seal immediately
3. Equilibrate at <span class="var">[80°C for 15 minutes]</span>
4. Inject headspace using headspace autosampler

## 10. CALCULATIONS

### 10.1 Calibration

- External standard calibration
- Linear regression: y = mx + b
- Correlation coefficient: <span class="var">[r² ≥ 0.990]</span>

### 10.2 Sample Calculation

$$Terpene\ (\%\ w/w) = \frac{C × V}{W × 10000}$$

Where:
- C = Concentration from calibration (µg/mL)
- V = Extraction volume (mL)
- W = Sample weight (mg)

### 10.3 Total Terpenes

$$Total\ Terpenes\ (\%) = \sum Individual\ Terpenes\ (\%)$$

## 13. TROUBLESHOOTING

| Problem | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Poor peak shape | Column degradation | Trim or replace column |
| Ghost peaks | Carryover | Increase solvent washes |
| Low response | Detector issue | Check FID gases, clean jet |
| RT shift | Carrier gas flow | Check gas supply, verify flow |
| Low recovery | Terpene loss | Work faster, minimize exposure |
| Coelution | Method resolution | Adjust oven program |
