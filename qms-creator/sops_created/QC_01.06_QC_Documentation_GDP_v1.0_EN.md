## 1. PURPOSE

**1.1 This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes the requirements and procedures for the creation, documentation, maintenance, and archival of all Quality Control records and documentation at Purely Plant Medical Cannabis Production Facility.**

**1.2 To ensure that all QC documentation is controlled, accurate, contemporaneous, and compliant with EU GMP Chapter 4 (Documentation) and Chapter 6 (Quality Control) requirements.**

**1.3 To maintain data integrity throughout QC testing activities in accordance with ALCOA+ principles (Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available) and EU Annex 11 (Computerized Systems) where applicable.**

**1.4 To establish Good Documentation Practice (GDP) standards specific to laboratory operations, analytical testing, and quality control functions.**

## 4. DEFINITIONS & KEY PRINCIPLES

### 4.1 ALCOA+ PRINCIPLES

**Attributable:**
- Each record must clearly identify WHO created or modified it
- Date and time of data entry must be recorded
- Electronic signatures required for computer-generated records
- Signature/initials must be legible or have supporting signature block

**Legible:**
- All handwritten entries must be clear and readable
- No use of obscure abbreviations or codes
- Permanent ink only (black or blue ballpoint)
- No correction fluid (use single line strike-through with initials + date)
- Electronic records must use clear fonts and formatting

**Contemporaneous:**
- Data recorded at the time of testing/observation
- NO retroactive data entry or back-dating
- Sequential numbering of pages and entries
- Time stamps included for electronic records
- Must not be completed after testing is finished

**Original:**
- First record (master copy) is the official document
- Copies are derivatives and must indicate "Copy" clearly
- Electronic original maintained with proper version control
- No alteration of original record without approval and documentation
- Audit trail maintained for all electronic modifications

**Accurate:**
- All recorded data must reflect actual observations/results
- Calculations must be verified and correct
- No guessing or estimated values presented as measured
- Units of measurement clearly stated
- Justification required for unusual or questionable results

**Complete:**
- All required fields completed (no blanks without explanation)
- All signatures obtained from authorized personnel
- All supporting data and evidence included
- N/A (Not Applicable) clearly marked if field legitimately not needed
- All calculations shown and verified

**Consistent:**
- Same terminology used throughout documentation
- Same units, scales, and reference standards throughout
- Documentation format consistent across similar tests
- Cross-referencing consistent between documents
- Consistent with historical data for similar tests (if applicable)

**Enduring:**
- Records maintained in stable, readable format
- Paper records stored appropriately (not degrading)
- Electronic records backed up and maintained per system requirements
- Microfilm or digital archival for long-term retention
- Protection from physical damage, loss, or deterioration

**Available:**
- Records retrievable when needed for quality decisions
- Proper indexing and filing system maintained
- Chain of custody maintained if records transferred
- Access controls maintain confidentiality while allowing authorized retrieval
- Retention requirements met for regulatory inspection

### 4.2 GOOD DOCUMENTATION PRACTICE (GDP) PRINCIPLES

**Timeliness:** Record information as it is generated, not after-the-fact.

**Accuracy:** Record exactly what is observed; correct errors appropriately.

**Legibility:** Use permanent ink, clear handwriting; no erasures.

**Authenticity:** Sign and date all entries; maintain audit trails for electronic records.

**Completeness:** Include all relevant information; explain any omissions.

## 5. PROCEDURE

### 5.1 LABORATORY NOTEBOOK STANDARDS

**5.1.1 Notebook Selection and Setup**

**Notebook Requirements:**
- Physical Notebooks: Bound (not loose-leaf), sequentially numbered pages
- Page numbering: Pre-printed or manually numbered at start of use
- Initials of scientist on every page (header or footer area)
- Date range of entries written on front cover
- Table of contents maintained (if applicable)

**Notebook Assignment:**
- Unique identifier assigned to each notebook
- Assignment recorded in QC records with date and analyst name
- Completion recorded when notebook full
- Archival location documented

**5.1.2 Notebook Entry Standards**

**Each entry must include:**
1. **Date and Time:** Full date (DD-MMM-YYYY) and time (HH:MM) of entry
2. **Analyst Name and Initials:** Full legible signature or typed name + initials
3. **Sample Identification:** Complete sample number, batch number, product name
4. **Test Method:** Method name and SOP reference (e.g., "HPLC Cannabinoid Analysis per QCS_01.09_v1")
5. **Raw Data:** Original numerical observations (not summaries)
6. **Observations:** Any relevant observations about sample, equipment, or conditions
7. **Calculations:** All mathematical calculations shown (not just final result)
8. **Equipment Used:** Instrument name, serial number, condition
9. **Standards/Reagents:** Reference standard lot number, expiration date, concentration
10. **Results:** Test result with units and acceptance criteria status

**Forbidden Practices:**
❌ Correction fluid or erasers
❌ Use of pencil for final data
❌ Blank spaces without explanation
❌ Retroactive dating
❌ Abbreviations without legend
❌ Copying from another source without documentation
❌ Multiple entries on same date without time differentiation

**Error Correction Procedure:**
- Strike through error with single line (not heavy obscuring)
- Write initials and date next to correction
- Brief explanation of error (optional unless significant)
- Original value still visible
- Example:
  ```
  Result: 19.5 21.2 mg/mL
           ----
         Analyst: JN Date: 15-JAN-2026
         (transcription error, instrument reading was 21.2)
  ```

### 5.2 RAW DATA MANAGEMENT

**5.2.1 Instrument-Generated Data**

**HPLC and Other Analytical Instruments:**
- Electronic data saved with unique filename including date and sample ID
- Filename format: `[METHOD]_[BATCH#]_[DATE]_[ANALYIST_INITIALS].txt`
- Example: `HPLC_CANNABIS_PP-2026-001_15JAN2026_JN.txt`
- Instrument output printed and attached to laboratory notebook
- Printout signed and dated by analyst who performed test
- Calculation sheet (if applicable) completed contemporaneously

**Data Integrity Verification:**
- Hash or checksum recorded for electronic data files
- Data backup maintained on secure server
- Audit trail enabled if using computerized systems
- No modifications to raw data after initial save

**5.2.2 Manual Calculations**

**Calculation Documentation:**
- All equations shown in full
- Intermediate calculation steps recorded
- Units clearly stated at each step
- Final result clearly identified
- Independent verification signature required for critical calculations
- Example:

```
HPLC Cannabinoid Calculation
Sample: PP-2026-001 Batch 1
Test Date: 15-JAN-2026

HPLC Peak Area Data:
  THC Peak Area = 2,847,300 counts
  CBD Peak Area = 185,400 counts
  IS Peak Area = 1,923,100 counts (Internal Standard)

Calculation (Linear Regression Method):
  % THC = (Peak Area THC / Peak Area IS) × Calibration Factor
  % THC = (2,847,300 / 1,923,100) × 0.00698
  % THC = 1.481 × 0.00698
  % THC = 10.34% w/w

Acceptance Criteria: 18.0 - 22.0% w/w
Result: FAIL (Low)

Recorded by: J. Nikolov, QC Analyst
Date: 15-JAN-2026, 14:30
Reviewed by: [QC_HEAD_NAME]
Date: 15-JAN-2026, 15:00
```

### 5.3 BATCH TESTING RECORDS

**5.3.1 Batch Receipt Documentation**

When batch received for testing:
- Batch ID and product name recorded
- Date and time of receipt recorded
- Condition of packaging assessed (documented)
- Sample quantity received recorded
- Assigned QC test number (e.g., QC-2026-001)
- Analyst name recorded
- Batch placed in quarantine/hold status

**5.3.2 Testing Assessment Checklist**

Completion of standardized form including:
- ☐ Sample received and verified
- ☐ All required testing identified
- ☐ Specifications obtained and reviewed
- ☐ Testing timeline estimated
- ☐ Analyst assigned
- ☐ Equipment verified ready/qualified
- ☐ Reference standards valid and within expiration
- ☐ All initial conditions documented

**5.3.3 Test Result Recording**

For each analytical test performed:
- Test method and SOP reference
- Analyst performing test
- Date and time of analysis
- Equipment ID/serial number used
- Raw data (as above)
- Calculated result
- Comparison to specification
- Pass/Fail/Investigate determination
- Any deviations or observations

**5.3.4 Batch Release Documentation**

Final batch testing record includes:
- Summary of all test results
- Compliance statement (all tests passed specifications)
- Head of QC signature and date
- Recommendation: "Release" or "Hold"
- Forward to Quality Assurance and Qualified Person for final approval

### 5.4 ELECTRONIC RECORDS & SYSTEMS

**5.4.1 System Requirements**

If using computerized systems for QC records:

**Data Integrity:**
- System configured to prevent alteration of original data
- Audit trail enabled showing all modifications (who, when, what)
- Automatic time-stamping of entries
- Electronic signatures compliant with EU Annex 11

**Access Control:**
- User accounts with unique IDs (not shared accounts)
- Password protection (minimum 8 characters)
- Role-based access (analysts see own data; QC Head sees all)
- Automatic logout after inactivity (15 minutes)

**Backup and Recovery:**
- Daily automated backups to secure server
- Weekly backup copies maintained off-site
- Recovery procedures tested quarterly
- Documentation of backup schedule maintained

**5.4.2 Electronic Signatures**

**When electronic signatures used (for approval of records):**
- Unique user ID assigned to each person
- Password known only to authorized user
- Intent-to-sign statement or electronic equivalent
- Time and date automatically recorded
- Signature cannot be repudiated (user cannot deny signing)
- Audit trail of signature maintained

**Signature placement:**
- Electronic signatures placed in dedicated signature block
- Multiple approvers clearly identified
- Each signature linked to specific document version

### 5.5 DOCUMENT RETENTION AND ARCHIVAL

**5.5.1 Retention Requirements**

| Record Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Batch testing records | Life of product + 5 years |
| HPLC spectra and raw data | 5 years |
| Method validation reports | Indefinite (minimum 10 years) |
| Equipment qualification | Life of equipment |
| Personnel training files | Duration of employment + 3 years |
| Laboratory notebooks | 5 years (retain original) |
| Reference material certs | Life of material used |

**5.5.2 Archival Procedures**

**Paper Records:**
- Original documents retained in quality control archive
- Location: `04_QUALITY_TESTING/Archive/[YEAR]/[BATCH_ID]/`
- Organized chronologically by batch number
- Climate-controlled storage (18-25°C, 40-60% RH)
- Protected from moisture, light, and physical damage

**Electronic Records:**
- Master copies maintained on secure server with backup
- Read-only archival format (PDF) for long-term storage
- Dual-medium archival (electronic + printed copy) for critical records
- Index/catalog maintained for retrieval
- Access restricted to authorized personnel (QA, QC Head)

**5.5.3 Retrieval and Audit Trail**

- Records retrieval logged and documented
- Chain of custody maintained if records removed from archive
- Return to archive documented
- Any records with evidence of tampering reported immediately

## 6. QUALITY CONTROL CHECKS FOR DOCUMENTATION

### 6.1 Documentation Quality Audits

**Quarterly Documentation Audit (by QA Manager):**
- Review 10-15 batch testing records
- Assess GDP compliance (ALCOA+ principles)
- Check all signatures and dates present
- Verify calculations accuracy
- Check data integrity and no erasures
- Document findings in audit report
- Issue corrective actions for deficiencies

**Monthly Documentation Review (by QC Head):**
- Review all documentation from prior month
- Identify trends in documentation quality
- Provide feedback to analysts
- Update training needs based on findings

### 6.2 Training on GDP

**Initial QC Training (mandatory before independent work):**
- Good Documentation Practice workshop (4 hours)
- ALCOA+ principles detailed training (2 hours)
- Laboratory notebook standards practice (2 hours)
- Electronic systems training (if applicable) (4 hours)
- Assessment: Written exam + practical demonstration

**Annual Refresher Training:**
- GDP/ALCOA+ refresher (2 hours)
- Review of regulatory updates on documentation
- Case studies of documentation errors and lessons learned
- Update on new electronic systems or changes
