## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure establishes controlled methods for the curing of dried cannabis inflorescence to optimize cannabinoid and terpene profiles, develop desirable sensory characteristics, ensure microbiological stability, and achieve target moisture content and water activity specifications. The procedure ensures consistent product quality in compliance with EU GMP and GACP requirements.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

- **All dried cannabis material** post-initial drying
- **All chemotypes** and cultivars processed at the facility
- **Batch and continuous** curing operations
- **All personnel** involved in curing activities
- **Quality control** monitoring of curing parameters

## 6. Pre-Curing Requirements

### 6.1 Material Readiness Assessment

**Material must meet the following criteria before curing:**

| Parameter | Specification | Test Method |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Moisture Content** | <span class="var">[10-14%]</span> | Loss on drying |
| **Water Activity** | <span class="var">[0.55-0.65 aw]</span> | Water activity meter |
| **Stem Snap Test** | Stems snap, don't bend | Physical assessment |
| **Visual Inspection** | No visible mold or pests | Visual |
| **Temperature** | <span class="var">[Equilibrated to curing room temp]</span> | Thermometer |

### 6.2 Material Preparation

1. **Verify** batch documentation from drying (PRO_03.02)
2. **Confirm** batch has been released from drying by QC
3. **Check** material has equilibrated to room temperature
4. **Inspect** for uniformity and any quality issues
5. **Record** incoming weight on Curing Batch Record (FM-PRO-030)

### 6.3 Container Preparation

1. **Clean** containers per SOP PRO_05.01 Cleaning Procedures
2. **Inspect** seals and closures for integrity
3. **Verify** containers are completely dry
4. **Label** containers with batch information:
   - Batch number
   - Cultivar
   - Date curing started
   - Target completion date
   - Operator initials

## 7. Curing Procedure

### 7.1 Initial Fill (Day 0)

**Step 1: Weigh Material**

1. Tare container on calibrated scale
2. Fill container to <span class="var">[70-80%]</span> capacity (do not over-pack)
3. Record net weight on batch record
4. Calculate fill density: <span class="var">[~0.15-0.20 g/cm³]</span>

**Step 2: Initial Moisture Assessment**

1. Place calibrated hygrometer inside container
2. Seal container
3. Allow <span class="var">[30 minutes]</span> equilibration
4. Record initial internal RH

**Step 3: Seal and Store**

1. Ensure airtight seal
2. Place in designated curing area
3. Position away from direct light
4. Record location on batch record

### 7.2 Burping Schedule

**"Burping" = opening containers to exchange air and release built-up gases**

| Phase | Days | Burping Frequency | Duration |
|-------|------|-------------------|----------|
| **Phase 1** (Active Curing) | <span class="var">[1-7]</span> | <span class="var">[2-3× daily]</span> | <span class="var">[10-15 min]</span> |
| **Phase 2** (Stabilization) | <span class="var">[8-14]</span> | <span class="var">[1× daily]</span> | <span class="var">[10-15 min]</span> |
| **Phase 3** (Extended Cure) | <span class="var">[15-28]</span> | <span class="var">[Every 2-3 days]</span> | <span class="var">[5-10 min]</span> |
| **Phase 4** (Long-term) | <span class="var">[29+]</span> | <span class="var">[Weekly]</span> | <span class="var">[5 min]</span> |

### 7.3 Burping Procedure

**For each burping session:**

1. **Don** appropriate PPE (gloves, gown)
2. **Open** container in curing room
3. **Inspect** visually for any issues:
   - Ammonia odor (indicates too wet, anaerobic)
   - Mold growth (reject if present)
   - Off-odors (investigate)
4. **Check** internal hygrometer reading
5. **Gently agitate** or rotate material to redistribute
6. **Allow** fresh air exchange for prescribed duration
7. **Re-seal** container
8. **Record** on Curing Log (FM-PRO-031):
   - Date and time
   - Internal RH reading
   - Observations
   - Operator initials

### 7.4 Moisture Management During Curing

**If Internal RH > <span class="var">[65%]</span>:**

1. Leave container open longer (<span class="var">[30-60 min]</span>)
2. Spread material on clean tray for <span class="var">[1-2 hours]</span> if significantly elevated
3. Increase burping frequency temporarily
4. Consider adding humidity control packets (lower RH rating)
5. Monitor closely for mold development

**If Internal RH < <span class="var">[55%]</span>:**

1. Reduce burping frequency
2. Add humidity control packets (<span class="var">[62% RH]</span> rating)
3. Ensure container seal is airtight
4. Consider adding small amount of fresh material (if same batch available)

**If Ammonia Odor Detected:**

1. **Immediately** spread material on clean trays
2. Allow thorough air exchange (<span class="var">[2-4 hours]</span>)
3. Test moisture content
4. Return to container only when odor resolved
5. Document deviation

### 8.2 Product Testing Schedule

| Test | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 4 | Release |
|------|--------|--------|--------|---------|
| **Moisture Content** | Day 1, 7 | Day 14 | Day 28 | Final |
| **Water Activity** | Day 1, 7 | Day 14 | Day 28 | Final |
| **Visual Inspection** | Daily | Daily | Every 3 days | Final |
| **Organoleptic** | Weekly | Weekly | At completion | Final |
| **Microbial** | If indicated | If indicated | Final | Final |

### 8.3 Sensory Evaluation During Curing

**Assess at weekly intervals:**

| Attribute | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3-4 | Target |
|-----------|--------|--------|----------|--------|
| **Aroma Intensity** | Grassy/hay-like | Developing terpenes | Full terpene expression | Rich, strain-specific |
| **Aroma Quality** | Chlorophyll notes | Chlorophyll diminishing | Complex bouquet | No hay/grass notes |
| **Bud Feel** | Slightly moist | Drying | Optimal tackiness | Sticky, springs back |
| **Visual** | Green, fresh | Slight color change | Mature coloration | Cultivar-typical |

## 9. Curing Completion Criteria

### 9.1 Release Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Test Method |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| **Moisture Content** | <span class="var">[8-11% w/w]</span> | Loss on drying (QC_04.05) |
| **Water Activity** | <span class="var">[0.55-0.60 aw]</span> | Water activity meter |
| **Minimum Cure Duration** | <span class="var">[≥14 days]</span> | Batch record |
| **Optimal Cure Duration** | <span class="var">[21-28 days]</span> | Batch record |
| **Visual Quality** | No mold, proper color | Visual inspection |
| **Aroma** | Characteristic, no off-odors | Organoleptic |
| **Microbial** | Within specification | Per QC_04.04 |

### 9.2 Extended Curing

For premium products, extended curing <span class="var">[4-8 weeks]</span> may be employed:

- Continue weekly burping
- Monitor water activity to ensure stability
- Document extended curing rationale
- Additional sensory evaluations

### 9.3 Curing Completion Process

1. **Perform** final moisture content and water activity testing
2. **Conduct** sensory evaluation
3. **Submit** samples for final QC testing (if not already complete)
4. **Complete** Curing Batch Record (FM-PRO-030)
5. **QC review** and approval
6. **Transfer** to packaging or storage with appropriate documentation

## 11. Deviation Management

### 11.1 Common Deviations and Responses

| Deviation | Immediate Action | Investigation Required |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Mold detected** | Quarantine, reject affected | Root cause analysis |
| **Ammonia odor** | Air out, assess moisture | Process review |
| **Room temp excursion** | Correct HVAC, document | Impact assessment |
| **Room RH excursion** | Adjust humidity controls | Impact assessment |
| **Container seal failure** | Transfer to new container | Batch impact review |

### 11.2 Deviation Documentation

All deviations require:
1. Immediate verbal notification to supervisor
2. Deviation Report (FM-QA-010) within <span class="var">[24 hours]</span>
3. Root cause investigation
4. Impact assessment on product quality
5. Corrective action implementation

## 13. Equipment Cleaning

### 13.1 Container Cleaning

**Between Batches:**

1. Remove all residual plant material
2. Wash with <span class="var">[hot water + food-grade detergent]</span>
3. Rinse thoroughly with purified water
4. Sanitize with <span class="var">[70% isopropanol or approved sanitizer]</span>
5. Air dry completely in clean area
6. Verify dry before reuse

### 13.2 Hygrometer Maintenance

1. Check calibration monthly using salt standards
2. Replace batteries as needed
3. Clean probe with lint-free cloth
4. Replace if readings drift beyond specification
