## FORM INSTRUCTIONS

### How to Complete This Form

1. **Request Information (Section 1)**
   - Obtain request number from Training Coordinator
   - Indicate urgency level based on regulatory or operational need

2. **Training Details (Section 2)**
   - Select appropriate training type(s)
   - Clearly state training objectives and justification
   - Reference relevant documents (SOP, CAPA, Change Control)

3. **Trainee Information (Section 3)**
   - List all employees requiring training
   - For large groups (>10), attach separate list

4. **Training Delivery (Section 4)**
   - Select most appropriate delivery method for content
   - Consider trainee availability and production schedules

5. **Materials (Section 5)**
   - Identify all materials needed
   - Allow adequate time for material preparation

6. **Trainer (Section 6)**
   - Internal trainers must be qualified for the subject
   - External providers must have appropriate credentials

7. **Assessment (Section 7)**
   - Select assessment method appropriate to training type
   - GMP-critical training typically requires written and/or practical assessment

8. **Budget (Section 8)**
   - Obtain cost estimates for external training
   - Consider production downtime costs

9. **Submit for Approval**
   - Department Manager approval required
   - QA review required for GMP-related training
   - Training Coordinator schedules and assigns resources
