## Прирачник за квалитет | Quality Manual

### 1. PURPOSE / ЦЕЛ

The Quality Manual defines the Quality Management System (QMS) for Purely Plant GmbH, a medical cannabis cultivation and processing facility. This document provides an overview of the QMS structure, scope, and key processes.
Целта на овој прирачник е да го дефинира Системот за управување со квалитет (QMS) за Purely Plant GmbH.

### 2. SCOPE / ОПСЕГ

This Quality Manual applies to all activities at Purely Plant GmbH related to the cultivation, processing, testing, and release of medical cannabis products.

### 3. QUALITY POLICY / ПОЛИТИКА ЗА КВАЛИТЕТ

Purely Plant GmbH is committed to:
- Producing high-quality medical cannabis products
- Compliance with EU GMP requirements
- Continuous improvement of processes
- Protecting patient safety

### 4. ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE / ОРГАНИЗАЦИОНА СТРУКТУРА

Key personnel and their responsibilities are defined in the organizational chart and job descriptions.

### 5. QMS PROCESSES / ПРОЦЕСИ НА QMS

The QMS includes the following key processes:
- Document Control (QA_00.02)
- Change Control (QA_00.05)
- CAPA System (QA_00.06)
- Deviation Handling (QA_00.07)
- Self-Inspection (QA_00.08)
- Training (QA_00.03)

### 6. REFERENCES / РЕФЕРЕНЦИ

- EudraLex Volume 4, Annex 13
- ISO 9001:2015
- Macedonian Pharmaceutical Law
