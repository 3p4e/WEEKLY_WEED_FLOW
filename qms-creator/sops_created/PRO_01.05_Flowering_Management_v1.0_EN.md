## 1. PURPOSE

This SOP establishes procedures for the flowering phase management of cannabis production, from photoperiod transition through harvest readiness determination at <span class="var">[FACILITY_NAME]</span>.

---

## 2. SCOPE

Applies to:
- Photoperiod transition (flip)
- Flowering room environmental management
- Nutritional management during flowering
- Canopy management and defoliation
- Flower development monitoring
- Harvest readiness determination

## 4. PROCEDURE

### 4.1 Photoperiod Transition (Flip)

#### 4.1.1 Pre-Flip Checklist

| Item | Verification |
|------|--------------|
| Plants meet veg criteria | Height, structure, health confirmed |
| Transfer documentation complete | <span class="var">[FM-VEG-004]</span> signed |
| Room prepared | Clean, sanitized, equipment checked |
| Environmental controls | Verified operational |
| Timer programming | Set for <span class="var">[12/12]</span> schedule |
| Batch record initiated | <span class="var">[FM-FLO-001]</span> started |

#### 4.1.2 Flip Day Procedure

1. Record flip date in batch record
2. Adjust timer to <span class="var">[12/12]</span> photoperiod
3. Verify dark period timing
4. Begin transition nutrient formula
5. Note expected harvest window: <span class="var">[Flip date + 8-10 weeks]</span> (cultivar dependent)

### 4.2 Flowering Phase Management

#### 4.2.1 Week 1-3: Stretch Phase

**Characteristics:**
- Plants stretch <span class="var">[50-100%]</span> in height
- Pre-flowers developing
- Rapid vegetative-to-generative transition

**Management:**
1. Maintain spacing for airflow
2. Final canopy adjustments:
   - Supercrop tall branches if needed
   - Tuck leaves for light penetration
3. Monitor for hermaphroditism (stress response)
4. Light defoliation if necessary (Day <span class="var">[21]</span>)

**Nutrition:**
- Transition formula (reducing N, increasing P-K)
- EC: <span class="var">[1.6-1.8 mS/cm]</span>

#### 4.2.2 Week 4-6: Flower Development (Bulk Phase)

**Characteristics:**
- Bud sites established
- Calyx and pistil development
- Trichome production beginning

**Management:**
1. Heavy defoliation recommended at Day <span class="var">[42]</span> (Week 6 start):
   - Remove large fan leaves blocking bud sites
   - Improve airflow in canopy
   - No more than <span class="var">[30%]</span> removal at once

2. Support structures if needed:
   - Install trellis or stakes for heavy branches

3. Monitor for:
   - Nutrient deficiencies (especially calcium, magnesium)
   - Pest pressure (especially spider mites, thrips)
   - Powdery mildew (humidity critical)

**Nutrition:**
- Full bloom formula
- EC: <span class="var">[1.8-2.0 mS/cm]</span>
- Consider PK boosters per cultivar protocol

#### 4.2.3 Week 7-8: Ripening Phase

**Characteristics:**
- Bud swelling
- Pistils changing color (white → orange/red)
- Trichome development progressing

**Management:**
1. Reduce humidity to <span class="var">[45% RH]</span> for mold prevention
2. Reduce temperature slightly (aids color development)
3. No more defoliation
4. Begin reducing nitrogen

**Nutrition:**
- Reduced strength: EC <span class="var">[1.6-1.8 mS/cm]</span>
- Low nitrogen formula
- Continue PK support

#### 4.2.4 Week 9+: Final Flush and Maturation

**Pre-Harvest Flush:**

1. Begin <span class="var">[7-14 days]</span> before target harvest
2. Flush protocol:
   - Plain water only (pH <span class="var">[6.0]</span>)
   - EC: <span class="var">[0.0-0.3 mS/cm]</span>
   - Reduced frequency
3. Purpose:
   - Mobilize remaining nutrients from leaves
   - Improve final product quality
   - Natural senescence of fan leaves (yellowing)

### 4.3 Harvest Readiness Determination

#### 4.3.1 Visual Indicators

| Indicator | Not Ready | Getting Close | Ready |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Pistil color | <span class="var">[Mostly white]</span> | <span class="var">[50-70% orange/brown]</span> | <span class="var">[70-90% orange/brown]</span> |
| Calyx swell | <span class="var">[Thin, not swollen]</span> | <span class="var">[Beginning to swell]</span> | <span class="var">[Fully swollen]</span> |
| Fan leaves | <span class="var">[Green]</span> | <span class="var">[Yellowing begins]</span> | <span class="var">[Significant yellowing]</span> |
| New pistil growth | <span class="var">[Active]</span> | <span class="var">[Slowing]</span> | <span class="var">[Stopped]</span> |

#### 4.3.2 Trichome Analysis (Primary Method)

**Equipment:** Digital microscope or jeweler's loupe (<span class="var">[60-100x]</span>)

**Examine trichomes on:**
- Calyxes (not sugar leaves)
- Multiple bud sites
- Various parts of plant

**Trichome Maturity:**

| Stage | Appearance | Effect Profile | Harvest Decision |
|-------|------------|----------------|------------------|
| Clear | Transparent, glass-like | Low potency | <span class="var">[Too early]</span> |
| Cloudy/Milky | Opaque, white | Peak THC, energetic | <span class="var">[Optimal for most]</span> |
| Amber | Orange/brown tint | More sedative, CBN | <span class="var">[Late harvest]</span> |

**Target for harvest:** <span class="var">[70-90% cloudy with 10-30% amber]</span> (adjust per cultivar spec)

#### 4.3.3 Harvest Decision Protocol

1. Perform trichome assessment at Week <span class="var">[7]</span> (baseline)
2. Repeat every <span class="var">[2-3 days]</span> as approaching maturity
3. Document findings in Harvest Readiness Assessment <span class="var">[FM-FLO-003]</span>
4. When criteria met:
   - Notify Cultivation Manager
   - Obtain harvest authorization
   - Schedule harvest per <span class="var">[PRO_02.01]</span>
   - Complete flowering batch record

## 5. CRITICAL CONTROLS

### 5.1 Light Leak Prevention

| Control | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Door seals | Verified, no gaps |
| Equipment indicators | Covered or disabled during dark |
| Emergency entry | Green light only if required |
| Room integrity check | <span class="var">[Weekly during dark period]</span> |

### 5.2 Humidity Control (Mold Prevention)

| Week | Maximum RH | Action if Exceeded |
|------|------------|-------------------|
| 1-3 | <span class="var">[60%]</span> | Increase dehumidification |
| 4-6 | <span class="var">[55%]</span> | Increase dehumidification, improve airflow |
| 7+ | <span class="var">[50%]</span> | Critical - immediate correction, inspect for mold |
