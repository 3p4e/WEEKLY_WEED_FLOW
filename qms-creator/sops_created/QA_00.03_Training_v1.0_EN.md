## 1. PURPOSE

### 1.1 Objective

This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the requirements for training and qualification of all personnel at <span class="var">[COMPANY_NAME]</span> to ensure they possess the necessary knowledge, skills, and competencies to perform their duties in compliance with EU GMP requirements.

### 1.2 Goals

This procedure ensures that:

- All personnel receive appropriate initial and ongoing training
- Training is documented and records are maintained
- Personnel are qualified before performing GMP activities independently
- Training effectiveness is assessed
- Training requirements are identified and addressed systematically
- Compliance with EU GMP Chapter 2 (Personnel) is maintained

---

## 2. SCOPE

### 2.1 Applicability

This SOP applies to:

- All permanent employees of <span class="var">[COMPANY_NAME]</span>
- Temporary and contract personnel performing GMP activities
- Consultants working on-site
- Visitors who access GMP areas (visitor training/orientation)

### 2.2 Training Categories Covered

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| GMP Fundamentals | Basic GMP principles and regulations |
| Job-Specific Training | SOPs and procedures relevant to job role |
| Equipment Training | Operation of specific equipment |
| Safety Training | Occupational health and safety |
| Quality System Training | Quality policy, systems, and procedures |
| Regulatory Training | Updates to regulations and guidelines |
| Specialized Training | Advanced or specialized skill development |

---

## 3. RESPONSIBILITIES

### 3.1 Quality Assurance Manager

- Overall responsibility for the training program
- Approving training curricula and materials
- Ensuring regulatory compliance of training program
- Reviewing training metrics and effectiveness
- Authorizing qualification of personnel for GMP activities

### 3.2 Training Coordinator

- Day-to-day management of training activities
- Maintaining training records and database
- Scheduling training sessions
- Tracking training completion and retraining needs
- Coordinating with department managers on training needs
- Preparing training reports and metrics

### 3.3 Human Resources Manager

- Integration of training into onboarding process
- Maintaining personnel files including training records
- Coordinating external training programs
- Supporting competency assessment processes

### 3.4 Department Managers

- Identifying training needs for their department
- Ensuring personnel complete required training
- Providing on-the-job training (OJT) and mentoring
- Assessing competency of their team members
- Releasing personnel for training activities
- Reviewing and updating department-specific training curricula

### 3.5 Trainers (Subject Matter Experts)

- Developing and delivering training content
- Assessing trainee understanding and competency
- Updating training materials as needed
- Maintaining trainer qualification status

### 3.6 All Personnel

- Completing assigned training within required timeframes
- Applying training to job performance
- Reporting training needs or gaps
- Maintaining personal training records awareness
- Not performing tasks without appropriate training

## 4. DEFINITIONS

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Competency** | Demonstrated ability to apply knowledge and skills to perform a task correctly |
| **Curriculum** | Structured set of training requirements for a specific role or function |
| **On-the-Job Training (OJT)** | Training conducted in the actual work environment under supervision |
| **Qualification** | Formal confirmation that a person has successfully completed training and demonstrated competency |
| **Read and Understand (R&U)** | Training method where trainee reads a document and confirms understanding |
| **Retraining** | Training provided due to procedure change, performance issue, or periodic requirement |
| **Training Gap** | Difference between required training and actual training completed |
| **Training Matrix** | Document showing required training for each role/position |
| **Training Effectiveness** | Assessment of whether training achieved its intended objectives |
| **Trainer Qualification** | Process of confirming a trainer has the knowledge and ability to train others |

### 5.2 Induction Training (New Employee)

#### 5.2.1 Pre-Start Requirements

Before starting work, new employees must complete:

| Training Module | Duration | Trainer | Assessment |
|----------------|----------|---------|------------|
| Company Orientation | <span class="var">[4 hours]</span> | HR | Attendance |
| GMP Fundamentals | <span class="var">[8 hours]</span> | QA Trainer | Written test (≥<span class="var">[80%]</span>) |
| Good Documentation Practices | <span class="var">[2 hours]</span> | QA Trainer | Written test (≥<span class="var">[80%]</span>) |
| Quality Policy & QMS | <span class="var">[2 hours]</span> | QA Manager | R&U sign-off |
| Hygiene & Gowning | <span class="var">[2 hours]</span> | Supervisor | Practical demonstration |
| Safety & Emergency | <span class="var">[4 hours]</span> | Safety Officer | Written test |
| Controlled Substances | <span class="var">[2 hours]</span> | QA/Security | Written test |

**Total Induction Duration:** <span class="var">[24 hours / 3 days]</span>

#### 5.2.2 Induction Training Content

**GMP Fundamentals Module:**
- What is GMP and why it matters
- EU GMP requirements overview
- Quality culture and responsibilities
- Contamination risks and prevention
- Deviation and error reporting
- CAPA basics
- Audit and inspection awareness

**Good Documentation Practices Module:**
- Importance of documentation
- ALCOA+ principles (Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate)
- How to make entries in records
- How to make corrections
- What to do with blank spaces
- Electronic records requirements
- Data integrity concepts

**Hygiene & Gowning Module:**
- Personal hygiene requirements
- Illness reporting policy
- Hand washing technique
- Gowning procedures for each area classification
- Jewelry and cosmetics policy
- Clean room behavior

#### 5.2.3 Induction Completion

Upon successful completion of induction:
1. Training Coordinator verifies all modules completed
2. Department Manager confirms readiness for department training
3. Employee signs Induction Completion Form
4. Training records entered into database
5. Employee progresses to department-specific training

### 5.4 On-the-Job Training (OJT)

#### 5.4.1 OJT Process

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      OJT PROCESS FLOW                           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                 │
│  STEP 1: ASSIGN TRAINER                                        │
│  • Qualified trainer assigned by Department Manager            │
│  • Trainer must be qualified on the procedure(s)               │
│                                                                 │
│  STEP 2: DEMONSTRATE                                           │
│  • Trainer demonstrates the procedure                          │
│  • Explains critical steps and rationale                       │
│  • Points out common errors to avoid                           │
│                                                                 │
│  STEP 3: OBSERVE                                               │
│  • Trainee observes trainer performing task(s)                │
│  • Trainee asks questions                                      │
│  • Minimum observations: [3 times]                            │
│                                                                 │
│  STEP 4: PERFORM UNDER SUPERVISION                            │
│  • Trainee performs task under direct supervision             │
│  • Trainer provides guidance and correction                   │
│  • Minimum supervised performances: [3 times]                 │
│                                                                 │
│  STEP 5: COMPETENCY ASSESSMENT                                │
│  • Trainee performs task independently                        │
│  • Trainer observes and assesses competency                   │
│  • Documented on OJT Record Form                              │
│                                                                 │
│  STEP 6: QUALIFICATION                                        │
│  • Trainer signs competency confirmation                      │
│  • Department Manager approves                                │
│  • Trainee authorized for independent performance            │
│                                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### 5.4.2 OJT Documentation

OJT must be documented using form FM-TRN-003 (OJT Record), including:
- Trainee name and position
- Procedure(s) covered
- Trainer name and qualification status
- Dates of observation and supervised practice
- Competency assessment checklist
- Trainer signature confirming competency
- Manager approval

### 5.5 Training Methods

#### 5.5.1 Available Training Methods

| Method | Description | When Used | Documentation |
|--------|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| **Classroom** | Instructor-led training | Complex topics, groups | Attendance record, assessment |
| **Read & Understand (R&U)** | Self-study of document | Simple SOPs, updates | Signed R&U form |
| **On-the-Job (OJT)** | Supervised hands-on | Practical skills | OJT record |
| **E-Learning** | Computer-based training | Standard modules, remote | System record, online quiz |
| **External Course** | Training outside organization | Specialized skills | Certificate, attendance |
| **Workshop** | Interactive group session | Problem-solving, skills | Attendance, exercises |
| **Mentoring** | One-on-one guidance | Complex roles, leadership | Mentoring log |
| **Simulation** | Practice scenarios | Emergency response, errors | Exercise record |

#### 5.5.2 Read and Understand (R&U) Process

For simple document training via R&U:

1. Training Coordinator assigns document to trainee
2. Trainee receives controlled copy or electronic access
3. Trainee reads document thoroughly
4. Trainee signs R&U form confirming:
   - Document was read completely
   - Content was understood
   - Questions were clarified
5. R&U form filed in training record
6. Training logged in database

**R&U is NOT appropriate for:**
- Complex procedures requiring demonstration
- Equipment operation
- Aseptic technique
- Any procedure with safety-critical steps

### 5.6 Assessment and Qualification

#### 5.6.1 Assessment Methods

| Method | Passing Score | Used For |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| Written Test | ≥<span class="var">[80%]</span> | Knowledge assessment |
| Oral Examination | Satisfactory | Understanding verification |
| Practical Demonstration | Pass/Fail per checklist | Skills assessment |
| Observed Performance | Competent/Not Yet Competent | OJT completion |

#### 5.6.2 Qualification Requirements

Personnel must be qualified before performing tasks independently. Qualification requires:

1. **Completion of all required training** for the task
2. **Passing all assessments** at required level
3. **Demonstration of competency** (practical tasks)
4. **Authorization** by Department Manager and/or QA

#### 5.6.3 Qualification Status

| Status | Meaning | Permitted Activities |
|--------|---------|---------------------|
| **In Training** | Training not complete | Only under direct supervision |
| **Qualified** | Training complete, competent | Independent performance |
| **Suspended** | Qualification temporarily removed | No independent performance |
| **Disqualified** | Qualification permanently removed | Retraining required |

### 5.7 Retraining Requirements

#### 5.7.1 Triggered Retraining

Retraining is required when:

| Trigger | Timeframe | Scope |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| SOP Revision (Major) | Before effective date | Affected personnel |
| SOP Revision (Minor) | <span class="var">[30 days]</span> | Affected personnel |
| Performance Issue | Immediate | Individual |
| Deviation (personnel error) | <span class="var">[5 working days]</span> | Individual + similar roles if systemic |
| Extended Absence (><span class="var">[6 months]</span>) | Before resuming duties | Individual |
| Audit Finding | As per CAPA | As defined |
| Equipment Change | Before use | Equipment users |
| Process Change | Before implementation | Process operators |

#### 5.7.2 Periodic Retraining

| Training | Frequency |
|----------|-----------|
| GMP Fundamentals | <span class="var">[Annual]</span> |
| Good Documentation Practices | <span class="var">[Annual]</span> |
| Safety Refresher | <span class="var">[Annual]</span> |
| Department SOPs | <span class="var">[Every 2 years]</span> or upon revision |
| Controlled Substances | <span class="var">[Annual]</span> |
| Equipment Qualification | <span class="var">[Every 2 years]</span> or after extended non-use |

### 5.8 Training Effectiveness Assessment

#### 5.8.1 Effectiveness Indicators

Training effectiveness shall be monitored through:

| Indicator | Measurement | Target |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Training Completion Rate | % completed on time | <span class="var">[≥95%]</span> |
| Assessment Pass Rate | % passing first time | <span class="var">[≥90%]</span> |
| Personnel Error Rate | Deviations due to personnel error | <span class="var">[Decreasing trend]</span> |
| Competency Retention | Performance in periodic assessments | <span class="var">[Maintained]</span> |
| Feedback Scores | Training evaluation ratings | <span class="var">[≥4/5]</span> |

#### 5.8.2 Effectiveness Review Process

1. **Immediate:** Assessment results reviewed after each training
2. **30-Day Review:** Performance observation by supervisor
3. **Quarterly:** Training metrics reviewed by Training Coordinator
4. **Annual:** Comprehensive review in Management Review

#### 5.8.3 Training Evaluation

After each training session, participants complete Training Evaluation Form (FM-TRN-004):
- Relevance of content
- Trainer effectiveness
- Training materials quality
- Training environment
- Suggestions for improvement

### 5.9 Trainer Qualification

#### 5.9.1 Trainer Requirements

To be a qualified trainer, individuals must:

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Subject Matter Expertise | Qualified on all procedures they will train |
| Experience | Minimum <span class="var">[6 months]</span> performing the task |
| Train-the-Trainer | Completed Train-the-Trainer course |
| Communication Skills | Demonstrated ability to communicate effectively |
| Assessment Skills | Trained on assessment and feedback techniques |
| Documentation | Trained on training record requirements |

#### 5.9.2 Trainer Qualification Process

1. Department Manager nominates trainer candidate
2. Candidate completes Train-the-Trainer course
3. Candidate conducts training under supervision
4. QA/Training Coordinator observes and evaluates
5. If satisfactory, trainer added to Qualified Trainers List
6. Trainer qualification reviewed <span class="var">[annually]</span>

#### 5.9.3 Qualified Trainers List

Maintained by Training Coordinator, including:
- Trainer name
- Department
- Qualified to train (list of procedures/topics)
- Qualification date
- Next review date
- Status (Active/Inactive)

### 5.10 External Training

#### 5.10.1 Approval Process

For external training (courses, conferences, certifications):

1. Employee/Manager submits External Training Request
2. Department Manager approves business need
3. QA reviews GMP relevance (if applicable)
4. HR approves budget
5. Training scheduled

#### 5.10.2 Documentation

Upon completion of external training:
1. Certificate/attendance proof submitted to Training Coordinator
2. Summary of learning shared with relevant personnel (if applicable)
3. Training logged in employee record
4. Material added to training library (if applicable)
