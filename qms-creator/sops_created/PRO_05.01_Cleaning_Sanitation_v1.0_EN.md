## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure establishes comprehensive cleaning and sanitation procedures for all production areas, equipment, and facilities involved in the manufacture of cannabis products. The procedure ensures prevention of cross-contamination, maintenance of hygienic conditions, compliance with EU GMP requirements, and consistent product quality.

---

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:

- **All production areas** (cultivation, harvest, drying, curing, packaging)
- **All processing equipment**
- **Controlled environment areas** (cleanrooms, grow rooms)
- **Support areas** (storage, corridors, personnel areas)
- **All personnel** performing cleaning activities
- **Contract cleaning services** (if applicable)

## 5. Cleaning Classification Levels

### 5.1 Classification Definitions

| Level | Description | When Applied |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Routine** | Daily/scheduled maintenance cleaning | Normal operations |
| **Between-Batch** | Cleaning between production batches | Same product, sequential batches |
| **Changeover** | Thorough cleaning between products | Different product or cultivar |
| **Deep Clean** | Comprehensive periodic cleaning | Scheduled, shutdown periods |
| **Decontamination** | Intensive cleaning following contamination | Post-contamination event |

### 5.2 Cleaning Requirements by Classification

| Cleaning Level | Detergent | Rinse | Sanitize | Verification |
|----------------|-----------|-------|----------|--------------|
| **Routine** | Yes | Yes | As needed | Visual |
| **Between-Batch** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Visual |
| **Changeover** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Visual + Swab |
| **Deep Clean** | Yes (2×) | Yes (2×) | Yes | Visual + Swab + Microbial |
| **Decontamination** | Yes | Yes | Sporicide | Swab + Microbial + QA Approval |

## 6. Area-Specific Cleaning Procedures

### 6.1 Cultivation Rooms (Grow Rooms)

**Frequency:**

| Task | Frequency |
|------|-----------|
| **Floor cleaning** | <span class="var">[Daily]</span> |
| **Bench/table wipe-down** | <span class="var">[Daily]</span> |
| **Wall cleaning (lower 1m)** | <span class="var">[Weekly]</span> |
| **Wall cleaning (full)** | <span class="var">[Monthly]</span> |
| **Ceiling/duct cleaning** | <span class="var">[Quarterly]</span> |
| **Between crop cycles** | <span class="var">[Deep clean]</span> |

**Procedure - Daily:**

1. **Remove** all loose plant debris
2. **Sweep** or vacuum floor with HEPA vacuum
3. **Mop** floor with neutral detergent solution
4. **Rinse** with clean water
5. **Allow** to dry or squeegee
6. **Wipe** bench surfaces with approved sanitizer
7. **Document** on Daily Cleaning Log (FM-PRO-050)

**Procedure - Between Crop Cycles:**

1. **Remove** all plants, pots, growing media
2. **Dry clean** - remove all visible debris
3. **Apply** alkaline cleaner to all surfaces
4. **Scrub** floors, walls, benches, equipment
5. **Rinse** thoroughly with clean water
6. **Apply** sanitizer per contact time
7. **Final rinse** (if required by sanitizer)
8. **Allow** to dry completely
9. **Fog** room with peracetic acid (optional for high-risk)
10. **Verify** cleanliness before restocking
11. **Document** on Deep Cleaning Record (FM-PRO-052)

### 6.2 Harvest and Processing Areas

**Frequency:**

| Task | Frequency |
|------|-----------|
| **Work surfaces** | <span class="var">[After each use, minimum daily]</span> |
| **Floors** | <span class="var">[Daily]</span> |
| **Equipment** | <span class="var">[After each batch]</span> |
| **Walls and ceilings** | <span class="var">[Weekly]</span> |
| **Deep clean** | <span class="var">[Monthly]</span> |

**Procedure - Daily / Post-Batch:**

1. **Remove** all product and waste
2. **Disassemble** equipment per manufacturer instructions
3. **Pre-rinse** or dry-brush to remove bulk residue
4. **Apply** cleaning solution
5. **Scrub** all surfaces
6. **Rinse** thoroughly
7. **Sanitize** with approved agent
8. **Air dry** or dry with lint-free wipes
9. **Reassemble** equipment
10. **Document** cleaning

### 6.3 Drying and Curing Rooms

**Frequency:**

| Task | Frequency |
|------|-----------|
| **Floor cleaning** | <span class="var">[Daily]</span> |
| **Rack/shelf cleaning** | <span class="var">[Between batches]</span> |
| **Full room cleaning** | <span class="var">[Monthly or between batches]</span> |
| **HVAC/dehumidifier** | <span class="var">[Monthly]</span> |

**Procedure:**

1. **Empty** room of all product
2. **Remove** drying racks/screens if possible
3. **Vacuum** all surfaces with HEPA vacuum
4. **Clean** racks separately (wash and sanitize)
5. **Mop** floors with detergent, rinse, sanitize
6. **Wipe** walls and ceiling with sanitizer
7. **Clean** HVAC grilles and dehumidifier filters
8. **Allow** room to dry completely
9. **Verify** humidity and temperature returned to specification
10. **Document** cleaning

### 6.4 Packaging Area

**Frequency:**

| Task | Frequency |
|------|-----------|
| **Work surfaces** | <span class="var">[Before each batch, after each batch]</span> |
| **Equipment** | <span class="var">[After each product/batch]</span> |
| **Floors** | <span class="var">[Daily]</span> |
| **Complete area** | <span class="var">[Weekly deep clean]</span> |

**Procedure - Line Clearance Cleaning:**

1. **Remove** all product, labels, packaging materials
2. **Wipe** all equipment surfaces with IPA or sanitizer
3. **Clean** scale platforms
4. **Wipe** conveyor surfaces (if applicable)
5. **Sweep/mop** floor around work area
6. **Visual inspection** for previous batch residue
7. **Document** on Line Clearance Form

### 6.5 Quality Control Laboratory

**Frequency:**

| Task | Frequency |
|------|-----------|

## 7. Equipment Cleaning Procedures

### 7.1 Trimming Equipment (Bucking Machines, Trimmers)

**Cleaning Frequency:** After each batch

**Procedure:**

1. **Power off** and lock out/tag out equipment
2. **Remove** plant material from all surfaces
3. **Disassemble** removable components (blades, screens, bowls)
4. **Soak** removable parts in approved cleaning solution (<span class="var">[15-30 min]</span>)
5. **Scrub** with nylon brush to remove resin
6. **Rinse** thoroughly with clean water
7. **Sanitize** with 70% IPA
8. **Air dry** completely before reassembly
9. **Wipe** main unit exterior with sanitizer
10. **Reassemble** equipment
11. **Lubricate** moving parts per manufacturer instructions
12. **Document** on Equipment Cleaning Log (FM-PRO-053)

**For Heavy Resin Buildup:**
- Use food-grade degreaser or isopropanol
- Extended soak time (<span class="var">[1-2 hours]</span>)
- May require multiple cleaning cycles

### 7.2 Extraction Equipment

**Refer to:** Equipment-specific cleaning SOP (equipment validation documentation)

**General Principles:**

1. Follow manufacturer cleaning procedures
2. Use only approved solvents/cleaning agents
3. Verify all solvent residues removed
4. Perform cleaning validation as required

### 7.3 Packaging Equipment

**Filling Equipment:**

1. Disassemble product contact parts
2. Clean with detergent, rinse, sanitize
3. Verify no product residue
4. Reassemble and verify function

**Labeling Equipment:**

1. Wipe rollers and surfaces
2. Remove any adhesive buildup
3. Verify label path is clean

**Scales:**

1. Remove weighing platform
2. Clean platform with IPA
3. Wipe scale body
4. Replace platform and verify calibration

### 7.4 HVAC and Environmental Equipment

| Equipment | Cleaning Task | Frequency |
|-----------|--------------|-----------|
| **Air Handling Unit Filters** | Replace pre-filters | <span class="var">[Monthly]</span> |
| **HEPA Filters** | Integrity test, replace per schedule | <span class="var">[Per validation]</span> |
| **Dehumidifier Coils** | Clean/inspect | <span class="var">[Monthly]</span> |
| **Dehumidifier Filters** | Clean or replace | <span class="var">[Weekly to monthly]</span> |
| **Humidifier** | Descale, sanitize | <span class="var">[Weekly]</span> |
| **AC Drip Pans** | Clean, sanitize | <span class="var">[Monthly]</span> |

## 8. Cleaning Verification

### 8.1 Visual Inspection

- Performed after all cleaning activities
- Document observations on cleaning log
- If not visually clean, re-clean before proceeding

**Visual Inspection Criteria:**

| Criterion | Acceptable |
|-----------|------------|
| **Visible residue** | None |
| **Staining** | None (or documented permanent staining) |
| **Debris** | None |
| **Standing water** | None |
| **Odor** | Neutral (no product or chemical odor) |

### 8.2 Swab Testing for Residue

**When Required:**

- Changeover cleaning
- Deep cleaning
- Following contamination events
- As part of cleaning validation

**Procedure:**

1. Allow surface to dry completely after cleaning
2. Use pre-moistened swab (sterile water or sampling solvent)
3. Swab defined area (<span class="var">[25 cm²]</span> or <span class="var">[100 cm²]</span>)
4. Transfer swab to appropriate medium/container
5. Submit to QC for analysis

**Acceptance Criteria for Cannabinoid Residue:**

| Surface Type | Limit |
|--------------|-------|
| **Product contact** | <span class="var">[<10 µg/100 cm²]</span> or <span class="var">[<0.1 µg/swab]</span> |
| **Non-product contact** | <span class="var">[Visual clean]</span> |

### 8.3 Microbial Verification

**When Required:**

- Controlled environment areas (periodic)
- Following contamination events
- As part of environmental monitoring program

**Methods:**

| Method | Application |
|--------|-------------|
| **Contact plates** (RODAC) | Flat surfaces |
| **Swabs** | Irregular surfaces |
| **Air sampling** | Environmental |

**Acceptance Criteria:**

| Area | TAMC (CFU/plate) | TYMC (CFU/plate) |
|------|------------------|------------------|
| **Cleanroom (ISO 7)** | <span class="var">[<50]</span> | <span class="var">[<25]</span> |
| **Controlled area (ISO 8)** | <span class="var">[<100]</span> | <span class="var">[<50]</span> |
| **General production** | <span class="var">[<200]</span> | <span class="var">[<100]</span> |

## 9. Cleaning Solution Preparation

### 9.1 General Guidelines

1. **Prepare** fresh solutions daily or per manufacturer instructions
2. **Use** purified water for preparation
3. **Measure** concentrations accurately
4. **Label** all containers with:
   - Solution name
   - Concentration
   - Preparation date
   - Expiration
   - Prepared by
5. **Store** solutions in designated area
6. **Discard** expired solutions

### 9.2 Specific Preparations

**Neutral Detergent (<span class="var">[1:100]</span>):**

1. Fill graduated container with <span class="var">[9.9 L]</span> water
2. Add <span class="var">[100 mL]</span> concentrated detergent
3. Mix gently
4. Label container

**Sodium Hypochlorite (<span class="var">[200 ppm]</span>):**

1. Determine available chlorine in stock solution
2. Calculate dilution: Volume = (200 × Final Volume) / (Stock ppm × 10)
3. Add calculated volume to water
4. Mix well
5. Prepare fresh daily

**70% Isopropanol (from 99%):**

1. Measure <span class="var">[700 mL]</span> of 99% IPA
2. Add to container
3. Add purified water to <span class="var">[1000 mL]</span>
4. Mix well

## 10. Personal Protective Equipment

### 10.1 PPE Requirements by Task

| Task | Gloves | Eye Protection | Gown/Apron | Footwear |
|------|--------|----------------|------------|----------|
| **Routine cleaning** | Chemical-resistant | If splash risk | Water-resistant apron | Safety shoes |
| **Using concentrated chemicals** | Heavy-duty chemical | Face shield | Chemical apron | Chemical boots |
| **Working with sodium hypochlorite** | Nitrile | Safety goggles | Apron | Safety shoes |
| **Working with sporicide** | Heavy-duty | Face shield | Chemical gown | Boots |
| **High-pressure washing** | Water-resistant | Face shield | Waterproof suit | Rubber boots |

### 10.2 PPE Donning/Doffing

1. Don PPE before preparing chemicals
2. Change gloves if torn or contaminated
3. Remove PPE before eating, drinking, or leaving area
4. Dispose of single-use PPE appropriately
5. Clean reusable PPE after use

## 11. Waste Management

### 11.1 Cleaning Waste Streams

| Waste Type | Disposal Method |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Used mop water (detergent)** | Drain to sanitary sewer |
| **IPA/ethanol waste** | Hazardous waste collection |
| **Sodium hypochlorite waste** | Neutralize, then drain |
| **Empty chemical containers** | Triple rinse, recycle or dispose |
| **Contaminated wipes** | Solid waste or hazardous per content |
| **Plant debris** | Organic waste per facility procedure |

### 11.2 Spill Response

**For Chemical Spills:**

1. Alert personnel in area
2. Don appropriate PPE
3. Contain spill with absorbent material
4. Clean from outer edge inward
5. Dispose of absorbent as hazardous waste
6. Clean area with water
7. Document incident

## 13. Cleaning Validation

### 13.1 Validation Requirements

Cleaning validation is required for:
- Product contact equipment
- Changeover between significantly different products
- Introduction of new cleaning procedures

### 13.2 Validation Protocol Elements

1. **Worst-case determination** (hardest to clean, lowest limit)
2. **Sampling plan** (locations, methods)
3. **Acceptance criteria** (residue limits)
4. **Analytical methods** (validated)
5. **Number of consecutive successful cleanings** (typically <span class="var">[3]</span>)

### 13.3 Revalidation Triggers

- Change in cleaning procedure
- Change in cleaning agents
- Equipment modification
- OOS results in verification testing

---

## 14. Deviation Management

### 14.1 Cleaning Failures

If cleaning verification fails:

1. **Do not use** equipment/area for production
2. **Repeat** cleaning procedure
3. **Repeat** verification testing
4. **If second failure**: Escalate to QA
5. **Investigate** root cause
6. **Document** deviation

### 14.2 Contamination Events

If contamination is discovered:

1. **Stop** production in affected area
2. **Quarantine** any potentially affected product
3. **Notify** supervisor and QA
4. **Perform** decontamination cleaning
5. **Verify** effectiveness with testing
6. **Obtain** QA approval before resuming operations
7. **Document** per deviation procedure

## 16. Safety Considerations

### 16.1 Chemical Safety

- Review SDS before using any chemical
- Never mix different cleaning chemicals
- Ensure adequate ventilation
- Use appropriate PPE
- Know location of emergency equipment (eyewash, shower)

### 16.2 Electrical Safety

- Do not wet electrical components
- Lock out/tag out before cleaning electrical equipment
- Dry hands before touching electrical switches

### 16.3 Slip/Fall Prevention

- Use wet floor signs
- Dry floors promptly
- Wear non-slip footwear
