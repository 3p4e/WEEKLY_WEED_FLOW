Conventions. A nominal N with tolerance t covers N − t to N + t − 0.01, matching the specification pages (20.00 ± 2.00 → 18.00 – 21.99). Two neighbours are joined when the lower one's top is exactly 0.01 below the upper one's bottom. Tolerance is capped at 10 % of the nominal and never below 0.01.

What is saved, and where. Every change is kept in this browser, on this device — nothing leaves it, because this file talks to nothing. To move your work, or to put it somewhere a cleared browser cannot reach, use Save a copy with my work in it: that writes a fresh copy of this page with the ladders, tolerances and any results you typed already inside it, and it opens on its own on any machine. Source results are read-only; the nominals, the tolerances and your own entered results are yours to change. “Reset” returns a strain to its specification nominals at ±10 %.
